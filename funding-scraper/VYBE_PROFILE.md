What the founders have told us, and what it means for which money fits. Update this
file when they share something new; every funding search should start here.

Sources: founder uploads on 1 Oct 2026 (the Vybe 1-page, the "Competitive

## What Vybe is

**The developer platform for wearable health.** "We build the wearable. They build
what's possible with it." The stack:

| Layer | What it is | Ownership |
|---|---|---|
| Hardware | Vybe Band, Ring, future sensors. ECG and HRV | Vybe IP |
| Proprietary engine | Firmware, signal processing, sensor fusion | Vybe IP |
| Data platform | Secure infrastructure, standardized data | Vybe IP |
| SDK + API | Licensed developer access to defined raw and processed data | Licensed, not open source |
| Applications | Performance, aging, tactical, research, health — built by others | Developers |

- **The first-party app** (Lifestyle Architecture, organized around Restore, Move,
  Nourish, Connect and Vitals) is the flagship demonstration, not the limit of the
  platform.
- **First milestone:** Vybe DevKit v0.1 (device, SDK, docs, API) placed with 5–10
  external design partners: universities, startups, researchers, tactical and
  performance organizations, and health companies.
- **The test:** 10 organizations saying "we couldn't build our product without Vybe".
- **Revenue:**
  - hardware sales
  - SDK/API licensing
  - enterprise and research deployments
  - platform fees
  - marketplace share
  - data and analytics services
  - reference-design licensing
- **No mandatory consumer subscription.**
- **Consumer launch:** May 2027, then major retail including Best Buy.
- **Vybe Health Intelligence (roadmap):** a normalized personal health timeline. It
  joins band data with:
  - Apple HealthKit and Clinical Records
  - Epic on FHIR, Health Gorilla, Particle and 1up
  - Junction (labs only)
  - CMS Blue Button and the VA Patient Health API

  An AI reasoning layer calls small audited tools and never queries the record
  directly. The system is BAA-scoped with zero data retention. It deliberately takes
  no data from competitor wearables.
- **Positioning:** a screenless, privacy-first health band; a WHOOP or Oura
  alternative with no subscription; "Your health. Finally in context."
- **Not a medical device.** It makes no diagnostic or causal claims. Connect is
  framed as pattern detection and personal reflection.

## What this opens up for funding

Search all of these lanes, not just "consumer wearable":

1. **Research infrastructure.** The DevKit is a research tool. This lane covers
   university pilot and seed funds that take industry partners, CTSA pilot programs,
   NSF I-Corps and Partnerships for Innovation, and STTR with a university partner
   (SAM required at the proposal stage).
2. **Hardware, IoT and developer platform.** Chip and sensor-vendor startup programs,
   hardware contests, manufacturing and prototyping sponsorships, and cloud and AI
   credits.
3. **Tactical and human performance.** Army H2F, xTech, AFWERX, SOCOM human
   performance, AFRL 711th HPW (Ohio), MTEC, NavalX. The founders' veteran status
   matters here.
4. **Veteran and aging health data.** VA innovation programs, AgeTech, and NIA
   healthy-aging prizes.
5. **Health AI, interoperability and privacy.** CMS and ASTP/ONC programs, AI-for-health
   accelerators, and digital-biomarker prizes.
6. **Founder-identity programs.** Women, minority and veteran founders.
7. **Consumer health-tech pitch prizes.** Sleep, recovery, women's health, longevity.
