# Investor and equity-raising platforms for Vybe Health

Researched 11 Oct 2026 for Vybe: pre-revenue (<$75K), under one year old, incorporated, Akron OH. The company is 51%+ veteran-, woman- and minority-owned. It makes a screenless ECG/HRV band and a DevKit/SDK platform, with a consumer launch in May 2027.

**Method.** Every fee, cap and cheque size below comes from the platform's or fund's own page, or from the SEC/eCFR. Where a platform does not publish its founder fees, I used the "compensation" field of a recent **SEC Form C filed on EDGAR** for that portal, which is a primary source. "Not stated" means I could not find the item on an official page. Web search was used only to find pages.

**Rules that apply to every Reg CF portal** (all from SEC/eCFR):
- **Cap:** $5M across all Reg CF offerings in any 12 months ([SEC](https://www.sec.gov/resources-small-businesses/exempt-offerings/regulation-crowdfunding)).
- **Financial statements**, set by the target plus everything sold under Reg CF in the last 12 months ([17 CFR 227.201(t)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-227.201)):
  - Up to $124,000: tax-return line items certified by the CEO.
  - $124,000–$618,000: statements **reviewed** by an independent CPA.
  - $618,000–$1,235,000: **reviewed** if this is the first Reg CF raise, otherwise audited.
  - Over $1,235,000: **audited**.
  - All statements must be US GAAP and cover the period since inception if that is shorter than two years.
- **Timeline floor:** the Form C must be public on the portal for at least **21 days** before any sale closes ([227.303](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/chapter-II/part-227/subpart-C/section-227.303)).
- **Testing the waters:** before filing, you may gauge interest orally or in writing, but you cannot take money or commitments ([227.206](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/chapter-II/part-227/subpart-B/section-227.206)).
- **Reg A+:** Republic and StartEngine both advertise "raise up to $75M". It is not realistic at Vybe's stage.

## Platform notes

### 1. JumpStart Ventures (Cleveland; Akron is in its region)
- **What it is:** JumpStart Ventures is the investing arm of JumpStart Inc. It has $140M under management and invests "pre-seed through series A" in Ohio and the Midwest.
- **Funds:**
  - **Evergreen** ($50M under management): Ohio tech companies.
  - **Focus** ($10M): companies led by Black, Latino and female entrepreneurs, B2B software and healthcare.
  - **Healthcare Collaboration Fund:** run with University Hospitals Ventures.
  - **NEXT:** Ohio and neighbouring states.
- **Stage:** Evergreen and Focus invest in "pre-seed and seed-stage companies with some demonstration of product-market fit", typically raising $500K–$2M rounds.
- **What they want:** "prelim market validation, beyond MVP, paying and/or beta customers", an 18+ month runway plan, and a $500M–$1B+ TAM. "Biomedical Tech & Devices" is a target sector.
- **How to apply:** online form at jumpstart.vc/funds/#apply; no fee.
- **Trailblazer HealthTech Accelerator:** for Ohio startups. Gives $50K of service-provider work paid by JumpStart, "no fees or ownership stakes", and ends with a pitch to investors.
- **Fit:** Vybe's DevKit design-partner pilots (5–10 organisations) are exactly the "beta customers" evidence they ask for.
- **Sources:**
  - https://jumpstart.vc/funds/
  - https://jumpstart.vc/funds/focus-fund/
  - https://jumpstart.vc/funds/evergreen-fund/
  - https://jumpstart.vc/entrepreneurs/
  - https://jumpstartinc.org/startups/healthtech

### 2. Wefunder
- **Community Round (Reg CF):** **7.9% + $1K/yr** of funds raised. Open to all investors from $100, SPV included. LLCs taxed as LLCs pay $5,000+/yr extra.
- **Private Round (Reg D 506(b)):** "$0 for founders". SPV setup up to $10K or 10%, paid by SPV investors.
- **Disbursement:** first disbursement can be requested once $50K is in escrow.
- **Building an investor list is free.**
- **Recent filed fees are lower than the pricing page:** Simplex Chat (Aug 2026) shows 6%; Rejuvenate Bio (Jun 2026) shows 6.9%.
- **Comparable campaign:** Levels (metabolic wearable) raised **$7,907,927 from 3,237 investors**. It already had $21M in 2023 revenue and $46M of VC behind it, so it is not a pre-revenue comparable.
- **Sources:**
  - https://wefunder.com/pricing
  - https://wefunder.com/levels
  - Form Cs on EDGAR (CIK 2140143, 1859067)

### 3. Hustle Fund
- **Stage:** "We invest in pre-seed rounds. Not seed."
- **Cheque:** first cheque $150K. They will lead or follow.
- **Excluded:** pharmaceutical and medical device companies, and physical retail. Vybe should keep its consumer-wellness, not-a-device positioning.
- **How to apply:** free Typeform ("Pitch us").
- **Source:** https://www.hustlefund.vc/faq

### 4. Republic
- **Founder fees are not on Republic's public help pages.** The help page only covers costs not paid to Republic: Form C $1,500; escrow about $1,500; financials $1,000–$4,000; attorney $0–$3,500.
- **Commission from 2026 Form Cs (portal OpenDeal Portal LLC):**
  - Cognivix (Jun 2026): $5K onboarding, plus the greater of $15K or 6% of amounts raised between $100K and $5M, plus 2% of securities.
  - Mightylicious (Sep 2026): the greater of $12K or 6% above $100K, plus a $5K onboarding fee, plus 1.5% of securities.
- **Investors** pay an admin fee of about 2.5% (capped at $250).
- **Live wearable comparable:** Yakuru Inc. (smart ring + supplements, $1,873 revenue), Form C filed Aug 2026, target $75K, max $1.235M. It had $106,980 on 11 Oct 2026, 8% of max, with the deadline on 30 Oct 2026.
- **Sources:**
  - https://republic.com/help/how-much-does-it-cost-to-raise-on-republic
  - https://republic.com/help/what-fees-does-republic-charge
  - https://republic.com/yakuru
  - EDGAR CIK 2133498, 2147043, 2096055

### 5. Ohio TechAngel Funds (OTAF)
- **Who:** managed by Rev1 Ventures. It has invested $37M in 60+ Ohio companies.
- **Status:** "currently accepting funding submissions for its OTAF V funds".
- **Cheques:** initial $150K–$280K in participating preferred or convertible notes. It syndicates rounds of $500K–$2MM+.
- **Sectors:** IT, software, bioscience, medical device and advanced materials.
- **Outside the Columbus region:** OTAF "participates in but typically does not lead". It requires companies to first engage their local ESP provider or ACA-member angel group. For Akron, the route is JumpStart.
- **Application fee:** none stated.
- **Related:** Rev1's partner **R1 Capital** writes $250K–$1M+ but concentrates on Columbus Region startups.
- **Sources:**
  - https://www.rev1ventures.com/techangel-fund/
  - https://www.rev1ventures.com/capital/

### 6. Rock Health Capital

- **Not stated:** cheque size and how to apply, on the pages read.
- **Fit:** Vybe Health Intelligence (FHIR, Blue Button, VA API, BAA-scoped) and the SDK are the digital-health angle.

### 7. AngelList RUV / Rollups
- **What it does:** "Raise capital from many investors through a single entity". AngelList handles wires, compliance and taxes. Testimonials mention raises of $1M+ and about $10M.
- **Price:** not shown on the official page I read; an RUV calculator is linked. The ~$8K figure is from third-party reviews only and is unverified.
- **Use it** when a group of individual veteran, Akron or health angels commit small cheques.
- **Sources:**
  - https://angellist.com/ruv
  - https://www.angellist.com/pricing

### 8. Golden Seeds
- **Who:** national angel network, 9 chapters, more than $200M invested in women-led companies.
- **Terms:** US women-led company, with a woman in an operating C-suite role. First rounds $250K–$2M; valuation usually under $10M.
- **What they expect:** a beta product and proof-of-concept revenue or significant pilots. The revenue requirement is waived only for bio/pharma, diagnostics and device companies.
- **Consumer-product criteria:** trailing-12-month net revenue over $1M and gross margin over 40%.
- **How to apply:** free Information Sessions and Office Hours first, then an application. No fee stated.
- **Fit:** strong on founder identity, weak on stage until after the May 2027 launch.
- **Sources:**
  - https://goldenseeds.com/entrepreneurs-seeking-funding
  - https://goldenseeds.com/funding-criteria-eligibility

### 9. StartEngine
- **Raise limits:** up to $5M/yr under Reg CF; up to $75M under Reg A+.
- **Fees:** StartEngine's /fees page returned not-found. RoboBurger's Form C (Apr 2026, StartEngine) shows **"7 – 13 percent"** cash plus **1% of securities**.
- **Wearable examples:** none found on StartEngine.
- **Sources:**
  - https://www.startengine.com/raise-capital
  - EDGAR CIK 1951099

- **Still active:** Netcapital is still filing Form Cs in 2026. The third-party report of a pivot away from crowdfunding was **not confirmed** on its own site.
