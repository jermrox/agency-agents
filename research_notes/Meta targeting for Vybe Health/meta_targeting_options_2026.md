# Meta Ads (Facebook/Instagram) Targeting Toolbox in 2026 — Reference for a Pre-Launch US Screenless Health Band

Research date: 2026-09-30. Scope: US advertiser. Sources are tagged **[Meta official]** (Meta Business Help Center, Meta for Business, Meta engineering/developer docs, Meta Transparency Center) or **[Practitioner]** (agencies, consultants, trade press). Anything older than 2025 is flagged with its date.

Method note: Jon Loomer's pages and most facebook.com pages were blocked from direct fetch by the network proxy; their content was retrieved through Exa search highlights (verbatim excerpts) rather than full-page reads. Meta's Marketing API `adinterest` search (the only authoritative way to check whether a detailed-targeting interest exists today) requires an access token / connected ad account, which this session did not have, so interest *availability* claims below rely on third-party interest-explorer pages and practitioner articles, and are flagged accordingly.

## KQ1. Which interest/behavior segments exist today for fitness, running, CrossFit, HYROX, triathlon, cycling, biohacking, quantified self, sleep, heart-rate monitor, wearable technology, and competitor brands (WHOOP, Oura, Garmin, Fitbit, Apple Watch, Polar, Amazfit)? Which were removed as "sensitive"?

### Takeaway
Broad fitness/sport interests (Physical fitness, Running, CrossFit, Bodybuilding, Weight training, Yoga, Meditation) and at least some wearable-brand interests (e.g., "Garmin Fitness") demonstrably exist; "HYROX" and "CrossFit" are cited by 2026 practitioner guides as selectable interests. What Meta removed was *health-condition/health-cause* targeting (2022, 2024), not fitness interests — but Meta consolidated many granular sport/food/music interests into broader groupings from June 23, 2025 and removed detailed-targeting *exclusions* in 2025, and the whole detailed-targeting layer is now only a "suggestion" for most performance goals (see KQ3). I could not verify the current existence or audience size of "WHOOP", "Oura Ring", "Fitbit", "Apple Watch", "Polar", "Amazfit", "Biohacking", "Quantified self", "Sleep", or "Heart rate monitor" as interests from a primary source.

### Cited Findings

**Fitness & wellness category (what Meta shows by default)**
- Meta's browsable "Fitness and wellness" interest category contains seven top-level entries: Bodybuilding, Meditation, Physical exercise, Physical fitness, Running, Weight training, Yoga; everything else (e.g., CrossFit) must be found via keyword search ("hidden interests") — [Practitioner, 2020 but consistent with 2026 explorer listing] [Audience Eye](https://audienceeye.com/facebook-ads-interests-list/); the 2026 Eachspy category listing still shows "Fitness and wellness: 7" top-level interests — [Practitioner, 2026] [Eachspy interests list](https://www.eachspy.com/facebook-ads-interests/)
- Ads Manager returns a maximum of 25 suggested interests per keyword; the Marketing API `adinterest` search (`https://graph.facebook.com/search?type=adinterest&q=[keyword]&limit=10000`) returns the full list with audience-size bounds — [Practitioner] [KlientBoost](https://www.klientboost.com/facebook/facebook-interest-targeting/); [InterestExplorer](https://www.interestexplorer.site/)
- Example interest sizes pulled from Meta data by AdTargeting (undated page, treat sizes as indicative only): "Physical fitness" 749M; "Weight Loss (Fitness & wellness)" 33.8M; "Men's Fitness" 26.1M; "Bodybuilding & Fitness" 16.3M; "Health club" 166.9M; "Treadmill" 18.75M; "Dumbbell" 18.56M; "Exercise equipment" 52.7M; "Garmin Fitness" 5,318,140; "Fitness app" 234,130 — [Practitioner, undated] [AdTargeting](https://adtargeting.io/blog/target-fitness-audience-on-facebook-ad)

**CrossFit / HYROX / running / triathlon / cycling**
- A 2026 interest-stacking guide uses "CrossFit", "Nike Training Club", "HYROX", "Powerlifting", "Yoga" as Include-tier interests (illustrative combined audience ~8M), and "Fitness & Exercise" as the "broad, post-consolidation" interest, and states that Meta's January 2024 removals and June 2025 consolidation "reduced the number of available granular interests" — [Practitioner, 2026] [Adamigo](https://www.adamigo.ai/blog/how-to-build-interest-based-audiences-for-meta-ads)
- A 2026 gym-marketing guide states two people "interested in HYROX training" may respond to different messages under Andromeda, implying HYROX is a targetable/recognizable interest — [Practitioner, May 2026] [Two-Brain Business](https://twobrainbusiness.com/paid-ads-guide/)
- "Running" is a top-level Fitness & wellness interest — [Audience Eye](https://audienceeye.com/facebook-ads-interests-list/)
- No primary source confirming current "Triathlon", "Cycling", "Marathon" interest names/sizes was found (see Gaps), though sports interests were among those Meta consolidated in June 2025 — [Practitioner, July 2025] [FanIQ](https://www.faniq.live/blog/meta-targeting-update)

**Competitor brands**
- "Garmin Fitness" appears as an interest (5.3M) — [Practitioner, undated] [AdTargeting](https://adtargeting.io/blog/target-fitness-audience-on-facebook-ad)
- No source found listing "WHOOP", "Oura", "Fitbit", "Apple Watch", "Polar" or "Amazfit" as Meta interests with sizes (searches returned only brand-marketing analyses). A competitor-ad analysis notes WHOOP "competes with Oura Ring, Garmin, Fitbit, Apple, and Polar for paid media attention across Meta platforms" but says nothing about targetability — [Practitioner, Mar 2026] [GoMarble](https://www.gomarble.ai/library/brands/whoop/)

**What was removed as "sensitive"**
- Jan 19, 2022 (enforced Mar 17, 2022): Meta removed detailed-targeting options "referencing causes, organizations, or public figures that relate to health, race or ethnicity, political affiliation, religion, or sexual orientation." Examples: health causes "Lung cancer awareness", "World Diabetes Day", "Chemotherapy" — [Meta official, Nov 2021/Dec 2021] [Meta for Business](https://www.facebook.com/business/news/removing-certain-ad-targeting-options-and-expanding-our-ad-controls); [Meta developers blog](https://developers.facebook.com/blog/post/2021/12/08/updating-metas-detailed-targeting-options/)
- The Markup's dataset of interests removed in the 2022 wave includes health items such as "Alzheimer's disease research" (1.4–1.7M), "American Heart Association" (6.1–7.2M), "American Diabetes Association", "Cancer awareness" (36–42M), "Chronic obstructive pulmonary disease awareness" (9.2–10.8M), "Continuous positive airway pressure" (2.7–3.2M), "Compression stockings", "Breast pump" — [Practitioner/journalism, 2022] [The Markup CSV](https://github.com/the-markup/facebook-removed-interests/blob/main/data/fb_removed_interests.csv)
- Jan 15, 2024 (ad sets had until Mar 18, 2024): Meta announced it was "removing or consolidating some detailed targeting options that relate to topics people may perceive as sensitive," which Meta described as relating to health, race and/or ethnicity, plus options that were "not widely used, redundant with others, too granular… or because of legal or regulatory requirements"; Meta did not publish the list — [Trade press quoting Meta, Jan 2024] [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/metas-removing-detailed-targeting-options-ad-campaigns/704375/); [Silly Goose Digital quoting Meta](https://sillygoosedigital.co.uk/blog/what-meta-removing-detailed-targeting-options-means-for-businesses/)
- Mar 31, 2025: Ads Manager no longer allows detailed-targeting *exclusions* in new ad sets; Jun 10, 2025: exclusions removed from boosted posts; Jun 23, 2025: Meta began consolidating many detailed interest categories (sports, food, music genres, car models) into broader groupings; active campaigns created before Jun 23 could run until Jan 15, 2026. Meta cited internal testing showing a 22.6% lower median cost per conversion when advertisers did not use exclusions — [Practitioner summarizing Meta notices, Jul 2025] [FanIQ](https://www.faniq.live/blog/meta-targeting-update); exclusion removal on Mar 31, 2025 also reported by [AdsUploader, May 2026](https://adsuploader.com/blog/meta-audience-targeting)
- Meta's own statement of the 2022 rationale: "we heard concerns from experts that targeting options like these could be used in ways that lead to negative experiences for people in underrepresented groups" — [Meta official] [Meta for Business](https://www.facebook.com/business/news/removing-certain-ad-targeting-options-and-expanding-our-ad-controls)
- 2022 test (dated): Publicis Health Media/Meta found lookalike audiences beat the deprecated health interests in 86% of 14 healthcare studies — [Trade press, Aug 2022] [Adweek](https://www.adweek.com/programmatic/publicis-health-media-and-meta-find-legacy-interest-based-targeting-less-effective-in-healthcare/)

### Inferences
- Fitness *activity* interests (CrossFit, running, weight training, HYROX) were not the target of the sensitive-topic purges; those hit health *conditions/causes*. A wearable brand's natural interest stack (fitness, running, CrossFit, HYROX, competitor brands) is therefore likely still selectable, but should be verified live in Ads Manager or via the `adinterest` API because the June 2025 consolidation may have merged some sport interests.
- "Sleep" and "heart rate" interests are more likely to have been affected: sleep apnea/CPAP-type health interests were removed in 2022, and anything framed as a health status is out of scope. Generic "Sleep" as a lifestyle interest may or may not exist — unverified.
- Because detailed targeting is a suggestion for nearly all relevant performance goals (KQ3), the practical value of confirming each brand interest is low; the exercise mostly informs creative personas rather than delivery.

### Gaps
- Could not confirm from a primary source whether "WHOOP", "Oura Ring", "Fitbit", "Apple Watch", "Polar", "Amazfit", "Biohacking", "Quantified self", "Sleep", "Heart rate monitor", "Wearable technology", "Triathlon", "Cycling", or "Marathon" exist as interests today, nor their US audience sizes. Recommended action: run `GET /search?type=adinterest&q=<term>` on the Marketing API, or use a free explorer (Eachspy, InterestExplorer) that queries the API live.
- Meta has never published the list of interests removed in Jan 2024 or consolidated in Jun 2025.

## KQ2. What exactly did Meta restrict in January 2025 for health & wellness advertisers, does a consumer fitness wearable get flagged, and what is the workaround?

### Takeaway
From January 2025 Meta assigns "data source categories" to pixels/datasets; a "Health & Wellness" flag triggers one of three tiers — Core setup (URL/parameter stripping), restriction of mid/lower-funnel standard events (Purchase, AddToCart, Lead, etc.), or full restriction of all events. Meta's own definition explicitly names "a patient portal or wellness tracker for depression" as an example, so wearables are inside the risk zone, but the category is driven by whether the site/product is "associated with medical conditions, specific health statuses, or provider/patient relationships"; a general-wellness fitness band is less likely to be flagged than a condition-specific one. Workarounds: optimize to upper-funnel standard events (PageView/ViewContent/Landing Page View), use registered, neutrally-named custom events (not available under full restriction), keep health terms out of URLs/event names, and lean on engagement/traffic/lead objectives.

### Cited Findings

**Meta's definition and mechanics [Meta official, as quoted by third parties; direct help-center pages were not fetchable]**
- Health & Wellness category is "associated with medical conditions, specific health statuses, or provider/patient relationships (for example, a patient portal or wellness tracker for depression)" — Meta help wording quoted by [Foxwell Digital, Feb 2025](https://www.foxwelldigital.com/blog/meta-special-ad-category-vs-sensitive-ad-category-whats-the-difference) and [Triple Whale](https://www.triplewhale.com/blog/meta-health-and-wellness-brands)
- Meta's "How to self-categorise your datasets" help page: prohibited information "includes health information, financial information or other categories… considered as sensitive"; advertisers can self-assign a data-source category; "Meta may also categorise your data sources… you may have the ability to request a review"; path: Events Manager > Dataset > Settings > Dataset categories — [Meta official] [Meta Business Help Centre](https://en-gb.facebook.com/business/help/1666235380728268)
- Three restriction tiers (Meta's in-product wording, as transcribed by Bram Van der Hallen, Jan 2025): "Core setup: Meta may restrict sharing custom parameters and parts of the URLs following the domain. Restriction on certain standard events: Meta may restrict the sharing of specific mid and lower funnel events. Meta then recommends shifting your focus to upper-funnel events… Full restrictions: Meta may fully restrict ALL events in specific regions or all regions… Meta then suggests adjusting your campaigns to focus on awareness, engagement and traffic-based objectives." In his EU example even PageView was blocked under full restriction — [Practitioner quoting Meta UI, Jan 2025] [LinkedIn – Van der Hallen](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/bramvanderhallen_metaads-activity-7287380037358501889-Jgkc)
- Meta help page "Understand data sharing restrictions based on data source categories": "We may fully restrict all events in specific regions or all regions. In these circumstances, Meta Business Tools cannot be used for campaign optimization where restrictions are in place." — quoted in [Search Engine Land, Mar 2025](https://searchengineland.com/meta-ads-restrictions-health-wellness-campaigns-453094)
- Meta help page "About prohibited information": "You must not share… data that is based on or includes, directly or otherwise, health… information about people… Diseases, medical conditions and injuries, sexual and reproductive health, mental health and psychological states"; and "Meta's systems are not a substitute for your own compliance mechanisms." — quoted in [Search Engine Land, Mar 2025](https://searchengineland.com/meta-ads-restrictions-health-wellness-campaigns-453094)
- Core setup definition (Meta wording): "Core setup is a set of data restrictions that can be applied to a Meta Business Tool by you or by Meta. There is an option in Meta Events Manager that you can use to turn on data restrictions…"; it restricts custom parameters and "anything in a URL after the domain" — [Practitioner quoting Meta, Jan 2025] [CustomerLabs](https://www.customerlabs.com/blog/meta-ads-restriction-health-wellness-killing-the-ad-how-to-fix-it/)
- Core setup impact list (from Meta's help text as relayed): cannot share custom parameters/URL parts; custom audiences relying on them may shrink; catalog additions via pixel may fail; some Events Manager reporting (sampled activities, test events) unavailable; automatic advanced matching may be unavailable. Tier 2 blocks optimization toward "add to cart, purchase, etc." while "upper-funnel events (landing page view, view content, etc.) and custom events are still available" — [Search Engine Land, Mar 2025](https://searchengineland.com/meta-ads-restrictions-health-wellness-campaigns-453094); same list in [PixelFlow docs](https://docs.pixelflow.so/troubleshooting/what-are-metas-health-and-wellness-restrictions-and-how-do-they-affect-tracking-ce3t3.md)
- Custom-event rules under restriction: custom events must be registered in Events Manager or they are dropped; must not mirror a standard event; must not directly or indirectly convey prohibited information; not available under full restriction. Meta: "Meta has, and will continue to, detect and filter out custom events that appear potentially sensitive, regardless of what data restrictions are applied" — [Practitioner relaying Meta guidance] [Triple Whale](https://www.triplewhale.com/blog/meta-health-and-wellness-brands); [Freshpaint, Feb 2025](https://www.freshpaint.io/blog/meta-data-restrictions); [Matchnode, reviewed Jul 2026](https://matchnode.com/metas-new-data-restrictions/)
- Standard events reported as *not* restricted: Donate, Search, ViewContent, PageView, App Install — [Practitioner, Feb 2025] [Freshpaint](https://www.freshpaint.io/blog/meta-data-restrictions)
- Where to check: Events Manager > Data Sources > (pixel) > Settings > "Manage data source categories" and "Data restrictions"/"Manage event blocking" — [Practitioner] [Herd](https://herd.io/blogs/performance-marketing/metas-restricted-categories-update); [Penrod, Jan 2025](https://penrod.co/metas-data-restrictions-for-healthcare-and-wellness-marketers/)
- Timeline: enforcement began ~Jan 6–13, 2025 — [Practitioner, Jan 2025] [Aimerce](https://www.aimerce.ai/blogs/what-to-know-2025-meta-health-and-wellness-data-sharing-restrictions); Meta's own confirmation in Dec 2024 was thin; Jon Loomer: "There's a ton of speculation and not a lot of confirmed facts from official Meta sources" — [Practitioner, Dec 2024] [Jon Loomer](https://www.jonloomer.com/qvt/health-and-wellness-restrictions/)
- Sub-categories seen in accounts: "Health and Wellness portals" (fully restricted), "Health and Wellness providers" and "Health and Wellness conditions" (partially restricted) — [Practitioner] [Matchnode](https://matchnode.com/metas-new-data-restrictions/)
- Appeals: automated, no supporting documents, "the domain will be the source of truth"; can re-request every 30 days; Freshpaint (Feb 2025) and Matchnode (Jul 2026) report no known successful overturn of a full restriction — [Freshpaint](https://www.freshpaint.io/blog/meta-data-restrictions); [Aimerce newsletter](https://newsletter.aimerce.ai/p/meta-pixel-changes-for-health-wellness-brands-a-quick-update); [Matchnode](https://matchnode.com/metas-new-data-restrictions/)

**Related 2025 enforcement: custom audiences and custom conversions [Meta official]**
- "Beginning September 2, 2025, we will start to roll out more proactive restrictions on custom audiences that may suggest information not permitted under our terms. For example, any custom audience or lookalike audience suggesting specific health conditions (e.g., 'arthritis', 'diabetes') or financial status… will be flagged and prevented from being used to run ad campaigns." Flagged audiences return `operation_status` 471; ad sets go `WITH_ISSUES`; review can be requested in Ads Manager/Audience Manager — [Meta official] [Marketing API – Custom Audience](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/marketing-api/reference/custom-audience/); [Lookalike Audiences guide](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/marketing-api/audiences/guides/lookalike-audiences/)
- The same notice covers custom conversions; notifications began Jul 15, 2025; flagged custom conversions "will no longer be matched" — [Meta partner notice reproduced by LiveRamp](https://docs.liveramp.com/connect/en/announcement--upcoming-meta-restrictions-on-certain-custom-audiences-and-custom-conversions--7-17-25-.html)
- ADM (Aug 2026) reports Meta "flagging and disabling custom or lookalike audiences and custom conversions whose names, rules, or metadata included or implied sensitive traits (e.g., 'arthritis_interest_list' or 'appointment booked' conversions)" — [Practitioner, Aug 2026] [Accelerated Digital Media](https://www.accelerateddigitalmedia.com/insights/guide-to-social-media-health-ad-restrictions-2026/)

**Does a consumer fitness wearable get flagged?**
- Foley Hoag (law firm) reading of Meta's definition: the category "potentially includes businesses that sell supplements or devices as a specific treatment, telehealth practices, pharmacies, fitness or dieting programs, or mental health services. By contrast, products intended to support general wellness (e.g., certain vitamins) or utilized for cosmetic purposes may be less likely to fall within the health and wellness category." — [Law-firm analysis, Jan 2025] [Foley Hoag](https://foleyhoag.com/news-and-insights/blogs/security-privacy-and-the-law/2025/january/meta-s-new-advertising-rules-key-considerations-for-health-and-wellness-businesses/)
- Herd: "not all 'health and wellness' businesses will be flagged… The distinction mainly boils down to if the product or service could reasonably disclose information about the condition or state of an individual" (general supplements vs. pregnancy tests) — [Practitioner] [Herd](https://herd.io/blogs/performance-marketing/metas-restricted-categories-update)
- Triple Whale: "Meta considers customer purchases of health products and/or remedies as 'health data'" — [Practitioner] [Triple Whale](https://www.triplewhale.com/blog/meta-health-and-wellness-brands)
- No source documents WHOOP, Oura, Garmin or a comparable consumer wearable being placed in the restricted category; WHOOP was still running 282 active Meta ads with SHOP_NOW CTAs to purchase/trial pages as of Mar 2026 — [Practitioner] [GoMarble](https://www.gomarble.ai/library/brands/whoop/)

**Workarounds [Practitioner consensus]**
- Foley Hoag: shift to "brand awareness or campaigns that utilize non-restricted data like consumer 'landing page views' or other forms of engagement"; consider "Branding Changes" to emphasize general wellness over medical conditions — [Foley Hoag](https://foleyhoag.com/news-and-insights/blogs/security-privacy-and-the-law/2025/january/meta-s-new-advertising-rules-key-considerations-for-health-and-wellness-businesses/)
- Freshpaint: plan "conversion optimization without patient portal data, such as using landing page views as a signal"; use neutral custom-event names (e.g., "event_T4B9"); reference only FBCLID — [Freshpaint](https://www.freshpaint.io/blog/meta-data-restrictions)
- Aimerce example mapping of standard → coded custom events (Purchase → "Aimerce_Target", AddToCart → "Aimerce_ATC", Lead → "Aimerce_SUB") — [Aimerce newsletter](https://newsletter.aimerce.ai/p/meta-pixel-changes-for-health-wellness-brands-a-quick-update)
- Practical hygiene: keep condition words out of URL paths, UTMs, event names and button labels (e.g., avoid "/sleep-apnea", "utm_source=hrv-quiz"); CAPI does not bypass detection because "CAPI sends cleaner, more structured data" — [CustomerLabs, Aug 2025](https://www.customerlabs.com/blog/meta-data-sharing-restrictions-what-it-means-for-health-brands/)
- Herd flags event-relabelling (firing PageView on purchase) as "murky waters" — [Herd](https://herd.io/blogs/performance-marketing/metas-restricted-categories-update)
- Note: XBorder/SEL: "In-app lead generation advertisers will not be impacted (beyond Core Setup)" and "ToFu/brand campaigns are not affected" — [Search Engine Land syndication](https://xborderinsights.com/how-to-prevent-meta-ads-restrictions-on-health-and-wellness-campaigns/)

### Inferences
- A screenless band marketed for sleep, recovery, HRV and "health" sits close to Meta's example ("wellness tracker for depression"). Risk drivers are on-site language: pages or URLs naming conditions (sleep apnea, anxiety, AFib), symptom quizzes, or "diagnose/treat" claims. Positioning around fitness/performance/general wellness (like protein and fitness services, which Meta's ad policy also exempts from the 18+ rule) is the lowest-risk framing.
- For a pre-launch brand with no purchases, the restriction is moot for now: the events you would optimize to (Lead via instant forms, Landing Page View, ViewContent, engagement, profile visits) are exactly the ones that survive Tier 2. Build the pixel/CAPI stack with neutral event naming from day one so a later flag does not force a rebuild.
- Classification is at the domain/dataset level, so a separate pre-launch landing domain with no health-condition copy is a defensible hedge.

### Gaps
- Could not fetch Meta's own "Understand data sharing restrictions based on data source categories" or "About core setup" help pages directly; wording is via reputable secondary quotes.
- No documented case of a consumer fitness wearable being flagged or not flagged; Meta publishes no list of flagged industries.
- Whether Tier 2 blocks the *Lead* standard event is inconsistently reported (PixelFlow says Lead still works; Matchnode/CustomerLabs say Lead can be blocked) — treat as account-specific.

## KQ3. How does Advantage+ audience work now (suggestions vs. constraints), when does Meta recommend interests vs. broad, and what do 2025–2026 practitioner tests (incl. WHOOP case study) show for small budgets and new accounts?

### Takeaway
Advantage+ audience is the default; only location, language, minimum age and custom-audience exclusions are hard "audience controls" — age max, gender, detailed targeting, custom and lookalike audiences are "suggestions" Meta can go beyond. Since late 2025/early 2026, detailed targeting cannot be made a hard constraint for 11 performance goals (conversions, value, landing page views, link clicks, leads, IG profile/FB page visits, etc.), so interest stacks are almost always suggestions. Meta's WHOOP case study (broad vs. segmented: −29% cost per purchase, +30% ROAS) and most 2025–26 practitioner tests favor broad + creative diversity — with the caveat from several practitioners that very low budgets (<~$3k/mo) or accounts with no conversion signal may still benefit from light interest guardrails, and that ASC/broad purchase optimization needs ~50 conversions/week to learn.

### Cited Findings

**Mechanics [Meta official]**
- Meta product page: "Advantage+ audience takes your usual audience selections, such as a customer list, a gender, or an interest and expands beyond them… When you add audience suggestions, the system prioritizes audiences matching this profile before searching more widely… You can set strict criteria where AI should not [deliver]" — [Meta official] [Advantage+ audience page](https://www.facebook.com/business/ads/meta-advantage-plus/audience)
- Meta claims (per the same product page, as cited Jun 2026): median CPA improvement of 14.8% top-of-funnel, 9.7% mid-funnel, 7.2% bottom-funnel at 99.9% confidence — [Practitioner citing Meta, Jun 2026] [Alex Neiman](https://alexneiman.com/meta-advantage-plus-audience-vs-detailed-targeting-2026/)
- Meta Help Center wording (as quoted): audience controls — "audience suggestions won't be shown outside your audience controls"; suggestions "share information with our AI about the audience you want to reach. Ads will also be shown to other audiences when it's likely to improve performance." Only minimum age remains a control; age max and gender are suggestions — [Practitioner quoting Meta, May 2026] [AdsUploader](https://adsuploader.com/blog/meta-audience-targeting)
- Ad-account-level controls (Marketing API): minimum age 18–25 (no maximum), geo exclusions, placement exclusions — [Meta official] [Advantage+ Shopping Campaigns docs](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/marketing-api/advantage-shopping-campaigns/)
- Feb 2025: Meta announced streamlined setup where Advantage+ (audience, placements, budget) is on by default for Sales, App and Leads campaigns; Advantage+ Shopping renamed Advantage+ Sales; Advantage+ Leads launched ("10% lower cost per qualified lead" vs Advantage+ off in early testing); Opportunity score expanded — [Meta official] [Meta for Business](https://www.facebook.com/business/news/supercharge-performance-advantage-opportunity-score); details via [Jon Loomer, Feb 2025](https://www.jonloomer.com/advantage-plus-sales-app-leads-campaigns/) and [The Keyword, Feb 2025](https://www.thekeyword.co/news/meta-tests-a-unified-ai-powered-advantage-campaign-setup)
- May 29, 2025: unified Marketing API — Advantage+ state requires all three levers: campaign-level budget, audience = geo-only or `advantage_audience:1`, no placement exclusions; ASC API removed in v25.0 (Q1 2026); `existing_customer_budget_percentage` deprecated (replicate with two ad sets) — [Meta official] [ASC docs](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/marketing-api/advantage-shopping-campaigns/); [ppc.land, Sep 2025](https://ppc.land/meta-launches-unified-api-structure-for-advantage-campaigns/)
- Meta: "advertisers who adopted opportunity score recommendations saw a 5% median decrease in their cost per result" — [Meta official] [Meta Advantage+ explained](https://www.facebook.com/business/news/meta-advantage-explained-in-two-minutes)

**Detailed targeting is now a suggestion for most goals [Practitioner, documenting Meta UI]**
- Jon Loomer (Feb 11, 2026): Advantage+ Detailed Targeting cannot be turned off for these performance goals — maximize conversions; value; landing page views; link clicks; app events; conversations; **Instagram profile and Facebook page visits**; calls; reminders set; leads; conversion leads. "It does not matter if you turn Advantage+ off, switch to original audiences… detailed targeting will only be used as suggestions… It puts the usefulness and impact of detailed targeting in significant doubt." Lookalikes became forced-suggestions for 9 goals in late 2025 — [Jon Loomer](https://www.jonloomer.com/detailed-targeting-suggestion/); [Jon Loomer, Feb 22 2026](https://www.jonloomer.com/qvt/nothing-about-targeting-is-the-same/)
- Implication for a follower/profile-visit campaign: goals *not* on that list (e.g., ThruPlay/video views, reach, post engagement) can still hard-constrain detailed targeting — inferred from the Loomer list above.
- Loomer's 2026 approach: does not use audience suggestions, detailed targeting or lookalikes; does not restrict age/gender (uses value rules instead); "the only inputs worth touching are locations and exclusions"; concedes "MAYBE they'd be useful for new businesses or new accounts where Meta doesn't have data, but that's purely a hypothetical" — [Jon Loomer Pubcast, 2026](https://pubcast.jonloomer.com/how-i-actually-approach-targeting-in-2026/)
- Loomer's audience-segment test (Jul 2024, dated): with or without suggestions, ~20–25% of budget went to remarketing segments automatically; suggestions were "completely unnecessary — or marginally impactful" for his mature account; he flags "New accounts, new pixels, or websites that get minimal activity may not have the same advantage" — [Jon Loomer, Jul 2024](https://www.jonloomer.com/how-metas-algorithmic-audience-targeting-impacts-ad-distribution/)
- Loomer split test: Advantage+ audience drove more registrations than detailed targeting (not statistically significant) and than lookalikes (significant, $0.68 lower CPM) — as summarized by [Alex Neiman, Jun 2026](https://alexneiman.com/meta-advantage-plus-audience-vs-detailed-targeting-2026/)
- Meta's 2026 "Describe your audience" free-text AI targeting input is rolling out alongside the profile-visits goal — [Practitioner, Mar 2026] [CoreBrief](https://corebrief.in/meta-ads-updates-2026/)

**Meta's WHOOP case study [Meta official]**
- WHOOP "tested the impact of removing targeting restrictions… and found… 29% lower cost per purchase using broad targeting, compared to business-as-usual segmented audience-targeting approach; 30% higher return on ad spend." WHOOP quote: "After relying on interest-based targeting for so long, we were surprised to find that expanding outside our core demographic yielded such positive results. Moving forward, we have implemented a broad audience strategy across all currently active regions and plan on launching new markets with this same strategy." — [Meta official, undated (pre-2025 era case study)] [Meta/Instagram success story](https://www.facebook.com/business/success/instagram/whoop)

**Practitioner tests and guidance, 2025–2026**
- Berkay Barboros (Feb 2025), 23 A/B tests across 20+ accounts, 30 days each: broad won 17/23 on CPA; CPMs were 15–30% *higher* for broad in 19/23 (wins came from CTR/CVR); "Broad failed in low-budget accounts. Below €3,000/month spend, broad targeting underperformed in 15 of 17 tests." Recommendation for €0–3k/mo: "3–5 interest segments in a single ad set, not separate ad sets per interest"; "Detailed targeting is still useful for niche products, small budgets, or seeding a new account before you have conversion data." — [Practitioner] [Berkay Barboros](https://berkaybarboros.com/blog/meta-ads-broad-targeting)
- Foxwell Digital (Nov 2025): consolidated to "fewer campaigns with fewer — oftentimes one — broad ad sets… one ad set with up to 20–30 creatives"; "Andromeda appears to group similar creatives together, treating minor variations as essentially the same ad"; wait "at least 5–7 days before making any decisions on ads" — [Foxwell Digital](https://www.foxwelldigital.com/blog/how-andromeda-has-changed-meta-advertising-a-practical-guide); audit guide (Sep 2025): "layering interests, using interests with lookalikes (or really, lookalikes at all nowadays)… tends to inhibit results" — [Foxwell Digital](https://www.foxwelldigital.com/blog/how-we-approach-meta-ads-audits-a-strategic-framework-for-better-performance)
- Foxwell on Perpetual Traffic (Tier 11's podcast, via summary): "Five structurally different ad concepts typically outperform thirty micro-variants"; "your creative decides your audience" — [Practitioner summary] [eChai](https://echai.ventures/d2c/d2c-meta-ads-fundamentals/targeting-vs-broad-advantage-plus)
- Common Thread Collective (Apr 2025): moving toward consolidation into fewer campaigns/ad sets (ABO), 7-day-click attribution to exit learning, cost controls for volume pushes — [Practitioner] [CTC](https://commonthreadco.com/blogs/ecommerce-playbook/2025-guide-to-meta-media-buying)
- New-account guidance (May 2026): "Start broad… Leave interest and behaviour targeting empty or use Advantage+ audience. Interest stacks narrow the pool before the algorithm has signal, which extends the learning phase and raises CPM." — [Practitioner] [AdLibrary](https://adlibrary.com/posts/how-to-launch-meta-ads-from-scratch); a cold-start composite (no pixel history, 8.4k IG followers): €50/day, Reels-only, broad 18–45, 3 UGC clips, then a 60-day IG engagement audience and a 1% lookalike from add-to-carts by week 3 — [AdLibrary, Apr 2026](https://adlibrary.com/posts/instagram-ad-campaign-setup-guide)
- ASC/Advantage+ Sales needs "roughly 50 purchase events per week at the account level to optimise well. Below that — or under £10K/month — ASC is guessing"; start with manual broad prospecting — [Practitioner, Jun 2026] [Grow with Sakib citing AdLibrary](https://growwithsakib.com/meta-ads-for-ecommerce/)
- Stackmatix (Sep 2026): "a small advertiser is usually better off with one or two broad ad sets"; over-segmentation prevents reaching "the 50 or so weekly conversions Meta needs" — [Stackmatix](https://www.stackmatix.com/blog/facebook-ads-targeting)
- Adamigo (2026) hybrid rule: Advantage+ audience ON when estimated audience >2M and cold; OFF for retargeting/small audiences; its illustrative test: "CrossFit + HYROX" narrowed by Engaged Shoppers vs "Fitness & Exercise" broad-ish: 2.8% CTR/$31 CPA vs 1.6% CTR/$26 CPA — [Adamigo](https://www.adamigo.ai/blog/how-to-build-interest-based-audiences-for-meta-ads)
- Neiman decision matrix (Jun 2026): "Newer brand, thin conversion data, deep first-party list → Advantage+ Audience + custom-audience suggestions"; "Awareness/reach goal where you need a precise audience → Original audiences (you can still hard-constrain here)" — [Alex Neiman](https://alexneiman.com/meta-advantage-plus-audience-vs-detailed-targeting-2026/)
- Tinuiti/Meta split test (Feb–Mar 2026, OLLY Body): holding targeting constant (US women 18–44, personal-care interests) and adding 10 diversified Reels-native assets to 11 BAU assets produced −12.4% CPM, +8.5% link clicks, −8.3% cost per link click, +14.1% CTR on top assets (95% confidence) — [Practitioner/Meta partner] [Tinuiti](https://tinuiti.com/work/meta-case-study-olly/)

### Inferences
- For a pre-launch account there is no purchase signal for Advantage+ to learn from, so the realistic choice is not "broad vs. interests" but which *proxy event* to feed (profile visits, leads, ThruPlay, landing page views) plus creative personas. Interests can still be used as suggestions to seed the first days; they cost nothing but also cannot be relied on to constrain delivery for LPV/leads/profile-visit goals.
- The one place a manual audience is still a hard fence is a video-views/reach/engagement goal — useful if the goal is to build a seed audience of *the right* video viewers for later retargeting/lookalikes rather than the cheapest viewers.
- WHOOP's own case study is the strongest category-specific evidence for broad, but it was run on a mature account with purchase optimization; it does not speak to pre-launch.

### Gaps
- No 2025–26 test found that isolates broad vs. interests specifically for pre-launch/lead-gen accounts with zero pixel history; evidence is extrapolated from small-budget e-commerce tests and practitioner opinion.
- The WHOOP case study page did not expose dates or the optimization event used; Tier 11's own written guidance (vs. their podcast with Foxwell) was not retrieved.

## KQ4. What is Andromeda and how does it change creative strategy?

### Takeaway
Andromeda (announced Dec 2, 2024) is Meta's GPU-based ads *retrieval* model — the first stage that narrows tens of millions of ads to a few thousand candidates per person — with a claimed 10,000× model-complexity increase, +6% recall and +8% ad quality. Because retrieval now reads creative content rather than advertiser-defined audiences, Meta and practitioners converge on "creative is the targeting": conceptually distinct creatives per persona, consolidated broad ad sets, and patience, with near-duplicate variants clustered together and adding little.

### Cited Findings
- [Meta official] Engineering blog (Dec 2, 2024): "Andromeda is Meta's proprietary machine learning (ML) system design for retrieval in ad recommendation… deployment across Instagram and Facebook applications has achieved +6% recall improvement to the retrieval system, delivering +8% ads quality improvement on selected segments"; runs on NVIDIA Grace Hopper and MTIA; "over 100x improvement in both feature extraction latency and throughput"; "end-to-end model inference QPS by over 3x"; "hierarchical indexing… address[es] the scalability challenges presented by the exponential growth of creatives"; expects "another 1,000x increase in model complexity" — [Engineering at Meta](https://engineering.fb.com/2024/12/02/production-engineering/meta-andromeda-advantage-automation-next-gen-personalized-ads-retrieval-engine/)
- [Meta official] Meta for Business: Andromeda "enabled a 10,000x increase in the complexity of models used for ads retrieval, the first step in the ranking process where we narrow down a pool of tens of millions of ads to the few thousand we consider showing someone… as businesses upload more and more creatives to support a diversification strategy, Meta Andromeda works behind the scenes… to pick the right creative"; "led to an 8% increase in ads quality" — [Meta for Business – AI innovation in ads ranking](https://www.facebook.com/business/news/ai-innovation-in-metas-ads-ranking-driving-advertiser-performance)
- [Meta official] Advantage+ shopping campaigns grew 70% YoY in Q4 2024; ad quality +8% after Andromeda — [Meta announcement Feb 6, 2025 as summarized] [Stackalone](https://www.stackalone.com/blog/meta/advantage-plus-streamlined)
- [Meta official] GEM (Generative Ads Recommendation Model) is Meta's newer ranking foundation model, "4x more efficient at driving ad performance gains" than prior ranking models; rolled out mid-2025 with broad impact by Q4 2025 — [Search Engine Land, Jan 2026](https://searchengineland.com/meta-ai-driven-advertising-system-andromeda-gem-468020)
- [Practitioner] Search Engine Land (Jan 2026): "Meta shifted away from audience-first advertising to creative-first matching… creatives became the primary signal as the system evaluated creative elements like visuals, themes, hooks, and language"; "The days of hyper-segmentation are gone"; set a no-touch window of "a week or 50–75 conversions (whichever comes first)" — [SEL](https://searchengineland.com/meta-ai-driven-advertising-system-andromeda-gem-468020)
- [Practitioner] Foxwell (Nov 2025): "Andromeda reads every element of your creative — the visuals, copy, tone, format — as signals about who might respond… A technical tutorial video naturally finds people seeking education. An emotional brand story reaches those who connect with narratives"; "testing blue versus red backgrounds or slightly different headlines is largely pointless"; watch spend distribution per creative as the signal of audience match — [Foxwell Digital](https://www.foxwelldigital.com/blog/how-andromeda-has-changed-meta-advertising-a-practical-guide); Foxwell (Dec 2025): "Your campaign structure doesn't matter as much as your creative org chart" — [Foxwell Digital](https://www.foxwelldigital.com/blog/the-new-meta-gem-era-how-andromeda-creative-diversification-amp-organic-signals-are-reshaping-performance-marketing)
- [Practitioner] Jon Loomer (Oct 2025) "Meta Andromeda and Creative Diversification: 7 Examples Explained" — exists but page content was not retrievable — [Jon Loomer](https://www.jonloomer.com/meta-andromeda-creative-diversification/)
- [Practitioner] Playbook numbers: "4–6 creative buckets… 12–20 truly different creatives per test wave… hold creative for 5–7 days" — [Marga Bagus, Oct 2025](https://margabagus.com/meta-andromeda-lattice-creative-breadth/); "15–25 diverse creatives… within a single broad ad set" — [PPC Atlas](https://ppcatlas.com/meta-ads-targeting); Motion benchmarks (2026): top-spending accounts ship 12–19 new creatives/week with a 9% winner rate vs 6–7/week and 4% for mid-tier — cited by [Alex Neiman](https://alexneiman.com/meta-advantage-plus-audience-vs-detailed-targeting-2026/)
- [Practitioner, unverified vendor claims] "Creative Similarity Scores above 60% trigger retrieval suppression"; "Brands testing 20+ new ads per month see 65% higher ROAS" — these appear in vendor blogs without a disclosed dataset and should not be treated as Meta facts — [Chatterbuzz](https://www.chatterbuzzmedia.com/blog/meta-andromeda-creative-targeting/); [AdsUploader Andromeda](https://adsuploader.com/blog/meta-andromeda)

### Inferences
- For a wearable brand, "creative as targeting" means building distinct concepts per persona (runner, HYROX/CrossFit athlete, sleep-obsessed biohacker, busy parent, WHOOP/Oura switcher, screen-fatigued minimalist) instead of separate interest ad sets per persona. Each concept should differ in hook, format and message — not just color or headline.
- Andromeda's 10,000× model and hierarchical indexing were built for "exponential growth of creatives"; small accounts with 3 ads give retrieval little to match on, which is one reason practitioners report faster fatigue and volatility on thin creative libraries.

### Gaps
- Meta has not published how Andromeda clusters similar creatives ("entity IDs"), any similarity threshold, or how much creative volume is optimal; those figures are vendor inferences.

## KQ5. Audience-size and learning-phase guidance in 2026 (minimum audience, 50 events/week, lookalike seeds, engagement custom audience windows)

### Takeaway
Meta's official numbers: ad sets typically exit learning after ~50 optimization events in the 7 days after the last significant edit; lookalike seeds need ≥100 people from one country (Meta recommends 1,000–5,000), with 1–10% sizes in Ads Manager (API allows up to 20%); Instagram, Facebook Page and video engagement audiences retain up to 365 days (lead forms 90, website 180); video audiences can be cut at 3s, ThruPlay, 25/50/75/95%. Custom audiences need at least 100 matched people to serve. Practitioners recommend 500K–5M (or 2–10M) estimated audiences for cold campaigns and consolidating rather than splitting small budgets.

### Cited Findings
- [Meta official] "ad sets exit the learning phase as soon as they can deliver stably. This usually occurs after about 50 results in the week after the ad set's last significant edit"; "Learning limited" if not enough results; avoid edits during learning, avoid high ad volumes, use realistic budgets — [Meta Business Help Centre – About the learning phase](https://en-gb.facebook.com/business/help/112167992830700)
- [Meta official] Significant edits that reset learning include targeting, optimization event, bid strategy, budget changes (>~20% per practitioner lists), adding/removing creative — [Meta help via practitioner list] [The Digital Ring](https://thedigitalring.com/insights/meta-ads-learning-phase-what-advertisers-need-to-know)
- [Meta official] Lookalikes: "Origin audiences must have at least 100 members"; "At least 100 seed audience members from a country"; `ratio` 0.01–0.20; "Similarity" = top 1%, "Greater reach" = top 5%; conversion lookalikes need ≥100 converters (200+ recommended); "Seed Custom Audience — Make it as large as possible"; lookalikes take 1–6 hours to populate; Meta is removing the location spec from lookalike creation (location now comes from ad-set targeting) — [Marketing API – Lookalike Audiences](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/marketing-api/audiences/guides/lookalike-audiences/)
- [Meta official] Ads Manager lookalike sizes 1–10%; "Smaller percentages more closely match your source audience"; "you may get better results… if you use an audience made from your best customers rather than… all of your customers"; lookalikes limited/unavailable for Special Ad Categories; targeting limited for audiences including under-18s — [Meta Business Help – About Lookalike Audiences](https://en-gb.facebook.com/business/help/164749007013531)
- [Practitioner citing Meta] Meta recommends 1,000–5,000 source people — [AdsUploader](https://adsuploader.com/blog/meta-audience-targeting); [Coinis](https://coinis.com/how-to/best-way-to-create-instagram-lookalike-audience)
- [Meta official] Up to 500 custom audiences per ad account; Instagram account custom audience = "people who interacted with your Instagram business [account]"; engagement audiences from Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp and Threads — [Meta Business Help – About custom audiences](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/744354708981227)
- [Practitioner citing Meta Business Help] Video engagement thresholds: 3 seconds, ThruPlay, 25%, 50%, 75%, 95% (some guides also list 10s/15s); retention 1–365 days for video, Instagram account and Facebook Page; lead forms 90 days; Shopping 180–365 (sources differ); Instagram options: everyone who engaged, profile visitors, post/ad interactors, savers, message senders — [AdPlay](https://adplay.ai/blog/facebook-engagement-custom-audience); [ConversionStudio, Apr 2026](https://conversion.studio/blog/facebook-custom-audiences); [Stackmatix, Sep 2026](https://www.stackmatix.com/blog/facebook-ads-targeting)
- [Meta official] Website custom audiences up to 180 days; Marketing API `delivery_status` 300 = "audience is smaller than it should be… inactive and cannot be used" — [Custom Audience reference](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/marketing-api/reference/custom-audience/); practitioner sources put the floor at 100 matched people — [AdPlay](https://adplay.ai/blog/facebook-engagement-custom-audience); one source claims 1,000 — [Stackmatix](https://www.stackmatix.com/blog/facebook-ads-targeting) (conflict; Meta's API doc does not state a number)
- [Meta official] Custom/lookalike/saved audiences unused for 2 years begin to expire — [Audiences Overview](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/marketing-api/audiences/overview/)
- [Practitioner] Estimated-audience guidance: 500K–5M for conversion e-com, under 100K "dangerously narrow" — [Adamigo](https://www.adamigo.ai/blog/how-to-build-interest-based-audiences-for-meta-ads); 2–10M recommended — [AdsUploader](https://adsuploader.com/blog/meta-audience-targeting); 1–4M — [KlientBoost](https://www.klientboost.com/facebook/facebook-interest-targeting/); "audiences below ~1M people often can't hit 50/7 at any reasonable budget" — [nowork-studio reference](https://github.com/nowork-studio/notfair/blob/main/meta-ads/manage/references/learning-phase.md)
- [Practitioner] Engagement audiences are "unaffected by browser privacy changes" and need no pixel — [AdPlay](https://adplay.ai/blog/facebook-engagement-custom-audience); ranking of lookalike seed quality puts 75%+ video viewers "Medium" and page engagers "Low-Medium" vs purchasers "Highest" — [ConversionStudio](https://conversion.studio/blog/facebook-custom-audiences)

### Inferences
- For pre-launch, the 50-events/week rule argues for optimizing to a high-volume event (profile visits, ThruPlay, landing page views, or instant-form leads) rather than website sign-ups behind a slow page; at $30–50/day, purchase- or even lead-optimized ad sets will likely sit in "Learning limited."
- A 365-day IG engagement audience plus 50%/75% video-viewer audiences are the natural first lookalike seeds (need ≥100 US members; aim for 1,000+), and can be layered as suggestions rather than fences.

### Gaps
- Direct fetch of Meta's video/Instagram custom-audience help pages failed; thresholds and windows are from practitioner pages that cite those help pages.
- Meta does not publish a numeric minimum custom-audience size; the "100" figure is practitioner consensus.

## KQ6. What can a pre-launch brand optimize for with no sales, and which objective best drives Instagram follows in 2026?

### Takeaway
There is still no "optimize for Instagram follows" goal. Options: Traffic objective → Instagram profile conversion location → "Instagram profile visits" (`PROFILE_VISIT`), or Engagement objective → "Maximize number of Instagram profile and Facebook Page visits" (`PROFILE_AND_PAGE_ENGAGEMENT`), both of which run only on Meta-owned placements and send people to the profile; plus Leads (instant forms), Engagement (ThruPlay/video views, post engagement), Traffic (landing page views/link clicks), and Awareness. Since July 7, 2025 an "Instagram follows" reporting column attributes follows to any campaign, so you can compute follow rate per ad and per objective yourself. Practitioners are split: profile-visit campaigns are the cleanest lever, but Loomer warns follow-optimized delivery tends to produce low-quality followers and that follows are a "vanity metric."

### Cited Findings
- [Meta official] Profile visit ads: campaign `objective` must be `OUTCOME_ENGAGEMENT` or `OUTCOME_TRAFFIC`; `destination_type=INSTAGRAM_PROFILE`; `optimization_goal=PROFILE_VISIT` for Traffic, `PROFILE_AND_PAGE_ENGAGEMENT` for Engagement; Special Ad Categories not supported; supports image, video, carousel; multidestination (IG profile + FB Page) variant exists; API creation "in limited availability" — [Marketing API – Instagram Profile Visit Ads](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/ads-commerce/marketing-api/ad-creative/profile-visit-ads/instagram-profile-visit); [Profile Visit Ads overview](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/ads-commerce/marketing-api/ad-creative/profile-visit-ads)
- [Meta official] Help page "About profile visit ads" exists — [Meta Business Help](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/1112249134336524) (not fetchable; listed for reference)
- [Practitioner documenting UI] Loomer (walkthrough updated Mar 2025): Traffic objective → Instagram profile conversion location → performance goal defaults to Instagram profile visits with "no other options"; ads run only on Instagram placements, no link, button "Visit Instagram profile"; "Delivery is optimised for the visit and nothing after it" — as summarized by [Virallized](https://www.virallized.com/instagram-ads-for-followers); original: [Jon Loomer](https://www.jonloomer.com/create-a-traffic-campaign-with-instagram-profile-conversion-location/)
- [Practitioner] Feb 2026: Loomer lists "Maximize number of Instagram profile and Facebook page visits" as a performance goal for which detailed targeting is suggestion-only — [Jon Loomer](https://www.jonloomer.com/detailed-targeting-suggestion/); Mar 2026 coverage describes it as a new Engagement-objective goal rolling out progressively — [CoreBrief](https://corebrief.in/meta-ads-updates-2026/); [LiveInstagram](https://liveinstagram.net/marketing-news/instagram-profile-visit-optimization-ads/)
- [Practitioner reporting Meta rollout] "Instagram follows" metric became available Jul 7, 2025 (select accounts, then wider by late Aug 2025) across all objectives at campaign/ad set/ad level; "Advertisers cannot yet optimize campaigns specifically for Instagram follows" — [ppc.land, Aug 2025](https://ppc.land/meta-introduces-instagram-follows-metric-for-ads-reporting/); Marketing API added `instagram_profile_visits` insights metric — [Digilogy, Mar 2026](https://news.digilogy.co/meta-profile-visits-goal-scale-instagram-dm-leads/)
- [Practitioner] Van der Hallen (Feb 2026) tactic: build custom metric "IG follow rate = Instagram follows / Instagram profile visits", find top-follow-rate ads from other campaigns, copy them into the profile-visits campaign; "you can't directly optimize for follows" — [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/bramvanderhallen_metaads-activity-7426931036682362880-d_j8)
- [Practitioner] Loomer (Sep 2025): "I'm not aware of any tests to optimize for this metric… Optimizing for page likes has historically yielded only very low-quality page likes… Follows is a vanity metric… The algorithm now favors the content itself over the size of your audience." — [Jon Loomer](https://www.jonloomer.com/qvt/instagram-follows/)
- [Practitioner] No published cost-per-follow benchmark: "Instagram publishes no cost per follower… quoting one would be inventing precision" — [Virallized](https://www.virallized.com/instagram-ads-for-followers); one UK case cites £0.01–£0.03 per profile visit at £3/day — [Sup Growth, Apr 2026](https://supgrowth.com/2026/04/21/instagram-ads-for-more-followers/) (single anecdote)
- [Practitioner] 2026 funnel guides: Reel "profile magnet" ads via Engagement, then retarget 50% video viewers / IG engagers / 30-day profile visitors; because profile visits are a cheap action "Meta will try to find people who click easily—not necessarily your ideal clients. So targeting matters more here than in conversion campaigns" — [JobDoor, Mar 2026](https://www.jobdoor.co.uk/how-to-get-instagram-followers-with-meta-ads/); two-step "video views to cold, then profile visits/conversions to 50%+ viewers" — [Improvado](https://improvado.io/blog/instagram-growth-strategies)
- [Practitioner] Small-budget guidance: "If you're new, start with 'Leads' or 'Messages' instead of 'Sales.' These objectives require fewer conversion signals and optimize faster" — [JobDoor](https://www.jobdoor.co.uk/how-to-get-instagram-followers-with-meta-ads/)
- [Practitioner] Instant-form lead campaigns historically did *not* force detailed-targeting expansion (pre-2025), but Loomer's Feb 2026 list now includes "maximize number of leads" among goals where detailed targeting is suggestion-only — [Jon Loomer, Feb 2025](https://www.jonloomer.com/advantage-plus-sales-app-leads-campaigns/); [Jon Loomer, Feb 2026](https://www.jonloomer.com/detailed-targeting-suggestion/)
- [Meta official] Advantage+ Leads campaigns: "10% lower cost per qualified lead" in early testing with Advantage+ on — [Meta for Business](https://www.facebook.com/business/news/supercharge-performance-advantage-opportunity-score)

### Inferences
- Best-supported 2026 stack for a pre-launch band wanting IG followers and a waitlist: (1) Engagement or Traffic → Instagram profile visits with Reels-first creative, reading the "Instagram follows" column and follow rate per ad; (2) Leads (instant form) for the waitlist, which also survives any later health-data restriction; (3) ThruPlay/video-view campaigns where detailed targeting can still be a hard fence, to build clean 50–95% viewer audiences as lookalike seeds; then retarget IG engagers (365-day) and viewers.
- Because profile-visit optimization chases cheap clicks, this is the one campaign type where using narrower fitness/wearable suggestions (or a video-view seed audience as the source) is defensible.

### Gaps
- No controlled 2025–26 test comparing cost per attributed follow across Engagement-profile-visits vs Traffic-profile-visits vs video views was found; only anecdotes.
- Meta's help page for the new Engagement "profile and Page visits" goal was not retrievable; rollout status by account is uncertain.

## KQ7. Age-minimum rules and ad-copy restrictions relevant to health-adjacent claims (personal health, before/after, implied conditions, wearables)

### Takeaway
Meta's Health & Wellness ad standard requires 18+ targeting for "dietary, health or weight loss products and services" (fitness services/health clubs, general food incl. protein, and dental products are exempt), bans negative body-image/"statements of inferiority" messaging, and — directly relevant — prohibits ads that "claim that results can be achieved solely by use of wearable products." Personal-attributes rules prohibit copy implying the viewer's health condition. Custom audiences named for health conditions are blocked since Sep 2025. Under-18 users can only be targeted by age and location anyway.

### Cited Findings
- [Meta official] Health & Wellness policy (Transparency Center): "Ads promoting or marketing dietary, health or weight loss products and services must be targeted to people at least 18 years or older. Ads must not imply or attempt to generate negative self-perception or declare there is a perfect body type…" Prohibited: "distasteful messaging that could make people feel negatively about the way they look… body-shaming"; weight-loss "side-by-side comparison after the use of a product or transformation for weight loss with exception to fitness classes impact (i.e., Pilates)"; dietary weight-loss products may show use and impact if they "clearly indicate the time taken to achieve noticeable results" — [Meta Transparency Center](https://transparency.meta.com/policies/ad-standards/restricted-goods-services/health-wellness/)
- [Meta official] Business Help Center "About Meta's Health and Wellness advertising policy": advertisers can't run ads that "Depict a close up on a specific body area with pinched fat"; "**Claim that results can be achieved solely by use of wearable products**"; "Contain statements of inferiority about physical appearance" — [Meta Business Help Center](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/2489235377779939)
- [Practitioner quoting policy, Aug 2026] 18+ rule "does not apply to products marketed for general wellbeing such as fitness services and health clubs, general food products including protein products, non-permanent cosmetic and beauty products, or dental products"; ads still can't claim to "cure, heal, or eliminate" listed incurable/terminal diseases ("This does not apply to claims about treating or managing symptoms"); before/after imagery is now judged by the claim rather than the image — [Accelerated Digital Media](https://www.accelerateddigitalmedia.com/insights/guide-to-social-media-health-ad-restrictions-2026/); [WebSearch summary of Adligator/Clikim 2026 coverage](https://adligator.com/blog/meta-health-wellness-ad-policy-update-2026)
- [Meta official] Advantage+ account controls allow setting a minimum age of 18–25 but no maximum — [Marketing API ASC docs](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/marketing-api/advantage-shopping-campaigns/); Meta: "Targeting options are limited for audiences that include people under 18" — [About Lookalike Audiences](https://en-gb.facebook.com/business/help/164749007013531)
- [Meta official] Since Sep 2, 2025 custom/lookalike audiences whose names/rules suggest health conditions are blocked (`operation_status` 471) — [Marketing API – Custom Audience](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/marketing-api/reference/custom-audience/)
- [Meta official] Business Tools Terms prohibit sharing "information about an individual's physical or mental health, such as: Diseases, medical conditions and injuries, sexual and reproductive health, mental health and psychological states" — quoted in [Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/meta-ads-restrictions-health-wellness-campaigns-453094)
- [Meta official, 2022 dated] Meta gave users controls to see fewer ads about weight loss, gambling, etc. — [Reuters, Nov 2021](https://www.reuters.com/technology/facebook-removes-target-options-advertisers-some-topics-2021-11-09/)
- [Practitioner] Sensitive Ad Categories (data-sharing) are distinct from Special Ad Categories (housing/credit/employment/politics); profile-visit ads do not support Special Ad Categories — [Foxwell Digital](https://www.foxwelldigital.com/blog/meta-special-ad-category-vs-sensitive-ad-category-whats-the-difference); [Meta developer docs](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/ads-commerce/marketing-api/ad-creative/profile-visit-ads/instagram-profile-visit)

### Inferences
- A screenless health band should default to 18+ (Meta's own ad-account minimum-age control makes this a one-time setting), avoid "wear it and you'll sleep better/lose weight" phrasing (the wearable clause), avoid pinched-fat/body-shaming visuals and before/after body transformations, and avoid second-person condition implications ("struggling with insomnia?"). Performance/fitness framing ("train smarter", "know your recovery") sits in the exempted general-wellbeing zone.
- Naming hygiene applies everywhere: audiences ("sleep_apnea_engagers"), custom conversions ("hrv_quiz_complete"), URLs and UTMs can each trigger automated flags independent of ad copy.
