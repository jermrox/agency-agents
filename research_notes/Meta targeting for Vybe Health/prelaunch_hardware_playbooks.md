# Pre-Launch Hardware/Wearable Brands on Meta Ads: Audience Building and Demand Capture (2023–2026 evidence, older examples flagged)

Scope note for the writer: Vybe Health context = screenless health band, no subscription, launch May 2027, ~30 Instagram followers, no Pixel data, US market, budget under $5K/month, and the founder does NOT want "join the waitlist" to be the message. Numbers below are labeled as **[Meta-official]**, **[brand-reported]**, **[agency-claimed]**, or **[practitioner test]**. Several primary pages (Meta Business Help Center, Jon Loomer, LaunchBoom, Fast Company, WordStream, etc.) were blocked by the research proxy for direct fetch; where content was obtained via a secondary reader (Exa) it is cited by its original URL.

## Key Question 1: Case studies and post-mortems of pre-launch wearable/hardware campaigns on Meta (Oura, WHOOP, Ultrahuman, Levels, Lumen, Nutrisense, Rabbit, Humane, Nothing, Kickstarter hardware)

### Takeaway
No wearable brand has published a numeric Meta-ads pre-launch post-mortem (spend, CPL, cost per follow). What exists is (a) founder retrospectives that consistently say "start the audience 6–12 months earlier than we did, seed influential testers earlier, and treat Facebook/IG as the conversion channel," (b) crowdfunding-hardware case studies with hard CPL numbers ($1.50–$8 per email lead, ~$2 average; $1 reservation leads converting ~25–30% to buyers), and (c) the cautionary AI-hardware cases (Humane, Rabbit) showing hype-first pre-launch without product proof destroys trust.

### Cited Findings

**Oura (2015 Kickstarter — older example, but the only founder post-mortem on pre-launch audience building for a health ring)**
- Oura's Kickstarter (Aug 18–Sep 24, 2015) raised $651,803 from 2,383 backers against a $100,000 goal (652%); average pledge $273.52; average 64.4 backers/day — [BackerKit/BackerTracker](https://www.backerkit.com/projects/oura/oura-ring-improve-sleep-perform-better); [Kickstarter page](https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/oura/oura-ring-improve-sleep-perform-better/posts/1347545) **[brand-reported]**
- Oura hit its $100K goal in one day, partly because it gave the Quantified Self community a few-hours head start on 500 limited $199 rings (minimum then rose to $229) — [Quantified Self](https://quantifiedself.com/blog/oura-ring-on-kickstarter/); [ArcticStartup](https://arcticstartup.com/the-worlds-first-wellness-ring-hits-kickstarter-goal/)
- Co-founder Petteri Lahtela on closing day: "Facebook is a platform that converts quite nicely into pledges on Kickstarter. We only had some 450 people on our Facebook page, which we opened a couple of weeks before the campaign; now we have something like 7,500... Still, we should have started telling the story much, much earlier on." Also: "We should have given products to some influential people for testing. We did that, but a little bit too late." and "we should concentrate more on generating traffic from outside the Kickstarter community" — [Nordic Startup News, Sep 2015](https://nordicstartupnews.com/4-lessons-from-a-650000-kickstarter-campaign/) **[brand-reported]**
- Oura's later (2024) paid social: "Meta ads are still the go-to channel," running 140+ ad variations at a time, adding 37 new ads in ~2 months around stress, sleep deprivation, pregnancy; uses whitelisting (ads through influencer handles); VP Growth Manbir Sodhia: "We don't limit our audience targeting to any particular group... we want to reach really broad audiences." — [The Digital Chapter, Mar 2024](https://www.thedigitalchapter.com/p/oura-ring-growthmarketing-casestudy) **[third-party teardown quoting brand]**
- Oura reached $5.2B valuation by April 2025 having started from that Kickstarter — [Fortune, Apr 2025](https://fortune.com/europe/article/finland-oura-kickstarter-valuation-wearable-tech/)

**WHOOP (2012–2018 early growth — older, but directly relevant to a screenless band)**
- WHOOP is a screenless wrist wearable; first product shipped September 2015 at ~$500; by Sept 2015 users included LeBron James and Michael Phelps, reached "through their personal trainers and performance coaches"; famous customers "didn't translate into reliable sales"; in 2017 it was "a week away from declaring bankruptcy"; 2018 pivot to membership from $199/yr; 2.7M+ users and $10.1B valuation by mid-2026 — [CNBC, Jun 9 2026](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/09/whoop-founder-how-i-built-a-10-billion-fitness-wearable-company.html) **[brand-reported via interview]**
- Ahmed (How I Built This, Jul 2025): the first consumer launch was "a snooze fest"; "The worst thing that could have happened to Whoop and would have killed the company, ironically, was selling 100,000 units in 2014, 15, 16, even 17"; trainers were seeded product free but athletes were then charged; "our engagement metrics became the lifeblood of our story" — [Wave AI notes of HIBT episode](https://pod.wave.co/podcast/how-i-built-this-with-guy-raz/whoop-will-ahmed-a1f5cef3); [Guy Raz newsletter, Jul 24 2025](https://guyraz.substack.com/p/build-for-use-not-flash)
- WHOOP's interactive video campaign (TrueX) hit a 9.1% CTR, "9x the industry benchmark" — [Infillion case study](https://infillion.com/case-studies/whoop/) **[vendor-claimed; not Meta]**
- Third-party analyses attribute WHOOP growth to founder-led content and the WHOOP Podcast, not paid social specifics — [NoGood, Jan 2025](https://nogood.io/2025/01/13/whoop-marketing-strategy/) **[agency analysis]**

**Levels (CGM, 2019–2022 pre-general-availability)**
- Levels ran invitation-only from 2019 to 2022 and built a 200,000+ waitlist before general availability; "vast majority of 200K+ waitlist members cited podcasts as primary discovery channel"; 500+ education-first blog articles drove 2M+ annual visitors — [0 to Traction product timeline](https://0-to-traction.com/products/levels); CEO Sam Corcos confirmed "more than 200,000 users on our waitlist ahead of public availability" (Apr 2022) — [Fitt Insider Q&A](https://insider.fitt.co/startup-qa-levels-ceo-sam-corcos/) **[brand-reported]**
- Corcos personally phone-interviewed ~600 waitlist signups to derive the marketing strategy — [Adamant podcast transcript](https://www.admnt.com/all-episodes/levels-2)
- Levels' head of growth framed it as a "pull product... instead of you pushing ads on people, people come to you"; heavy in-house content investment — [Levels podcast #80](https://www.levelshealth.com/podcasts/80-building-a-startup-a-qa-with-latitude-sam-corcos)
- Levels waitlist was 37,000 by Oct 2020 — [Mixergy #2000](https://www.podcast24.co.uk/episodes/mixergy-startup-stories-with-1000-entrepreneurs-and-businesses/2000-how-levels-got-a-37-000-person-waitlist-txX0QSx4Fs)

**Lumen (breath metabolism device)**
- Podcast advertising, not Meta, became Lumen's #1 growth channel; ad spend scaled 10x in 9 months in 2024 at ~$15 average CPM — [ADOPTER Media case study, Feb 2025](https://adopter.media/case_study/scale-podcast-advertising/) **[agency-claimed]**

**Nutrisense (CGM program) — Meta account rebuild 2025/26**
- Agency ran 8 creative concepts/month, each 2–3 briefs with variants; shifted primary KPI from CPL to new-visitor % and lead-to-order rate; a stricter-exclusions ad set (excluding visitors, leads, and content engagers) pushed new-visitor share into the "low 90s%", lowered CPM/frequency, and Meta self-allocated ~half the campaign budget to it in 7 days; quiz-based landing pages used for pricing tests — [Taikun Digital case study](https://taikundigital.com/case-studies/full-funnel-meta-rebuild-for-a-health-and-wellness-brand) **[agency-claimed, in-flight, no absolute numbers]**

**Rabbit R1 (AI hardware, Jan 2024)**
- $199 device; 50,000 preorders in five days after a CES 2024 launch video; company had internally hoped for 500 units on launch day — [Fast Company, Jan 22 2024](https://www.fastcompany.com/91013196/how-design-drove-10m-in-pre-orders-for-rabbit-r1-ai-hardware) **[brand-reported]**; 20,000 pre-orders in 48 hours — [TechCrunch, Apr 2024](https://techcrunch.com/2024/04/24/rabbits-r1-is-a-little-ai-gadget-that-grows-on-you/)
- Retrospective critique: "tamping down expectations might have been a savvier marketing strategy than fomenting irrational exuberance" — [Fast Company](https://www.fastcompany.com/91118796/humane-ai-pin-rabbit-r1-reviews)

**Humane AI Pin (2023–2025) — cautionary post-mortem**
- Marketed "for a year with ads and at glitzy events like Paris Fashion Week"; ~10,000 orders by early April 2024 vs. 100,000 hoped; no head of marketing until after launch; founders ignored alpha-tester warnings — [NYT, Jun 2024](https://www.nytimes.com/2024/06/06/technology/humane-ai-pin.html); [Ars Technica summary](https://arstechnica.com/gadgets/2024/06/report-humane-ai-pin-did-7-million-in-sales-wants-to-sell-for-1-billion/)
- Returns outpaced sales May–Aug 2024; ~$9M lifetime sales; ~7,000 units still in customer hands by Aug 2024; shut down Feb 2025, sold to HP for $116M after raising $230M — [The Verge, Aug 2024](https://www.theverge.com/2024/8/7/24211339/humane-ai-pin-more-daily-returns-than-sales); [The Verge, Feb 2025](https://www.theverge.com/gadgets/615135/humane-ai-pin-shutdown-disaster)

**Nothing (Phone 1, 2022 — older)**
- Pre-launch relied on drip-fed reveals, street posters, community investors (several thousand via Crowdcube), ~100,000 livestream viewers at launch; Verge called the hype-vs-delivery gap "the double-edged sword of hype" — [The Verge, Jul 2022](https://45179bb5-2a1f-4bc9-a93f-f86df27a1ff5.atarimworker.io/2022/7/14/23216359/nothing-phone-1-london-launch-hype)

**Crowdfunding hardware with hard Meta numbers**
- Flexr foldable pull-up bar (2017 — older): spent $9,257 on Facebook ads pre-launch for 4,624 email leads (~$2/lead); first leads cost ~$8, ended at ~$1.50 after testing; gating the video behind the email form cut CPL 50%; still images beat video/carousel by 25%+; $32,756 in first 24 hours; >$100K of $177K raised attributable to Facebook ads; started pre-launch ads only ~14 days out — [MarketingStrategy.com](https://www.marketingstrategy.com/marketing-strategy-studies/kickstarter/) **[brand-reported]**
- Seatmate Chairs (2024 Kickstarter, $488,623 from 1,696 backers): founder spent "about a thousand dollars on outreach ads on Instagram and gained my first thousand followers" (~$1/follow) but judged them low-intent; then grew 1K→18K followers via a targeted-engagement service, routed every new follower via welcome DM to a LaunchBoom $1 reservation funnel; 26% of waitlist → backer; $216K on day one — [Magnet case study](https://www.marketingbymagnet.com/case-studies/seatmate-chairs) **[vendor case study quoting founder]**
- Hammerleds (moto hardware, 2024/25): 4,800 landing-page visitors Nov–Mar, 19% subscribed (948 leads), nurtured with 3 videos/emails, 238 pre-orders = 25% lead-to-order — [BusyMonday](https://busymonday.io/case-study-hammerleds-preorder-launch-campaugn/) **[agency-claimed]**
- Bumpboxx BB-777 ($7M+ Kickstarter, 2025/26): 1.5-month pre-launch; ~2,300 VIPs in WhatsApp groups; VIP launch emails opened >70%; retargeting ads that simply taught people how to back on Kickstarter returned 4.8–6.8 ROAS; live-campaign ROAS 4.4 — [TCF case study](https://www.tcf.team/case-studies/bb-777-full-case-study) **[agency-claimed]**
- Sourhouse (2022 Kickstarter, $103,948 from 1,000+ backers): launched Instagram a year before; ~1,000 IG followers + ~1,000 newsletter subs at launch; giveaway with bigger partners (Bob's Red Mill) drove thousands of views; a digital cookbook lead magnet later drove 80,000+ email subs and "we even push many digital ads to the lead magnet instead of trying to make a direct sale" — [Buffer, Jul 2024](https://buffer.com/resources/built-following-before-product/) **[brand-reported]**
- LaunchBoom (crowdfunding agency, 1,000+ creators, $175M+ raised): "someone who puts down a $1 deposit is 30 times more likely to buy than someone who only gives their email"; recommends Meta ads in pre-launch to reach "superfans"; advertising is typically 15–25% of funding goal — [LaunchBoom 17 steps, May 2025](https://www.launchboom.com/crowdfunding-guides/how-to-launch-a-successful-kickstarter-campaign/); [LaunchBoom cost guide, Mar 2025](https://www.launchboom.com/crowdfunding-guides/how-much-does-a-kickstarter-campaign-cost-updated/) **[agency-claimed]**; targets of "<$3 email signups and <$10 VIP reservations" and a $1,000–$2,000 testing budget are reported in a third-party review — [Hyperstarter review of LaunchBoom](https://www.hyperstarter.com/crowdfunding-agency/launchboom-accelerator-system) **[secondary]**
- Jellop (Kickstarter's ads partner, Jun 2026): price rewards at 3–5x incremental production cost; break-even ROAS formula ROAS = 1/(75% − COGS%), i.e., ~2–2.5x minimum; five-figure campaigns average <$200/backer, six-figure >$250/backer — [Kickstarter Updates, Jun 2026](https://updates.kickstarter.com/beyond-ads-what-creators-ask-us-most-about-running-a-successful-kickstarter/) **[agency/platform-claimed]**
- Pre-launch list-building via landing pages, FB lead ads, and reservation funnels typically targets 5,000–20,000 qualified leads at $5K–$25K; testing at ~$20/ad set for 3–4 days, scaling winners ~20% every two days — [Hyperstarter Jellop review](https://www.hyperstarter.com/crowdfunding-agency/jellop-facebook-ads-secrets) **[secondary/agency]**

### Inferences
- The consistent founder regret across Oura (2015), WHOOP, and Sourhouse is starting the audience too late and seeding credible testers too late; Vybe's 8-month runway is an asset only if audience building starts now.
- WHOOP's "seed trainers, charge athletes, watch 24/7 wear-rate" pattern is a no-subscription-compatible playbook: seed a small number of high-influence practitioners (coaches, PTs, sleep clinicians) and use their real usage as the content engine, rather than paying macro-influencers.
- The Humane/Rabbit contrast with WHOOP ("if we'd sold 100K units year one we'd have died") argues for a modest, proof-heavy pre-launch narrative rather than a hype-heavy one, especially for a first-generation health device.
- Crowdfunding CPL data ($1.50–$3 email, <$10 reservation, 25–30% lead-to-order for $1 reservations) is the most concrete benchmark available for a hardware pre-launch on Meta, even though Vybe is not crowdfunding.

### Gaps
- No public numeric Meta-ads pre-launch data for Ultrahuman, Hume Health, Luna Band, Bee, Limitless, Eight Sleep, or Withings launches. Ultrahuman founder interviews (Memfault Coredump #017, Oct 2025; Fitt Insider) cover hardware/ops, not paid acquisition. Bee/Limitless coverage is about acquisitions (Amazon bought Bee Jul 2025; Meta bought Limitless Dec 2025) not pre-launch tactics.
- Oura's early paid-social spend and CAC have never been disclosed.

## Key Question 2: Which Meta objective is best for growing Instagram followers in 2025–2026, and typical cost per follow in fitness/tech

### Takeaway
There is still no "optimize for follows" goal; the closest is the profile-visit ad (Traffic objective → Instagram profile conversion location → optimization goal PROFILE_VISIT, or Engagement objective → PROFILE_AND_PAGE_ENGAGEMENT). Since July 2025 Meta reports an "Instagram follows" metric across all objectives, so cost per follow can be measured per ad. Reported cost per follow ranges from ~$0.25–$1 (agency/boost-level tests, non-US-heavy) to ~$1 for a US hardware brand's cold Instagram ads; there is no reliable fitness/tech-specific benchmark.

### Cited Findings
- **[Meta-official]** Profile visit ads: "When individuals see a profile visit ad... they see an ad with a Visit Profile or Visit Page call-to-action button... encourage them to consider following or engaging"; creatable in Ads Manager or via Boost in the Instagram app — [Meta Business Help Center: About profile visit ads](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/1112249134336524)
- **[Meta-official]** Marketing API: Instagram profile visit ads support `OUTCOME_ENGAGEMENT` and `OUTCOME_TRAFFIC`; `destination_type=INSTAGRAM_PROFILE`; optimization goal is `PROFILE_VISIT` under Traffic and `PROFILE_AND_PAGE_ENGAGEMENT` under Engagement; CTA `VIEW_INSTAGRAM_PROFILE`; requires the FB Page connected to the IG business account — [Meta Developer Docs: Instagram Profile Visit Ads](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/ads-commerce/marketing-api/ad-creative/profile-visit-ads/instagram-profile-visit); new multidestination variant (IG profile and/or FB Page, Meta picks) — [Meta Developer Docs](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/ads-commerce/marketing-api/ad-creative/profile-visit-ads/multidestination-profile-visit)
- **[Meta-official via trade press]** "Instagram follows" metric added to Ads Manager reporting July 7, 2025; available at campaign/ad set/ad level across all objectives; enables a profile-visit→follow conversion rate — [PPC Land, Aug 26 2025](https://ppc.land/meta-introduces-instagram-follows-metric-for-ads-reporting/)
- Meta is rolling out an Advantage+-style "recommended setup" to Traffic, Awareness and Engagement objectives (manual options remain) — [Meta Business Help Center](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/272348126756182)
- The Engagement objective "does not have a 'follower growth' optimization type for Instagram. Page Likes is a Facebook-only option" — [Coinis guide](https://coinis.com/how-to/best-way-to-set-instagram-ads-objective-for-engagement) **[practitioner]**
- Jon Loomer documented the Traffic → Instagram profile conversion location setup in Aug 2023 (page blocked for fetch; setup confirmed by the API docs above) — [Jon Loomer, Aug 2023](https://www.jonloomer.com/create-a-traffic-campaign-with-instagram-profile-conversion-location/)
- **[practitioner test, tiny budget]** Tom DuPree III tested 4 setups at $1/day x 3 days each with existing Reels: in-app boosts optimized for views produced "a ton of views and likes but no followers"; an in-app boost with interest targeting got 3.92% CTR and some follows; an Ads Manager Traffic campaign optimized for profile visits targeting a lookalike of 365-day IG engagers won (5.25% CTR, most profile visits); ~24 new followers over the week on ~$15 total (≈$0.60/follow, not cleanly attributable) — [tomdupreeiii.com](https://tomdupreeiii.com/newsletter/i-tried-4-different-ways-to-grow-on-instagram-with-ads)
- **[brand-reported]** Seatmate (US pet-office chair, pre-Kickstarter): ~$1,000 of Instagram ads → first ~1,000 followers (~$1/follow), founder judged them low-intent — [Magnet case study](https://www.marketingbymagnet.com/case-studies/seatmate-chairs)
- **[agency-claimed]** Pulsenmore (at-home prenatal ultrasound medtech, pre-availability, 8 non-US markets, women 20–45): 11,551 new followers across TikTok/Meta/IG; follower cost fell to $0.93; 143,388 Meta ThruPlays at under $0.01 each; UGC/creator-led video was the top format — [House of Marketers](https://houseofmarketers.com/pulsenmore-paid-social-awareness-case-study/)
- **[aggregator]** "There is no official cost per follower rate, because follows are not a standard ad objective"; 10,000 followers via paid typically "$20,000 to $40,000" ($2–$4/follow) — [Fibre2Fashion guide, 2025](https://emerge.fibre2fashion.com/blogs/10719/cost-of-gaining-10-000-instagram-followers-with-paid-marketing-in-2025-a-complete-guide) (unsourced estimate)
- **[aggregator benchmarks]** Instagram cost per engagement $0.03–$0.08; CPM $5–$15 in 2025 — [AdNabu](https://blog.adnabu.com/meta-ads/instagram-ads-cost/); [Karola Karlson](https://karolakarlson.com/instagram-ads-cost-and-bidding/)
- Engagement objective is a mid-funnel tool: "Meta's algorithm finds users... who have a history of interacting with ads and content... It does not optimize for purchases" — [Coinis](https://coinis.com/how-to/best-way-to-set-instagram-ads-objective-for-engagement)
- Warning: "Profile visits can rise without follower quality improving" — [Seer Interactive](https://www.seerinteractive.com/insights/meta-ad-objectives-and-how-to-use-them)
- Boosting for a "Profile Visit" goal on posts that already over-index organically (>50 likes / 3x average engagement in 24h) is a recommended low-risk way to build followers; $20/day/ad set for 3–4 days is a common test minimum — [GetKoro, Feb 2026](https://getkoro.app/blog/instagram-ads-vs-boosted-posts) **[practitioner]**

### Inferences
- For Vybe, run the profile-visit ad via Ads Manager (Traffic objective, IG profile destination, PROFILE_VISIT) rather than the in-app boost, so the "Instagram follows" column can be used to compute cost per follow per creative and so custom/lookalike audiences can be applied.
- A realistic planning assumption for US health/tech is $0.75–$2 per follow from cold traffic in 2026 (bracketed by Seatmate's ~$1 and the $2–$4 aggregator estimate); Pulsenmore's $0.93 blended and the ~$0.60 hobby test are lower bounds achieved with creator-led video or warm lookalikes.
- Because Andromeda-era delivery matches creative to people, the follow rate will be driven more by whether the Reel itself earns a profile tap (hook + "who is this brand" curiosity) than by objective choice.

### Gaps
- No published fitness/wearable-specific cost-per-follow benchmark from Meta or a large dataset; every number above is a single account.
- Could not verify whether Meta has since added a follow-optimization goal (search of official docs as of Sep 2026 shows only PROFILE_VISIT / PROFILE_AND_PAGE_ENGAGEMENT).

## Key Question 3: Seeding audiences from zero (no pixel): engager audiences, video-view audiences, lead-form audiences, customer lists, lookalikes — minimum sizes and time to usefulness

### Takeaway
Everything Vybe needs to seed audiences lives inside Meta and needs no pixel: Instagram-account engagement audiences (up to 730 days retention per the API; UI commonly shows 365), video-view audiences by watch depth, lead-form audiences (90 days), and customer lists. Lookalikes technically need 100 people in one country; Meta recommends 1,000–5,000. In the Andromeda era, practitioners increasingly use these as Advantage+ "suggestions" rather than hard targeting.

### Cited Findings
- **[Meta-official]** Custom audiences from on-platform interaction include an "Instagram account custom audience: Target people who interacted with your Instagram business account"; video engagement, lead-form engagement, Facebook Page engagement, shopping, and Instant Experience audiences are also available; up to 500 custom audiences per ad account — [Meta Business Help Center: About custom audiences](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/744354708981227)
- **[Meta-official]** Instagram business-profile engagement rules include: everyone who engaged (`ig_business_profile_all`), profile visitors, people who messaged, people who saved organic content or an ad, plus per-action rules (ad/organic like, comment, share, save, CTA click, carousel swipe). Max retention: Instagram business profile 730 days; Page 730 days; Lead-gen ads 90 days; Shopping 365 days — [Meta Marketing API: Engagement Custom Audiences](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/marketing-api/audiences/guides/engagement-custom-audiences/)
- **[Meta-official]** Lookalike source: "Origin audiences must have at least 100 members"; needs at least 100 seed members from one country (or set `allow_international_seeds`); for conversion-based lookalikes "at least 100 unique conversions... 200 or more... recommended" — [Meta Marketing API: Lookalike Audiences](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/marketing-api/audiences/guides/lookalike-audiences/); Meta's help text quoted by Jon Loomer: "Your source audience must contain at least 100 people from a single country"; Loomer found the minimum "isn't consistently enforced" — [Jon Loomer, Aug 2023](https://www.jonloomer.com/qvt/lookalike-audiences-error/)
- Meta recommends a source audience of 1,000–5,000 people; "the quality of your audience also matters" — [Meta: About lookalike audiences](https://en-gb.facebook.com/business/help/164749007013531) (quality statement on page; the 1,000–5,000 figure is restated by [Coinis](https://coinis.com/how-to/create-instagram-lookalike-audience) and [Ankita Mankotia](https://ankitamankotia.com/lookalike-audiences-in-meta-ads-a-complete-guide/))
- Audiences unused in active ad sets for 2+ years are flagged/removed on a rolling basis — [Meta Marketing API: Audiences Overview](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/marketing-api/audiences/overview/)
- Video-view audience thresholds available: 3s, 10s, 15s, ThruPlay, 25/50/75/95%; practitioners treat 75%+ viewers as the intent threshold and 25% as reach; video, IG and Page audiences show up to 365 days in the UI; lead form 90 days — [AdPlay.ai](https://adplay.ai/blog/facebook-engagement-custom-audience) **[practitioner]**; [Agorapulse](https://www.agorapulse.com/blog/facebook-advertising/video-view-retargeting/)
- Practitioner guidance: start engagement retention at 30 or 90 days and expand to 180/365 if too small to exit learning; a creator account cannot open every engagement type, so use a business account — [Coinis](https://coinis.com/how-to/engagement-instagram-custom-audience)
- 100–500 matched profiles yields an inconsistent lookalike; 500–1,000 starts to show pattern recognition — [SocialRails](https://socialrails.com/blog/facebook-lookalike-audience-guide) **[practitioner]**
- Post-Andromeda advice: feed customer/engager lists to Advantage+ as a suggestion, because "Advantage+ already does the look-alike modeling internally"; lookalikes "serve as useful signal inputs rather than hard boundaries" — [Jetfuel Agency](https://jetfuel.agency/create-facebook-lookalike-audiences-in-7-steps-2025-guide/); [MTM Agency, Oct 2025](https://themtmagency.com/blog/meta-andromeda-october-2025-update-why-creative-diversity-now-defines-ad-performance)
- Oura's growth VP explicitly targets broad, not segments — [The Digital Chapter](https://www.thedigitalchapter.com/p/oura-ring-growthmarketing-casestudy)
- The pre-launch Sleep-supplement brand case (zero audience → 52K followers + 8,400 signups in 6 months) used paid only to boost top organic posts to lookalike audiences and a lead magnet to convert followers to email — [Scalarly](https://scalarly.com/case-studies/brand-launch-consumer.html) **[agency-claimed, EU]**

### Inferences
- With ~30 followers, Vybe should run the first 4–6 weeks as a video-view/ThruPlay + profile-visit "seeding" phase whose real output is engagement custom audiences (video 50%/75%/95% viewers, IG profile visitors, savers, messagers). At the Pulsenmore-style sub-$0.01 ThruPlay rate even $500 buys tens of thousands of video viewers, so a 1,000+ person 75%-view seed and a 100+ person "saver/messager" seed are attainable within the first month.
- A 1% US lookalike from 1,000–5,000 high-intent engagers is the first sensible lookalike (weeks 5–8); until then, use broad/Advantage+ with the engager audiences as suggestions, per Andromeda guidance.
- Lead-form audiences expire at 90 days, so any lead-form leads must be exported to a customer list (email/SMS) to persist across the 8-month runway.

### Gaps
- No Meta-official statement of "time to usefulness" for lookalikes; the 1,000–5,000 recommendation is the only official sizing.
- Could not directly load the Meta Business Help Center page text for lookalike size (blocked); relied on the developer docs and secondary quotes.

## Key Question 4: Budget structure for a small account ($20–50/day): ad sets, escaping learning-limited, when to consolidate into Advantage+/CBO, test-and-scale cadence

### Takeaway
At $20–50/day the account cannot generate 50 optimization events/week on anything expensive, so the practical answer is: (1) choose a cheap, high-frequency optimization event (ThruPlay, profile visit, lead) so 50/week is reachable at the budget; (2) run one campaign per objective with one broad ad set and many diverse creatives (Andromeda guidance), not many small ad sets; (3) test creative, not audiences; (4) scale spend ≤20–30% every 3–5 days.

### Cited Findings
- **[Meta-official rule restated]** Learning phase requires ~50 optimization events per ad set within 7 days; count is per ad set (ads share the pool), rolling 7 days; only the chosen optimization event counts; if you can't hit 50, "use a higher-frequency proxy event"; consolidate ad sets; hold 7 days without significant edits — [AdLibrary explainer, May 2026](https://adlibrary.com/posts/meta-ads-learning-phase-50-events-guide) **[practitioner restating Meta help]**
- Budget math: a $20/day ad set with a $50 CPA "is mathematically limiting" (50 events = ~7/day); CBO helps funnel spend to the ad set showing earliest signal; aim for 1M+ potential reach (under 500K raises learning-limited risk) — [Cometly](https://www.cometly.com/post/facebook-ads-learning-phase-optimization); [Modern Marketing Institute](https://www.modernmarketinginstitute.com/blog/how-to-exit-the-meta-ads-learning-phase-fast-and-start-scaling-profitably-in-2026) **[practitioner]**
- Small-budget structure: "If your total monthly budget is $5,000, run two to three ad sets maximum rather than six to eight"; raise spend "no more than 20-30% every 3-5 days"; broad + creative variety beats interest stacks — [Stackmatix](https://www.stackmatix.com/blog/facebook-ads-minimum-budget-requirements); [Spiral](https://spiral.ad/learn/scaling-meta-2025) **[practitioner]**
- Solo/side-project budgets of $5–$30/day are viable but "at least $20–30/day" is recommended for anything conversion-like; below that campaigns often never exit learning — [GetKoro, Jan 2026](https://getkoro.app/blog/instagram-ads-on-a-budget)
- Common Thread Collective (Apr 2025): moving from "every new concept got a new campaign" to consolidation into fewer campaigns/ad sets with ABO for spend control; "we really use creative for scale... but it's diverse creative"; only test 1-day view attribution if struggling for volume — [CTC 2025 Guide to Meta Media Buying](https://commonthreadco.com/blogs/ecommerce-playbook/2025-guide-to-meta-media-buying) **[agency]**
- Andromeda (Meta's rebuilt retrieval engine; rolled out Dec 2024–Oct 2025): Meta's guidance is broad targeting, Advantage+ placements, one campaign per goal with one broad ad set and many meaningfully different ads (the old ~6-ads-per-ad-set ceiling no longer applies; 10–50 cited); Meta's internal data suggested ~8–10% performance gains when structured this way; Meta introduced a "creative similarity score"; interest targeting/lookalikes become suggestions — [MTM Agency, Oct 2025](https://themtmagency.com/blog/meta-andromeda-october-2025-update-why-creative-diversity-now-defines-ad-performance); [Scoreify, Oct 2025](https://blog.scoreify.net/p/meta-s-andromeda-update-everything-you-need-to-know-about-changes-in-ad-delivery-and-what-works-best); [Blaze Media, Oct 2025](https://blazemedia.co.uk/2025/10/29/meta-andromeda-update-guide); [Rêve, Nov 2025](https://reve.agency/meta-andromeda-update/) **[practitioner interpretations; Meta has published little directly]**
- Creative cadence heuristics post-Andromeda: 50% refreshes of winners / 30% adjacent angles / 20% new swings; monthly or twice-monthly drops; prune zero-spend ads; formats per concept: 9:16 video, 1:1 talking head, static, carousel — [Scoreify](https://blog.scoreify.net/p/meta-s-andromeda-update-everything-you-need-to-know-about-changes-in-ad-delivery-and-what-works-best); [Cocan Media](https://cocanmedia.com/blog/meta-andromeda-update-facebook-ads-guide)
- Rêve caution: "Do not run more ads than your budget can properly test at once" — [Rêve](https://reve.agency/meta-andromeda-update/)
- Brands under ~$10K/month should "focus on building a library of 20–30 tested assets rather than aggressive weekly rotation" — [AI Content Drop case study](https://aicontentdrop.com/blog/supplement-brand-ai-ads-case-study) **[vendor-claimed]**
- Crowdfunding testers: ~$20/ad set for 3–4 days judged on cost per lead, scale winners ~20% every two days — [Hyperstarter Jellop review](https://www.hyperstarter.com/crowdfunding-agency/jellop-facebook-ads-secrets)
- Small-account attribution note: Nutrisense moved to 7-day click + 1-day engaged view, dropping 1-day view — [Taikun](https://taikundigital.com/case-studies/full-funnel-meta-rebuild-for-a-health-and-wellness-brand)
- 2025 industry benchmarks (WordStream/LocaliQ, 1,000+ campaigns): Traffic-objective CTR 1.71%, CPC $0.70; Leads-objective CPC $1.92, CPL $27.66 (+20.9% YoY) — [WordStream Facebook Ads Benchmarks 2025](https://www.wordstream.com/blog/facebook-ads-benchmarks-2025); Health & Fitness lead-ads CPL reported at $52.98 — [LocaliQ](https://localiq.com/blog/facebook-advertising-benchmarks/) **[aggregator; mostly local-service advertisers]**
- Metricool (44,355 advertisers, H1 2026): Meta cost per lead $6.00 (+14% YoY) across its SMB-heavy sample — [Netinfluencer summary, Sep 2026](https://www.netinfluencer.com/meta-ad-roas-fell-nearly-1-usd-per-dollar-spent-in-2026-report-finds/)

### Inferences
- At $30/day (~$900/mo): one Engagement/Traffic campaign (video views or profile visits) with a single broad US 25–54 ad set and 6–12 genuinely different Reels can clear 50 ThruPlays/profile visits per week easily; a second campaign optimized for leads ($10–15/day) will likely sit learning-limited (CPL $6–$30 → 2–15 leads/week) and should be judged on CPL trend, not learning status.
- Consolidate: two campaigns max (audience-build + lead capture), each one ad set, budget at campaign level. Add a third (retargeting engagers) only once the engager pool exceeds ~10K, otherwise frequency will spike at even $5/day.
- Test creative concepts (angles), not audiences; kill anything Meta won't spend on after 3–4 days; refresh 3–5 new creatives every two weeks.

### Gaps
- Meta's Business Help Center learning-phase page itself was blocked; the 50-event rule is well established but cited here via practitioners.
- No published test of Advantage+ Engagement/Traffic "recommended setup" vs. manual for profile-visit campaigns.

## Key Question 5: Pre-launch offer design that doesn't depend on a waitlist — follows, saves, notify-me, quizzes, lead magnets, giveaways with creators, UGC seeding

### Takeaway
Evidence favors (a) education-first content that earns saves/shares/follows (Levels' 500-article, zero-CTA approach; Sourhouse; the sleep-supplement launch), (b) a lead magnet or quiz as the email-capture mechanism instead of "join the waitlist" (Sourhouse's cookbook, Nutrisense's quiz, Stitch Space's free patterns via ManyChat), (c) giveaways used as partnership vehicles with bigger accounts rather than generic prize draws, and (d) product seeding to practitioners whose real usage becomes content (WHOOP trainers, Oura's regret). Referral mechanics and low-friction reservations still convert best to purchase, but can be framed as "founding member" or "$1 reserve" rather than "waitlist."

### Cited Findings
- Levels: education-first content "with zero sales CTAs," 500+ articles, 2M+ annual visitors; podcasts were the primary discovery channel per waitlist survey — [0 to Traction](https://0-to-traction.com/products/levels) **[brand-reported/secondary]**
- Sourhouse: digital cookbook lead magnet → 80,000+ email subs; "we even push many digital ads to the lead magnet instead of trying to make a direct sale"; giveaway with Bob's Red Mill/Chronicle Books gave a reason to collaborate and sourced UGC entries; influencer cohort converted ~13% of Kickstarter backers via Kickbooster affiliate program; questions/surveys in feed drive comments — [Buffer, Jul 2024](https://buffer.com/resources/built-following-before-product/) **[brand-reported]**
- Sleep-supplement pre-launch (product unavailable for 4 months): 60+ posts banked before publishing; 30-day "Sleep Challenge" hashtag; 25 micro-influencers (5K–30K) doing product-free sleep-education collabs; referral waitlist (share with 3 friends → early access + founding-member discount) drove 34% of 8,400 signups with viral coefficient 1.4; 52K followers in 6 months; 62% of first-week sales from waitlist members; 8.7% engagement rate; paid used only to boost top organic posts to lookalikes; free "Sleep Optimization Guide" converted TikTok followers to email — [Scalarly, Dec 2025](https://scalarly.com/case-studies/brand-launch-consumer.html) **[agency-claimed, UK/EU, budget EUR 30–80K]**
- Stitch Space (pre-launch marketplace): ManyChat comment-to-DM campaign offering 3 free sewing patterns for registration; ~1,000 IG + ~1,000 TikTok followers pre-launch; 1,000+ users at launch; IG grew to 4,688 followers / 336K views — [Contra case study](https://contra.com/p/3ZPbA7ke-how-stitch-space-generated-1000-launch-users) **[agency-claimed]**
- Nutrisense uses quiz-based landing pages (quiz start rate as a leading KPI) — [Taikun](https://taikundigital.com/case-studies/full-funnel-meta-rebuild-for-a-health-and-wellness-brand)
- Giveaways as qualification tools: Mwave (Australian PC retailer, 202 Gleam campaigns since 2016, 203,000 users) ties every giveaway to a launch moment; entry actions include follow on Instagram, newsletter signup, product-page clicks; one campaign's "vote for brand of the year" mechanic hit 32.81% conversion; 17.56% viral share conversion; creator-promoted GPU giveaway hit 41.15% conversion — [Gleam case study, May 2026](https://gleam.io/success/mwave-gleam-case-study) **[vendor-claimed]**
- Flexr: email leads were sent to a Facebook Group and asked to post why they wanted the product, creating visible social proof and a launch-time crowd — [MarketingStrategy.com](https://www.marketingstrategy.com/marketing-strategy-studies/kickstarter/)
- Bumpboxx: ~2,300 VIPs in WhatsApp groups; backers self-organized two Facebook groups (~5K people); "the community writes your best ads" (collector video with 3M+ views borrowed into paid; ASMR cuts; "how to back" education ads at 4.8–6.8 ROAS) — [TCF](https://www.tcf.team/case-studies/bb-777-full-case-study)
- Reservation ($1 deposit) leads are 30x more likely to buy than email-only leads (LaunchBoom) — [LaunchBoom](https://www.launchboom.com/crowdfunding-guides/how-to-launch-a-successful-kickstarter-campaign/); Seatmate's waitlist→backer 26% with that funnel — [Magnet](https://www.marketingbymagnet.com/case-studies/seatmate-chairs)
- WHOOP seeded trainers, then charged; engagement (24/7 wear) became the story — [Wave notes, HIBT Jul 2025](https://pod.wave.co/podcast/how-i-built-this-with-guy-raz/whoop-will-ahmed-a1f5cef3)
- Oura regret: "We should have given products to some influential people for testing... a little bit too late" — [Nordic Startup News](https://nordicstartupnews.com/4-lessons-from-a-650000-kickstarter-campaign/)
- Meta reports 71% of consumers say they purchase within a couple of days after seeing creator content across its apps — [CompleteAITraining summary of Meta Dec 2025 announcement](https://completeaitraining.com/news/meta-debuts-ai-and-a-new-api-to-help-brands-turn-creator/) **[Meta-claimed]**
- Instagram engagement custom audiences can be built specifically from people who *saved* a post or ad, and from people who *messaged* the account — [Meta Marketing API](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/marketing-api/audiences/guides/engagement-custom-audiences/) **[Meta-official]** — meaning saves and DMs are retargetable assets, not vanity metrics.

### Inferences
- For a no-subscription, screenless band, the natural "non-waitlist" offer set is: (1) a saveable education series (e.g., "what your resting HR/HRV actually means" carousels/Reels) optimized for saves and profile visits; (2) a quiz or downloadable guide (metabolic/recovery/sleep self-assessment) as the email capture, framed as value, not a queue; (3) comment-to-DM (ManyChat-style) delivery of that guide, which simultaneously builds the "messaged" custom audience; (4) a founder/practitioner "field notes" format from seeded prototypes worn by coaches/clinicians; (5) 2–3 creator-partnered giveaways tied to a moment (New Year, marathon season) with follow + email entry actions; (6) late in the runway (Feb–Apr 2027), a "founding member" $1 or refundable reserve for people already in the engager/email pool.
- Everything the founder wants to measure (follows, saves, DMs) is retargetable on Meta without a pixel, so the pre-launch offers should be chosen partly for the custom audiences they create.

### Gaps
- No controlled evidence isolating giveaways' effect on *later* purchase conversion for hardware; Mwave and Sourhouse are retailer/consumables examples.
- No published data on "Notify me" button/Meta reminder-ads performance for hardware pre-launch.

## Key Question 6: Creator/whitelisting (partnership ads) on Meta for wearables — how it works, typical costs, effect on CPM/CTR/CPA

### Takeaway
Partnership ads (formerly branded content ads) let the brand run ads from a creator's handle. Meta's own A/B data: ~19% lower cost per result (≈2,400-advertiser test, Jun 2025) and 13% higher CTR; agency data show 20–35% lower CPA and 1.5–3x CTR but with a ~19% higher CPM in one $130M dataset. Typical whitelisting fees are a 6–12-month usage window priced on top of the content fee; micro creators are cheapest. Oura uses this tactic.

### Cited Findings
- **[Meta-official]** "Add their content with partnership ads to help reduce cost per result by 19% on average. Source: Meta internal data. A/B test across ~2,400 advertisers comparing business-as-usual creative assets with partnership ad creative across all placements for link clicks and offsite conversions, Jun 14–21, 2025." — [Meta for Business post](https://www.facebook.com/metaforbusiness/posts/your-shoppers-already-trust-creators-add-their-content-with-partnership-ads-to-h/1586625773505122/)
- **[Meta-claimed]** Dec 2025 update: partnership ads deliver on average 19% lower CPA and 13% higher CTR; new creator-content hub, faster ad-code permissions, and an API — [CompleteAITraining summary](https://completeaitraining.com/news/meta-debuts-ai-and-a-new-api-to-help-brands-turn-creator/); partnership ads reached ~$10B annual revenue run-rate per Meta — [LinkedIn post, Aug 2026](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/sebastian-novin_meta-says-adding-partnership-ads-to-regular-activity-7495035672542748672-k9Qj) **[secondary]**
- **[Meta-claimed via agencies]** Combined BAU + partnership ads: 53% higher CTR, 19% lower CPA, +296% engagement (fashion client), +35% ROAS in creator segments — [BMG360, Aug 2025](https://www.bmg360.com/blog/post/meta-partnership-ads-vs-traditional-creative); [Billo, Nov 2025](https://billo.app/blog/creator-partnership-ads/)
- **[agency dataset]** Agentio analysis of $130M spend / 65,000 ads / 137 brands: partnership ads vs. licensed UGC from brand handles: +19% CTR, +10% CVR, −5% CPA, but +19% CPM; Explore placement −57% CPM; Stories was the exception (+25% CPA) — [Netinfluencer, Aug 2026](https://www.netinfluencer.com/meta-partnership-ads-outperform-licensed-ugc-across-130m-usd-in-ad-spend-report-finds/)
- **[agency benchmark]** AdRiseLab 90-account benchmark: whitelisted ads 20–35% lower CPA than brand-page UGC, largest in beauty/wellness/lifestyle; thumb-stop-rate delta 18–28%; standard usage window 6 months (12 for higher fee); creators grant "Branded Content"/"Advertise" permissions via Business Suite; brand selects creator handle as publisher; "Paid partnership" label appears automatically; engagement lands on the creator's post; the article flags its unlinked numbers as "directional estimates" — [AdRiseLab, May 2026](https://adriselab.com/blog/creator-whitelisting-meta-ads-2026)
- Other agency claims: 30–50% lower CPM and 1.5–3x CTR — [Influencer-Hero](https://www.influencer-hero.com/resources/guide-to-whitelisting-meta-partnership-ads); up to 40% cheaper CPM and 30% lower CPA — [iQfluence](https://iqfluence.io/public/blog/whitelisting-social-media) **[agency-claimed, unsourced]**
- **[Meta-official]** Supported objectives/conversion locations for partnership ads include Traffic → "Instagram or Facebook profile" (Yes), Engagement → video views/post engagement (Yes), Leads → website or instant forms (Yes); Instagram Stories unsupported with Advantage+ catalog ads — [Instagram Help Center: Supported objectives and placements for partnership ads](https://help.instagram.com/1173889323625775/)
- Oura runs whitelisted ads through influencer handles as a core paid-social tactic — [The Digital Chapter](https://www.thedigitalchapter.com/p/oura-ring-growthmarketing-casestudy)
- Pulsenmore's follower-growth program was built on local creator collaborations with UGC video as the "evergreen" asset — [House of Marketers](https://houseofmarketers.com/pulsenmore-paid-social-awareness-case-study/)
- Levels invested in making co-founder Casey Means a creator with ~1M followers via podcasts as a growth strategy — [Founding Journey, Oct 2024](https://foundingjourney.com/p/sam-corcos)

### Inferences
- Partnership ads are directly usable for Vybe's stated goal because the Traffic → Instagram profile destination is a supported conversion location for partnership ads: a creator's Reel about the band can be run as a profile-visit ad that sends people to Vybe's profile.
- Expect higher CPM but higher CTR/lower cost per action; for a follower-growth goal, the CTR/thumb-stop gains matter more than CPM.
- With sub-$5K/month, prioritize 3–5 micro/nano creators (practitioners: coaches, PTs, RDs) with 6-month Meta-only usage rights over any macro creator; the Andromeda "creative diversity" requirement is also satisfied cheaply by multiple creator faces.

### Gaps
- No published whitelisting fee schedule from a neutral source for 2025–2026; AdRiseLab's tier pricing was truncated in the accessible text and is agency-estimated. No wearable-specific partnership-ad case with numbers.

## Key Question 7: Retargeting windows and sequencing over a 6–9 month runway; measurement without purchase events

### Takeaway
Meta's retention ceilings (IG/Page engagement up to 730 days per API, 365 in UI; video 365; lead forms 90) comfortably cover an 8-month runway, so the whole pre-launch engager pool can be retargeted at launch. Sequence by temperature (video viewers → profile visitors/savers/messagers → leads → reservations), exclude prior converters aggressively to keep new-visitor share high, and measure with cost per ThruPlay, cost per profile visit, profile-visit→follow rate, cost per follow (new IG follows metric), save rate, CPL, lead→reservation rate, and frequency/new-reach — plus periodic holdout or geo tests if budget allows.

### Cited Findings
- **[Meta-official]** Max retention: Instagram business profile 730 days; Page 730 days; lead-gen ads 90 days; shopping 365 days — [Meta Marketing API](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/marketing-api/audiences/guides/engagement-custom-audiences/); UI commonly exposes up to 365 days for video/IG/Page and 90 for lead forms — [AdPlay.ai](https://adplay.ai/blog/facebook-engagement-custom-audience)
- **[Meta-official]** Instagram follows metric available across all objectives from July 7, 2025, enabling profile-visit→follow conversion rate and cost per follow at ad level — [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/meta-introduces-instagram-follows-metric-for-ads-reporting/)
- Video retargeting tiers: 25% = broad, 75% = intent inflection, 95% = hyper-targeted; longer windows suit consideration purchases — [AdPlay.ai](https://adplay.ai/blog/facebook-engagement-custom-audience); [Agorapulse](https://www.agorapulse.com/blog/facebook-advertising/video-view-retargeting/)
- Nutrisense's key measurement shift: new-visitor % and lead-to-order rate replaced CPL as primary KPIs; strict exclusions (visitors, leads, content engagers) fixed a funnel that was "circling its warm audience"; CPM down, reach up, frequency down — [Taikun](https://taikundigital.com/case-studies/full-funnel-meta-rebuild-for-a-health-and-wellness-brand) **[agency-claimed]**
- Hook-rate benchmark: 3-second view rate >25% as a creative health check; "boost post" lacks conversion tooling — [GetKoro, Jan 2026](https://getkoro.app/blog/instagram-ads-on-a-budget) **[practitioner]**
- Instagram benchmarks: profile-visit rate typically 1–5% of impressions; bio-link CTR 0.5–3% of profile visits — [Gainsty, May 2025](https://www.gainsty.com/blog/instagram-performance-metrics) **[aggregator, unsourced]**
- Reels traffic quality caveat: one advertiser saw landing-page-view rate of 58% on Facebook Feed vs 20% on Instagram Reels — [Reddit thread snapshot, Jun 2026](https://reddit.sentinel-team.org/posts/1twfw1k/snapshots/2026-06-04T11%3A47%3A11.320221Z) **[anecdote]**
- Awareness-objective baselines (B2B-heavy but indicative of reach pricing): Meta CPM $4.19, CTR 0.20% Q1 2026 (Refine Labs) — [PaidSignal](https://www.paidsignal.com/b2b-paid-awareness-benchmarks-q1-2026-analysis/)
- Incrementality for a brand launching Meta with no history: Lumens (lighting retailer) used geo-lift tests via Measured, shifted 63% of spend to awareness/view-content, measured 4–6% business lift — [Block & Tam](https://www.blockandtam.com/lumens) **[agency-claimed; large budget]**
- Pre-launch retargeting sequencing that worked: Flexr's email leads → Facebook Group → repeated "be online at 11am, 500 units" message → $32,700 in 24h — [MarketingStrategy.com](https://www.marketingstrategy.com/marketing-strategy-studies/kickstarter/); Hammerleds: leads nurtured with 3 videos over months → 25% lead-to-order — [BusyMonday](https://busymonday.io/case-study-hammerleds-preorder-launch-campaugn/); Bumpboxx: VIP launch-day emails opened >70% with one-third to one-half clicking — [TCF](https://www.tcf.team/case-studies/bb-777-full-case-study)
- Crowdfunding pre-launch timing conventions: LaunchBoom prescribes a reservation funnel + email + Meta ads before launch and 15–25% of goal on marketing; Flexr started only 14 days out and wished for more — [LaunchBoom](https://www.launchboom.com/crowdfunding-guides/how-to-launch-a-successful-kickstarter-campaign/); [MarketingStrategy.com](https://www.marketingstrategy.com/marketing-strategy-studies/kickstarter/)
- Meta attribution: Nutrisense account moved to 7-day click + 1-day engaged view, removing 1-day view — [Taikun](https://taikundigital.com/case-studies/full-funnel-meta-rebuild-for-a-health-and-wellness-brand)

### Inferences (proposed runway, Oct 2026 → May 2027)
- Months 1–2 (Oct–Nov 2026): seed. One campaign, video views/ThruPlay + profile visits, broad US; goal = 20K+ 3s viewers, 2K+ 75% viewers, 500+ profile visitors, 100+ savers/messagers, 200–500 email leads via guide/quiz. Metrics: cost per ThruPlay, hook rate >25%, cost per profile visit, follow rate, cost per follow, CPL.
- Months 3–5 (Dec–Feb): build. Add 1% lookalikes from 75%-viewers and leads as Advantage+ suggestions; add partnership ads from 3–5 seeded practitioners; start a small retargeting campaign to 365-day engagers with deeper education + quiz; exclude leads from prospecting to protect new-visitor share. Metrics: new-visitor %, frequency <2/week on retargeting, lead→quiz completion, email engagement.
- Months 6–7 (Mar–Apr 2027): pre-sell. Introduce founding-member/refundable reserve to the warm pool only; run "how to order / what you get" education retargeting; refresh creative to launch-proof (real users, accuracy data). Metrics: lead→reservation rate (benchmark 25–30% for $1-deposit funnels), cost per reservation (<$10 LaunchBoom target).
- Month 8 (May 2027): launch. Retarget the entire 365-day engager pool + customer lists; keep 90-day lead-form audiences refreshed via customer-list uploads.
- Measurement without purchases: treat the "Instagram follows" metric plus saves and DMs as primary, CPL as secondary, and build a simple holdout (e.g., exclude one state from ads for two weeks and compare organic follow/lead velocity) rather than trusting last-click.

### Gaps
- No published 6–9 month pre-launch retargeting schedule from a wearable brand; the sequencing above is synthesized from crowdfunding and DTC health cases.
- No neutral benchmark for cost per 1,000 accounts reached among health-tech segments specifically; only general Meta CPMs ($4–$15 depending on objective and source).
- Meta's own Business Help Center pages on learning phase, custom audience retention (UI), and partnership-ads setup could not be fetched directly due to proxy blocks; developer docs and Instagram Help Center were accessible and are cited instead.
