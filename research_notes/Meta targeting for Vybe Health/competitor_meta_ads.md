## Key Question 1: What ads are WHOOP, Oura, Amazfit, Polar, Garmin, Ultrahuman, Hume and Luna running (formats, hooks, offers, CTAs, landing pages, longevity)?

### Takeaway
WHOOP and Oura run very large, mostly static, direct-offer libraries (WHOOP ~210–282 active creatives, Oura ~67 active) with short creative lifecycles (WHOOP's longest-running active ad ~92 days, Oura's ~20 days), while Garmin runs the biggest budget with evergreen ads lasting 300–440 days. Amazfit's Meta presence is almost entirely smartwatch/HYROX creative; its Helio Strap barely appears in paid social, and Polar, Ultrahuman, Hume and Luna have no measurable tracked Meta ad footprint in the tools used.

### Inferences
- WHOOP's Meta engine is a high-volume static "offer treadmill" (75% direct-offer hooks, ~97% static images, 33–92-day lifecycles) rather than a creative-storytelling machine; its differentiated messaging (women's hormonal insights, longevity) exists but has been cycled off.
- Oura's paid social is even more product-centric (88% studio-product) but layers creator whitelisting (#OuraPartner) heavily in DACH, and is the only incumbent running explicit women's-health/fertility creative on Meta.
- Amazfit is not using Meta to sell the Helio Strap; its paid social sells watches to HYROX/endurance athletes. Combined with Zepp's admission that it under-supplied the Strap, the $99 "no-subscription band" message is essentially unadvertised on Meta in the US.
- Polar, Hume and Luna's Meta footprints are too small to register in ad-intelligence tools, i.e., the "no subscription" position is winning earned media and Reddit word-of-mouth, not paid Meta share of voice.

- Could not open GoMarble, adlibrary.com or the Meta Ad Library directly to confirm US-only counts, landing-page URLs, or exact CTA distributions for Oura/Amazfit/Garmin; WHOOP's "93% SHOP_NOW" is a snippet-level claim.

## Key Question 2: Which audiences do these brands visibly speak to in creative, and who is nobody speaking to?

### Takeaway
Creative splits cleanly: WHOOP = performance/competence and "train smarter" (now broadening to habits and longevity); Oura = women, Gen Z/millennial self-expression, stress/cycle and longevity; Garmin = spec-driven athletes plus parents (kids' watches); Amazfit = HYROX/trail athletes. Nobody in the tracked set is running Meta creative aimed at the explicit "no subscription / own your data" value shopper, at men 45+, at shift workers/first responders, or at price-sensitive beginners.

### Inferences
- The two incumbents have effectively partitioned the market by gender and mindset: WHOOP owns competitive men 25–44; Oura owns women 20–40 plus a longevity flank. Every challenger (Amazfit, Polar, Hume, Luna) is defined by "WHOOP without the subscription" in press, but none is putting that message in front of Meta audiences at scale.
- Under-served in paid social creative: (1) the explicit "no subscription, no screen" value buyer (Reddit shows demand, see KQ5, but no brand is buying it on Meta); (2) men 45+ and pre-retirees interested in recovery/longevity (Oura's "spry seniors" is the only nod, and it is TV/OOH-led); (3) beginners/returners intimidated by "Strain" and HYROX language; (4) non-athlete high-stress workers (nurses, shift workers, parents) where "sleep + recovery without another screen" is a natural fit; (5) privacy/data-ownership buyers, which Polar claims in press but does not amplify on Meta.

## Key Question 3: What do EU ad-transparency disclosures reveal about WHOOP's and Oura's chosen age ranges and gender targeting?

### Takeaway
The Meta Ad Library does expose, for every ad delivered in the EU, the advertiser-selected age range, gender and location plus reach broken down by age/gender/country, but this session could not open the Ad Library or scraper outputs, so no WHOOP- or Oura-specific EU targeting parameters were retrieved. Indirect evidence (German-language WHOOP and Oura ads; Oura's Swedish Natural Cycles fertility ad) confirms both brands run EU-localised sets that would carry these disclosures.

### Cited Findings
