# Organic Instagram and Facebook follower growth for a new small brand account (2025-2026)

Context: Vybe Health (@vybehealthinc). As of 2026-09-28 it has 17 IG followers, 3 photo posts, 0 Reels. Profile visits are 35-46/day, driven by the founder following and commenting, but posts reach only 6-11 accounts. Allowed levers: posting, following, commenting. No DMs, no ads.

Source-quality note: Meta's own pages (transparency.meta.com, about.instagram.com) could not be fetched directly because the network proxy blocked them. Official Meta statements below come from about.fb.com and creators.instagram.com pages found through search, or from Social Media Today, TechCrunch or The Verge reporting. Many 2026 "algorithm guide" blogs repeat numbers that no primary source backs up, such as "sends are worth 3-5x likes". Those are flagged as unverified wherever they appear.

## 1. What Instagram/Meta has said (2025-2026) about ranking and which signals matter

### Takeaway
Mosseri has repeatedly named three top signals: watch time, likes per reach and sends per reach. Sends matter more for reach to non-followers, and likes matter more for reach to followers. Instagram's recommendation system tests each post on a small pool of non-followers first, including posts from small accounts, and widens distribution when that pool responds well. That makes original, shareable Reels the main way for a tiny account to reach non-followers. Its only other route is to followers, and a 17-follower account barely has any.

### Cited Findings
- **Jan 2025, Mosseri:** "The top three signals that matter most for ranking are watch time, likes and sends. So when looking at your insights, pay close attention to average watch time, likes per reach, and sends per reach." He added: "Likes are slightly more important for connected content, and sends are slightly more important for unconnected content." — [Social Media Today, 22 Jan 2025](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-algorithm-insights-2025/738034/)
- **Older (2024), Mosseri:** "sends per reach correlate more, in my experience, with overall reach than anything else"; "the rate is more important than the count." — [Social Media Today, May 2024](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-chief-post-share-rates-key-driver-reach/716540/); also [SMT, June 2024 (Colin & Samir interview)](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-chief-shares-insights-into-its-algorithms-creator-monetization-a/718540/)
- **Mosseri on video length (secondary report):** watch time is counted in seconds watched, not percentage, so 10 seconds watched on a 1-minute video counts about the same as 10 seconds on a 10-second video. — [Marketing Malone (summarizing Mosseri's 2025 video series)](https://marketingmalone.com/blog/instagram-algorithm/)
- **Sept 2026, Mosseri's algorithm video series** (SMT via Yahoo, 24 Sep 2026). He named four principles: (1) **Originality**, meaning precedence for original content and original creators over re-posters; (2) **Recency**, since "when something is posted matters a lot" and the feed is "roughly in chronological order"; (3) **Breaking content**, where Instagram shows posts from *small creators who post publicly to accounts that don't follow them* to gauge response, "with a view to boosting posts from new creators"; (4) **Model size**. He also said creators should watch engagement *rates*, not total views or likes, recommended experimenting with **trial Reels**, and said creators should "post as much as they can" to raise their chances of a hit while avoiding burnout. Users can shape their feeds through Favorites, "Not interested" and **Your Algorithm** (add or remove topics). — [Yahoo Tech / Social Media Today, 24 Sep 2026](https://tech.yahoo.com/social-media/articles/instagram-chief-shares-algorithm-insights-200528354.html)
- **Staged testing for recommendations (announced Apr 2024, older):** Instagram "will show eligible content to a small audience… the top performing set of Reels will be shown to a slightly wider audience, then the best of these… to an even wider group," to "give all creators an equal chance." Previously, recommended Reels were "ranked primarily based on how an account's followers engaged with them," which favored the largest accounts. — [TechCrunch, 30 Apr 2024](https://techcrunch.com/2024/04/30/instagram-is-updating-its-ranking-systems-to-surface-more-content-from-smaller-original-creators/); [The Verge, 30 Apr 2024](https://www.theverge.com/2024/4/30/24144571/instagram-algorithm-ranking-recommendations-reposted-content)
- **Originality and aggregator rules (Apr 2024, older, still cited as current):** accounts that repost content they did not "create or enhance in a material way" more than 10 times in 30 days are removed from recommendations. They become eligible again 30 days after their last unoriginal repost. When duplicates exist, the original replaces reposts in recommendations. — [The Verge, 30 Apr 2024](https://www.theverge.com/2024/4/30/24144571/instagram-algorithm-ranking-recommendations-reposted-content)
- **Nov 2024 (older):** Mosseri "rebalanc[ed] ranking to prioritize content from people you follow… you should see unconnected reach go down and connected reach go up." — [Mosseri on Threads, 21 Nov 2024](https://www.threads.com/@mosseri/post/DCo4WiOvCB0)
- **What the Reels ranking predicts (secondary summary of Instagram's official ranking explainer):** whether a viewer will reshare the Reel, watch it to completion, like it, or visit the audio page. Quality filters reduce distribution for low resolution, watermarks from other apps, borders, muted (no-audio) videos, excessive text overlay and reposted content. — [Hanami Social, Apr 2026](https://hanamisocial.com/en/blog/instagram-reels-algorithm-2026/). I could not fetch the primary Meta Transparency page.
- **Video length (secondary):** Mosseri reportedly said in Jan 2025 that videos over 3 minutes will not be recommended to non-followers. — [Hanami Social](https://hanamisocial.com/en/blog/instagram-reels-algorithm-2026/). Instagram raised the Reel length limit to 3 minutes in Jan 2025. — [Instagram for Creators, 21 Jan 2025](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/the-latest-with-instagram)
- **Trial Reels (official):** Trial Reels are "shown to non-followers first." Followers do not see them in Feed or the Reels tab, and they stay off the profile grid unless the creator shares them to everyone. The creator can set them to auto-share with followers "if we determine it's performing well based on the views it receives" within 72 hours. — [Meta Newsroom, 10 Dec 2024 (updated 26 Jun 2025)](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/12/trial-reels-try-content-non-followers-first-see-what-perfoms-best/)
- **Trial Reels results (official, June 2025):** "after trying trial reels, 40% of creators started posting reels more often and of those who did, 80% saw an increase in reels reach from non-followers." Meta also said it had "made trial reels available to everyone." — [Meta Newsroom, 12 Jun 2025](https://about.fb.com/news/2025/06/inspiring-creativity-that-brings-people-together/)
  - **Conflicting report on eligibility:** a July 2025 report says Mosseri announced on Threads that Trial Reels were available to "all public accounts with at least 1,000 followers," with smaller accounts possibly added later. — [The Keyword, 17 Jul 2025](https://www.thekeyword.co/news/instagram-expands-trial-reels-to-all-public-creators-with-1k-or-more-followers). **Vybe (17 followers) should check in-app whether the Trial toggle appears.**
  - Trial Reels became schedulable via API in Dec 2025. — [Meta for Developers, Dec 2025](https://bit.ly/4aYZOMD). The claim that they became schedulable in-app in Feb 2026 appears only in secondary blogs, such as [Dataslayer](https://www.dataslayer.ai/blog/instagram-algorithm-2025-complete-guide-for-marketers). Unverified.
- **Edits app (official):** launched 22 Apr 2025. It is a free video editor with up to 10-minute camera capture, a frame-accurate timeline, watermark-free export, direct sharing to IG/FB, and "performance insights." — [Meta Newsroom, 22 Apr 2025](https://about.fb.com/news/2025/04/introducing-edits-streamlined-video-creation-app/); [Instagram for Creators](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/edits-video-creation-app)
- **"Your Algorithm":** a user-facing topic dashboard. Mosseri referenced it in Sept 2026 as a way for users to add or remove topics. — [Yahoo/SMT, Sep 2026](https://tech.yahoo.com/social-media/articles/instagram-chief-shares-algorithm-insights-200528354.html). The launch date (Dec 2025) and global rollout (early 2026) come only from secondary sources such as [Dataslayer](https://www.dataslayer.ai/blog/instagram-algorithm-2025-complete-guide-for-marketers).
- **Mosseri's Dec 31, 2025 year-end memo** is widely reported as prioritizing "raw, real human content" over polished or AI content in 2026. — secondary: [Dataslayer](https://www.dataslayer.ai/blog/instagram-algorithm-2025-complete-guide-for-marketers), [Hanami Social](https://hanamisocial.com/en/blog/instagram-reels-algorithm-2026/). I did not see the memo text itself.
- **Profile grid:** Instagram released grid reordering on 8 June 2026. Accounts can pin up to 3 posts, or 6 with Instagram Plus. — [CreatorFlow, Sep 2026, citing SMT/Instagram](https://creatorflow.so/blog/instagram-profile-optimization/)

**Unverified claims seen repeatedly in 2026 blogs. Do not rely on these:**
- "Sends carry 3-5x the weight of likes" ([PostEverywhere](https://posteverywhere.ai/blog/how-the-instagram-algorithm-works), [Dataslayer](https://www.dataslayer.ai/blog/instagram-algorithm-2025-complete-guide-for-marketers)). These cite the May 2024 SMT article, which contains no such multiplier.
- "Two-way conversation became a ranked signal in March 2026," "Early Access Reels," and "original content gets 40-60% more distribution." All from [Dataslayer](https://www.dataslayer.ai/blog/instagram-algorithm-2025-complete-guide-for-marketers), with no primary source.
- "Instagram down-weighted likes in April 2026" and an "April 2026 livestream" naming interest, recency, relationship and frequency. From [LatestDevelopments](https://latestdevelopments.com/instagram-algorithm-2026-the-3-ranking-factors-confirmed-by-adam-mosseri/), with no primary source.

### Inferences
- For a 17-follower account, reach to followers is capped at roughly the follower count. Almost all possible growth has to come from recommendations to non-followers. Instagram's staged testing and the "breaking content" principle both say small public accounts get a test audience, but only for eligible, original content. Of Vybe's formats, Reels are the ones built for that route (see section 2).
- Vybe's 3 photo posts reaching 6-11 accounts is consistent with static images getting almost no push to non-followers.
- The KPIs Instagram itself recommends are rates: sends/reach, likes/reach and average watch time.

### Gaps
- I could not read Meta Transparency Center's "Instagram Reels" and "Facebook Feed" system cards directly, because the proxy blocked them.
- Meta has published no numeric weights for any signal.

## 2. Benchmarks: format, posting frequency, growth rates for small accounts

### Takeaway
The large 2026 datasets disagree on which format wins reach. Socialinsider's Aug 2026 data has carousels slightly ahead of Reels. Metricool's 2026 study says Reels reach 29% more accounts than carousels and 212% more than single images. They agree that single images are the weakest format and that small accounts grow fastest in percentage terms. Posting 3-5 times a week or more is associated with more than double the follower growth of posting 1-2 times a week.

### Cited Findings
**Engagement and reach by format**
- Socialinsider (35M posts): 2025 engagement rates were Reels 0.52%, carousels 0.55%, images 0.37%. Images fell 17% YoY and overall engagement about 24% YoY. In Q2 2026: Reels 0.48%, carousels 0.50%, images 0.33%, overall 0.45%. — [Socialinsider, Feb 2026 (updated with Q2 2026)](https://www.socialinsider.io/social-media-benchmarks/instagram)
- Socialinsider, average views per post for **1-5K follower brands**: Reels 580, carousels 993, images 417. Average comments: Reels 3, carousels 2, images 1. Average saves: 1 each. — [Socialinsider](https://www.socialinsider.io/social-media-benchmarks/instagram)
- Socialinsider reach rate, Aug 2026: IG overall 3.20%. By format: carousels 4.50%, Reels 4.10%, images 4.00%. By size: **1-5K = 6.65%**, 5-10K = 5.75%, 100K-1M = 3.50%. — [Socialinsider, 3 Sep 2026](https://www.socialinsider.io/blog/social-media-reach/)
- Socialinsider also says Reels 30-60 seconds long have the highest reach rate (5.60%) and that "Reels are the top-performing format for reach, but only up to 50K followers." This comes from a search snippet of [Socialinsider Reels statistics](https://www.socialinsider.io/blog/instagram-reels-statistics/), which I did not open in full. It **conflicts** with the carousel-led reach figure above.
- Metricool 2026 (24.36M posts, 375,118 accounts, Jan-Feb 2025 vs 2026): Reels generate over 4x the interactions of single-image posts (1,035 vs 238 average). Reels are the only format whose engagement rate rose (+24.76%). Carousels get 9x more saves than single images and more than double their reach and views. Average watch time per Reel more than doubled to 8.5 seconds. Reel shares rose 67%. — [Metricool press release, 16 Jun 2026](https://metricool.com/press-release-instagram-study-2026/); [NetInfluencer](https://www.netinfluencer.com/instagram-views-up-27-percent-in-early-2026-as-publishing-increases-report-finds/); [Metricool trends](https://metricool.com/instagram-trends/)
- Metricool: "Reels reach 29% more people than carousels and 212% more than single-image posts"; carousels get the most views per post (27K+ on average, 20% more than Reels). — [Metricool newsletter, 21 Jun 2026](https://newsletter.metricool.com/p/instagram-tell-me-your-goal-and-i-ll-tell-you-what-can-help)
- Metricool, timing: 65% of a Reel's views and 61% of its interactions come in the first 3 days, and day 1 alone brings about 30% of a Reel's lifetime views. — [Metricool trends](https://metricool.com/instagram-trends/)
- Metricool, **mentions or tags**: tagging another account gave **Tiny accounts (<2K) +108% reach and +167% comments**, and Small accounts (2-10K) +37% reach. — [Metricool trends](https://metricool.com/instagram-trends/)
- Metricool, hashtags: posts with at least one hashtag had 32% fewer views and 34% fewer interactions. Metricool describes this as correlational. Posts that ask a question drew 37% more comments. — [eMarketer, 30 Jun 2026](https://www.emarketer.com/content/carousels-questions-drive-instagram-engagement)

**Posting frequency**
- Buffer (2.1M posts, about 102K accounts, fixed-effects model): versus weeks with no posts, follower growth rose **+0.12 percentage points at 1-2 posts/week, +0.26 at 3-5, +0.44 at 6-9, +0.66 at 10+**. 3-5 posts/week "more than doubles" growth compared with 1-2/week. Reach per post rises about 12% at 3-5, 18% at 6-9 and 24% at 10+, relative to baseline. Weeks with no posts fell below the account's baseline growth. — [Buffer, 13 Aug 2025](https://buffer.com/resources/how-often-to-post-on-instagram/); [Buffer data blog (methodology)](https://symphonious-shortbread-cd2770.netlify.app/posts/instagram-posting-frequency/index.html); [SMT coverage](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/study-shows-posting-more-instagram-leads-to-more-reach/757633/)
- Metricool 2026: for Small accounts (2-10K), interactions peak at **7-14 posts/week**. Huge accounts (>1M) posting less than once a week average nearly 6x the interactions per post of those posting daily. — [Metricool trends](https://metricool.com/instagram-trends/)
- Socialinsider: brands' average monthly output in 2025 was Reels 8, carousels 5, images 7. Reel posting rose 33% YoY. — [Socialinsider](https://www.socialinsider.io/social-media-benchmarks/instagram)

**Follower growth rates**
- Socialinsider average IG audience growth rate, 2025, **annual**: **1-5K followers 22.00%** (38.00% in 2024), 5-10K 20.29%, 10-50K 17.20%, 100K-1M 11.25%. — [Socialinsider](https://www.socialinsider.io/social-media-benchmarks/instagram)
- Metricool 2026: **only 21% of accounts under 10K followers grew** in the study window. Among Small accounts (2-10K), 11% moved up a follower tier, as did 10% of Tiny accounts (<2K), versus 3% of Medium accounts. — [Metricool press release](https://metricool.com/press-release-instagram-study-2026/); [NetInfluencer](https://www.netinfluencer.com/instagram-views-up-27-percent-in-early-2026-as-publishing-increases-report-finds/)
- Databox benchmark (business IG accounts, monthly medians): reach 15,367, profile visits 266, website clicks 8, followers 3,272, **new followers 11.5/month**. Bottom quartile: 740 followers, 2 new followers/month. Top quartile: 154 new followers/month. — [PanelFollows citing Databox, Aug 2026](https://panelfollows.com/en/blog/instagram-business-account-conversion)
- I found no benchmark specific to accounts under 1K followers. Socialinsider's smallest tier is 1-5K, and Metricool's "Tiny" tier is under 2K.

### Inferences
- A 22% annual growth rate on a small base means very few followers in absolute terms. At that benchmark rate a 17-follower account adds only a handful per year, so a "fast" goal needs well-above-average tactics. This is simple arithmetic on the Socialinsider rate, not a forecast.
- The median business account gaining 11.5 followers a month (Databox) shows how low the organic baseline is.
- The format sources conflict on whether Reels or carousels lead reach. The consistent finding is that images trail both and that Reels see the most growth in shares and watch time. A reasonable mix is Reels for reach to non-followers, carousels for saves and views, and few single images.

### Gaps
- There is no public benchmark for accounts with 0-1K followers.
- The Socialinsider and Metricool reach definitions differ (per follower vs per post average), so their numbers are not directly comparable.

## 3. Follow/comment outreach: does it work, safe limits, what gets accounts restricted

### Takeaway
Meta publishes no daily or hourly limit on follows or comments. The only published limit is a lifetime cap of 7,500 accounts followed. Restrictions are dynamic and pattern-based: bursts, machine-regular timing, copy-paste comments, follow/unfollow cycling, automation apps, and high activity on new accounts. Every "safe limit" table online is a community estimate. I found no rigorous 2025-2026 study measuring whether outbound follow/comment activity converts to followers. Vybe's own data (profile visits jumping to 35-46/day) shows that it drives visits.

### Cited Findings
- Instagram's only published follow limit is 7,500 accounts followed in total. There is no official daily or hourly cap, and figures like "150/day" or "60/hour" are community estimates. — [SocialRails (updated Jul 2026)](https://socialrails.com/blog/instagram-follow-limits-explained); [ChatPlace, Aug 2026](https://blog.chatplace.io/en/instagram-limits/); [TechPrevue, Jul 2026](https://www.techprevue.com/action-blocked-on-instagram/)
- Instagram does confirm that the same comment (including the same emoji) cannot be posted repeatedly. — [TechPrevue](https://www.techprevue.com/action-blocked-on-instagram/)
- Reported triggers: bursts of identical actions, perfectly regular timing, a sudden spike after inactivity, aggressive follow-then-unfollow cycling, third-party automation tools, and new devices, locations or VPNs. New accounts get the least tolerance. — [SocialRails](https://socialrails.com/blog/instagram-follow-limits-explained); [GetLikes, Sep 2026](https://getlikes.com/guides/instagram-action-blocked-try-again-later/); [ChatPlace](https://blog.chatplace.io/en/instagram-limits/)
- Community "safe" ranges by account age, **not official**. Under 1 month: 20-50 follows/day, 5-10/hour. 1-3 months: 50-100/day. 6+ months: 150-200/day. — [Gramlens, Jun 2026](https://gramlens.com/blog/instagram-follow-limits); a similar table is in [ShadowPhone, Jan 2026](https://www.shadowphone.io/blog/instagram-rate-limits-2026)
- Community comment ranges, **not official**. New accounts: about 15-30 comments/day. Established: 50-80. Recommend comments of 4 or more words, never identical. — [ShadowPhone](https://www.shadowphone.io/blog/instagram-rate-limits-2026)
- Third-party tests say around 150-200 follows or unfollows a day, or a burst within minutes, triggers a temporary block. Reported block lengths: a first block lasts a few hours to 24-48 hours, and repeats last longer, sometimes up to a week. These are user reports, not rules. — [GetLikes](https://getlikes.com/guides/instagram-action-blocked-try-again-later/); [SocialRails](https://socialrails.com/blog/instagram-follow-limits-explained)
- People who follow back through follow-for-follow tend to unfollow again later (churn). — [GetLikes](https://getlikes.com/guides/instagram-action-blocked-try-again-later/)
- Related platform guidance: Mosseri has long described "your history of interacting with someone," for example commenting on each other's posts, as a feed-ranking signal. — [The Verge, 2021 (older)](https://www.theverge.com/2021/6/8/22524332/instagram-how-it-works-blog-post-adam-mosseri-shadowban)

### Inferences
- Vybe's data (profile visits up while posts reach only 6-11 accounts, and only 17 followers) suggests outreach is sending visitors, but the profile is not converting them (see section 5). There is also not yet enough content to reward a visit. Outreach works as a way to get visits. The follow decision depends on the profile and the posts.
- The conservative approach for an account this new is varied, human-paced activity at or below the low end of the community ranges. Each comment should be unique and substantive, with no unfollow sweeps.

### Gaps
- There is no official Meta number for any action limit.
- I found no controlled study of follow- or comment-outreach conversion rates in 2025-2026.

## 4. Facebook Page growth (2025-2026)

### Takeaway
Facebook's feed is now heavily recommendation-driven: 41% of US Feed views come from unconnected accounts. Meta is pushing original Reels and demoting unoriginal content. Organic reach per post on Pages is low (1.25% of followers on average), but small Pages reach a larger share of their followers (4.35% for 1-5K). Page follower growth rates doubled in 2025. The median post from a Page with under 1K followers reaches only about 21 people.

### Cited Findings
- **Meta, Mar 2026 (official):** "views and time spent watching original Reels on Facebook approximately doubled in the second half of 2025" YoY. Content "filmed or produced directly by a creator or owner of a Profile or Page is considered original." Re-uploads, and "low-value changes" such as borders, captions or speed changes, are unoriginal and deprioritized. Repeat offenders may be made "non-recommendable." — [Meta Newsroom, 13 Mar 2026](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/03/rewarding-original-creators-on-facebook/); [TechCrunch, 13 Mar 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/13/facebook-makes-it-easier-for-creators-to-report-impersonators/)
- Facebook announced a crackdown on unoriginal content in July 2025. Repeat reposting leads to reduced reach and then demonetization. — [Off The Wall Media, Jul 2025](https://www.offthewallmedia.ie/post/meta-s-new-crackdown-why-facebook-is-turning-down-the-copycat-volume); see also [TechCrunch 2026 recap](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/13/facebook-makes-it-easier-for-creators-to-report-impersonators/)
- Meta's Q4 2025 Widely Viewed Content Report, as reported secondhand. 41% of US Feed views came from unconnected accounts and 46.8% from friends, Groups and followed Pages. 98.5% of Feed views were on posts with no external link. Facebook surfaced over 25% more same-day Reels in Q4 2025 than in Q3. In June 2025 all video was routed into the Reels surface. — [Meikuio, Jun 2026 (secondary summary of Meta reports)](https://meikuio.com/facebook-algorithm-2026/)
- Meta Creator Fast Track (Mar 2026) pays established creators up to $3,000/month for 15 or more Facebook Reels across 10 or more days in each 30-day period. Reposts of videos from IG, TikTok or YouTube count toward the total. Meta also boosts participants' reach. Aimed at established creators, not new brands. — [Tubefilter, 18 Mar 2026](https://www.tubefilter.com/2026/03/18/meta-creator-fast-track-payouts/)
- **Benchmarks.** Socialinsider (25M posts, 130K+ Pages): average engagement rate 0.15%. By format in 2025: status 0.20%, album 0.18%, Reel 0.18%, image 0.15%, link 0.05%. In Q2 2026 Reels led at 0.13%. **For Pages under 10K, albums perform best.** Page follower growth in 2025: **1-5K = 22.73%**, 5-10K = 21.40%, 10-50K = 38.20%, with an overall 23.20%, up from 12.20% in 2024. Average posting: 39 posts/month, down 22% YoY. — [Socialinsider Facebook benchmarks, Mar 2026](https://www.socialinsider.io/social-media-benchmarks/facebook)
- Socialinsider reach, Aug 2026: FB overall 1.25%. By format: images 1.20%, albums 1.15%, status 0.80%, **Reels 0.55%**, links 0.20%. By size: **1-5K = 4.35%**, 100K-1M = 0.50%. — [Socialinsider, Sep 2026](https://www.socialinsider.io/blog/social-media-reach/)
- Buffer (52M posts, as reported): the median Facebook post reaches 365 people, and **Pages under 1,000 followers reach a median of 21 people per post**. — [Apaya, Jul 2026 citing Buffer](https://apaya.com/blog/social-media-benchmarks-facebook)
- Buffer's cross-platform fixed-effects analysis (4.8M channel-weeks across FB, IG and X) found more posting means more follower growth. Compared with weeks with no posts, it added about 8 followers/week at 1-2 posts, about 10 at 3-5, about 21 at 6-10 and about 32 at 10+. — [Buffer data blog, Jun 2025](https://symphonious-shortbread-cd2770.netlify.app/posts/posting-frequency-eda/index.html)
- Groups: vendor analyses suggest Group posts reach far more members than Page posts reach followers. The source calls these figures "directional rather than audited." — [Nerdbot, Jul 2026](https://nerdbot.com/2026/07/29/how-brands-are-using-facebook-to-reach-millions/)
- Cross-posting: Meta's Fast Track program explicitly allows reposting your own videos from Instagram. — [Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/2026/03/18/meta-creator-fast-track-payouts/). Under Meta's originality rules, content "filmed or produced directly by" the Page owner counts as original. — [Meta Newsroom](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/03/rewarding-original-creators-on-facebook/). **I found no Meta data on how IG-to-FB crossposted Reels perform versus native uploads.**

### Inferences
- The formats disagree on Facebook: Reels have the weakest reach rate in Socialinsider's data even though Meta says original Reels views doubled. For a tiny Page, the sensible approach is to crosspost original Reels from IG at low effort and add albums or photo posts, which lead reach and engagement for small Pages.
- Posting link posts on the FB Page will likely underperform, given the link-post reach rate and the 98.5% no-link share of Feed views.

### Gaps
- No Meta data on crossposted vs native Reels performance.
- No audited data on brand-run Group reach.
- Could not access Meta Transparency Center's Facebook recommendations pages directly.

## 5. Profile conversion: turning visitors into followers

### Takeaway
There is no reliable public benchmark for profile-visit-to-follow conversion. Meta deprecated the profile-views API metric on 8 Jan 2025, so third-party tools cannot build one. The measure to track is follows ÷ profile visits in native Insights. Using benchmark medians, about 1.7% of reached accounts visit a profile. Vybe's roughly 40 visits/day against a total of 17 followers points to a large conversion problem.

### Cited Findings
- Meta deprecated the profile views metric from its Insights API on 8 Jan 2025. As a result, "no major benchmark study published since then reports a profile-visit-to-follow rate." The recommended measure is follows ÷ profile visits over 7- or 28-day windows. — [CreatorFlow, Sep 2026](https://creatorflow.so/blog/instagram-profile-optimization/)
- Highlights moved out of the circles under the bio into their own tab. Mosseri wanted to "get more of the content above the fold." Profiles get 3 pinned posts, or 6 with Instagram Plus, and grid reordering launched 8 Jun 2026. The Name field (30 characters) is searchable. — [CreatorFlow](https://creatorflow.so/blog/instagram-profile-optimization/)
- Databox medians: reach → profile visits about 1.7%, and profile visits → website clicks about 3%. Instagram supports up to 5 bio links natively. Socialinsider's story study (161,180 stories) found the biggest exit on the first frame (about 23.8%). — [PanelFollows citing Databox and Socialinsider, Aug 2026](https://panelfollows.com/en/blog/instagram-business-account-conversion)
- A vendor-claimed PV→F benchmark of "15-30%", with example audits moving PV→F from 9% to 17% after a bio rewrite, pinned posts and re-cut Reels. **Low reliability:** vendor content, unverifiable, and it conflicts with CreatorFlow's point that no valid benchmark exists. — [Viralfy, Jul 2026](https://www.viralfy.ai/en/blog/en-instagram-analytics-audit-25-engagement-in-30-days-cases-ms7l2br4)

### Inferences
- Rough, directional arithmetic from Vybe's own numbers: about 40 visits/day over several weeks against 17 total followers means well under 5% of visits become follows. The Databox medians (266 visits vs 11.5 new followers per month) imply about 4% even if every follow came from a visit, which gives a rough reference point. A profile with 3 photos and no Reels gives visitors little evidence of what they would get by following.
- The best profile levers available now: a name field with the category keyword, a bio saying who it is for and why to follow before the May 2027 launch, three pinned posts that each do one job, and enough Reels on the grid that a visitor can judge the account's value.

### Gaps
- No credible, current, cross-account PV→F benchmark exists.
- No controlled data on the effect of highlights or bio changes.

## 6. Documented case studies: small health/fitness brands

### Takeaway
The public "case studies" are almost all agency or vendor marketing. They are unaudited, often mix in paid spend, and some look illustrative. The recurring tactics are education-led Reels and carousels, consistent cadence, creator collaborations and a waitlist or community mechanic. The strongest quantitative evidence for collaboration comes from large datasets, not case studies: accepted collab posts gave XS brands (<10K followers) about 5x the interactions of their standard posts.

### Cited Findings
- **Emplifi H1 2026** (520,198 posts, 3,791 brand profiles): over 73% of brands used collab posts. **Accepted collaborations (creator-published, brand invited) from XS brands (<10K) earned 5x more interactions than their standard posts.** Reels saw the strongest gains. Small brands did best with "a limited number of carefully selected partners." — [Emplifi, 15 Sep 2026](https://emplifi.io/resources/instagram-collaborations-boost-organic-engagement/); [SMT via Yahoo, 16 Sep 2026](https://tech.yahoo.com/social-media/articles/report-highlights-value-creator-led-190438626.html)
- Academic study (100 collab posts): collab reach "is not merely additive" and tracks a weighted average of the accounts' reach. **"Only the 'smaller' account benefits."** — [Schöttl et al., Journal of Digital & Social Media Marketing, 2026](https://ideas.repec.org/a/aza/jdsmm0/y2026v13i4p363-376.html)
- Metricool: tagging or mentioning accounts gave Tiny accounts (<2K) +108% reach. — [Metricool trends](https://metricool.com/instagram-trends/)
- **Kamello** (kava/kanna wellness beverage, May-Jul 2026, run by agency Flying V Group; **paid ads also ran**): organic reach went from 4,496 to 58,896, followers from 624 to 2,787 (+347%), and profile visits plus link clicks from 1,090 to 18,787. Main tactic: category-education content. — [Flying V Group case study](https://www.flyingvgroup.com/kamello-instagram-organic-growth-case-study/)
- **Sleep-supplement DTC brand** (agency case, unnamed, UK/EU): built a library of 60+ posts before launch, ran a waitlist with a share-3-friends referral, a 30-day branded challenge, and 25 micro-influencer (5K-30K) product-free collaborations. The claimed result was 0 to 50K followers. Unaudited, and it used PR as well as organic. — [Scalarly, Dec 2025](https://scalarly.com/case-studies/brand-launch-consumer.html)
- Metricool highlights accounts that far outperform their category (for example, a tile-tutorial creator at 26x category reach) and attributes it to expertise-led educational content. These are creators, not health brands. — [Metricool trends](https://metricool.com/instagram-trends/)
- Excluded as unreliable: a "fitness coach 500→85K" story ([LeeSeoHits](https://leeseohits.com/blog/case-studies/fitness-coach-10k-month-business-instagram-case-study)) and a vendor's "0→100K fitness account" ([SociaVault](https://sociavault.com/blog/zero-to-100k-followers-data-driven-growth-case-study)). Neither is verifiable, and both read as illustrative or promotional.

### Inferences
- For a pre-launch health band that can't use DMs, collaborations are the most evidence-backed way to borrow a creator's audience: collab posts with aligned small creators or health professionals, plus tagging. Emplifi's 5x figure is for creator-published collabs, and setting one up usually needs some coordination, which may conflict with the "no pitches yet" constraint. Tagging relevant accounts in original posts is permitted under current rules.
- Education-led content ("what your resting heart rate actually tells you", privacy explainers) matches the recurring pattern in the cases above and the save/share signals in section 1.

### Gaps
- I found no audited case study of a pre-launch wearable or health-hardware brand growing organically on IG or FB.
- None of the case studies isolate the effect of outbound follow/comment outreach.
