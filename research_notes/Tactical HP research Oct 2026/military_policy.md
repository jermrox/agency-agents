# Military Human Performance and Fitness Policy Documents (2025-2026)

## What are the current Army H2F policies (FM 7-22 updates, Army Directives, ARs related to fitness)?

### Takeaway
The Army has undertaken a sweeping overhaul of fitness policy in 2025-2026, anchored by the updated FM 7-22 (Change 2, August 2025), a new two-test fitness structure (AFT + CFT) under Army Directive 2026-07, and a complete replacement of body composition methods with waist-to-height ratio under Army Directive 2026-13.

### Cited Findings
- FM 7-22, Holistic Health and Fitness, was updated with Change 2 dated 1 August 2025. It establishes the Army's doctrine for readiness training, covering physical, nutritional, mental, spiritual, and sleep readiness. The proponent is the U.S. Army Center for Initial Military Training (USACIMT), TRADOC. — [Amazon listing for FM 7-22 W/Change 2](https://www.amazon.com/FM-7-22-Holistic-Health-Fitness-ebook/dp/B0FM7XWM1X)
- The Army Fitness Test (AFT) replaced the ACFT as the official test of record on June 1, 2025. Starting October 1, 2025, all AFT general scores must be recorded in DTMS for promotion points, with AFT Combat scores required beginning January 1, 2026. — [Army Fitness Test page](https://www.army.mil/aft/)
- Army Directive 2026-07, signed by Secretary of the Army Dan Driscoll on April 17, 2026, established the Combat Field Test (CFT) as a new pass/fail, mission-based assessment for Soldiers in 24 designated combat MOSs. The CFT consists of 7 continuous events (1-mile run, 30 dead-stop pushups, 100m sprint, 16 sandbag lifts, 50m water can carry, 50m movement drill, second 1-mile run) completed in 30 minutes or less while wearing ACU and combat boots. A 365-day diagnostic/grace period commenced April 17, 2026. — [Army National Guard: AD 2026-07](https://www.armyng.com/2026/04/army-dir-2026-07-army-physical-fitness.html); [Modern War Institute analysis](https://mwi.westpoint.edu/the-combat-field-test-raised-the-armys-fitness-ceiling-now-its-time-to-raise-the-floor/)
- Army Directive 2026-13 (Army Body Composition Program and Standards), signed July 1, 2026 (effective July 7, 2026), replaced all Army body fat assessment methods with the waist-to-height ratio (WHtR). The standard is a WHtR of less than 0.55 (not equal to). The WHtR is not rounded; .549 passes, .550 fails. No secondary methods (tape test, body fat calculation) are authorized. All Soldiers must be measured at least twice per calendar year. — [Army Pubs: AD 2026-13](https://armypubs.army.mil/epubs/DR_pubs/DR_a/ARN47030-ARMY_DIR_2026-13-000-WEB-1.pdf); [Army NG summary](https://www.armyng.com/2026/07/army-directive-2026-13.html)
- The H2F System expansion plans target 111 active-duty brigades by May 2027, supported by 1,000 performance coaches. H2F has also expanded its scope to include functional proponency for the Pregnancy and Postpartum Performance Training (P3T) program. — [Defense News (Oct 2024)](https://www.defensenews.com/land/2024/10/14/army-wants-all-troops-on-new-fitness-program-by-2032-hows-it-going/); [Army.mil H2F expansion article](https://www.army.mil/article/289422/army_expands_program_that_transforms_how_soldiers_prepare_for_combat)
- As of September 3, 2026, the Army published a doctrine publications update for the H2F System, establishing a common operating language and unified training standards across the entire Force. — [Army.mil H2F Doctrine Update](https://www.army.mil/article/295085/h2f_system_update_doctrine_publications_update)

### Inferences
- The Army's fitness architecture now has three distinct layers: the AFT (general fitness, scored), the CFT (combat-specific, pass/fail), and body composition via WHtR. This represents a significant shift from the single-test ACFT model.
- The elimination of the tape test and height/weight tables in favor of WHtR suggests a DoD-wide push toward simpler, more objective body composition screening.

### Gaps
- The specific content changes in FM 7-22 Change 2 vs. Change 1 could not be confirmed from public sources; the army.mil domain was blocked for direct fetch.
- Specific AR updates (e.g., AR 600-9 Army Body Composition Program revision status in light of AD 2026-13) were not confirmed.

## What SecDef/SecWar memoranda have been issued on force readiness, fitness standards, or human performance?

### Takeaway
Secretary of War Pete Hegseth issued a foundational memorandum on September 30, 2025 establishing sex-neutral, combat-focused fitness standards across all services, followed by implementation guidance from the Under Secretary of War on December 18, 2025, and a separate March 31, 2025 memorandum on combat arms standards.

### Cited Findings
- Secretary of War Memorandum, "Military Fitness Standards," dated September 30, 2025, ordered comprehensive changes to military fitness across all services. Key mandates: sex-neutral standards rooted in combat effectiveness; two fitness tests annually (one Service Fitness Test + one Combat Field Test for combat arms or Combat Readiness Test for non-combat arms); daily physical fitness training for all service members; transition to waist-to-height ratio (WHtR) for body composition beginning January 1, 2026. — [SecWar Memo (PDF via army.mil)](https://api.army.mil/e2/c/downloads/2025/09/30/9c939542/military-fitness-standards-osd010715-25-fod-final.pdf)
- Under Secretary of War for Personnel and Readiness Memorandum, "Additional Guidance on Military Fitness Standards," dated December 18, 2025, provided implementation details for the September 30 directive. It directed the DoD-wide WHtR standard of less than 0.55 and twice-yearly body composition assessments. — [Additional Guidance (PDF via media.defense.gov)](https://media.defense.gov/2026/Jan/12/2003855613/-1/-1/1/ADDITIONAL-GUIDANCE-ON-MILITARY-FITNESS-STANDARDS.PDF)
- A separate memorandum on "Combat Arms Standards" was issued March 31, 2025, establishing specific combat-readiness fitness requirements for combat arms personnel across the services. — [Combat Arms Standards (PDF via media.defense.gov)](https://media.defense.gov/2025/Mar/31/2003678527/-1/-1/1/COMBAT-ARMS-STANDARDS.PDF)

### Inferences
- The September 30, 2025 memo is the single most consequential fitness policy document of this period -- it triggered cascading changes across all five services in their fitness testing, body composition, and training requirements.
- The renaming of the office from Secretary of Defense to Secretary of War (reflected in the memoranda headers) is consistent with the organizational rebranding that occurred in 2025.

### Gaps
- Whether additional SecWar memoranda specifically addressing mental health, sleep, or nutrition optimization were issued could not be confirmed from public search results.

## What MARADMINs from marines.mil address fitness standards, body composition, or PFT/CFT changes?

### Takeaway
Two key MARADMINs drove the Marine Corps fitness overhaul: MARADMIN 613/25 (PFT sex-neutral scoring for combat arms) and MARADMIN 066/26 (WHtR body composition standard of 0.52), both effective January 1, 2026. The Marine Corps standard of 0.52 WHtR is notably stricter than the DoD-wide 0.55 benchmark.

### Cited Findings
- MARADMIN 613/25 announced changes to the Physical Fitness Test effective January 1, 2026. It institutes sex-neutral scoring for combat arms Marines, requiring a minimum PFT score of 210 points (70% of 300) using the male, age-normed scoring standard per MCO 6100.13A. Non-combat arms Marines continue under existing sex- and age-normed standards. Marines failing to meet the 210-point threshold by end of reporting period face remedial PT, possible PMOS reclassification, and/or promotion restriction. — [Marines.mil: Advance Notification](https://www.marines.mil/News/Messages/Messages-Display/Article/4363582/advance-notification-of-changes-to-the-marine-corps-physical-fitness-test-and-b/); [Marines.mil: Press Release](https://www.marines.mil/News/Press-Releases/Press-Release-Display/Article/4363803/marine-corps-announces-updated-physical-fitness-standards/)
- MARADMIN 066/26 announced changes to body composition standards effective January 1, 2026. The traditional height-and-weight screening was replaced by a WHtR standard of less than or equal to 0.52, applicable semiannually to both active and reserve components. This is stricter than the DoD-wide standard of 0.55. — [Military.com: Marines Ditch Height-Weight](https://www.military.com/feature/2026/02/27/marines-ditch-traditional-height-weight-method-new-test-effective-now.html); [The Battle Bunker: USMC Height/Weight 2026](https://thebattlebunker.com/blogs/logbook/usmc-height-and-weight-standards-2026)
- A fitness performance incentive allows Marines scoring 285+ on both PFT and CFT to have up to 26% body fat (males) or 36% (females). Marines scoring 250+ receive an additional 1% allowance. — [The Battle Bunker: USMC PFT/CFT Standards 2026](https://thebattlebunker.com/blogs/logbook/usmc-fitness-standards-2026-what-is-new)
- The governing order remains MCO 6100.13A, with the MARADMINs providing interim implementation guidance aligned with the September 30, 2025 SecWar memorandum. — [DVIDSHUB: USMC announces updated standards](https://www.dvidshub.net/news/554807/marine-corps-announces-updated-physical-fitness-standards)

### Inferences
- The Marine Corps chose a significantly stricter WHtR standard (0.52) than the DoD floor (0.55), consistent with its historical posture of exceeding minimum DoD fitness requirements.
- The sex-neutral scoring requirement only applies to combat arms MOSs, not the entire Marine Corps, which is a narrower implementation than some initial reporting suggested.

### Gaps
- Whether additional MARADMINs were issued in mid-2026 with further refinements or corrections could not be confirmed.
- The specific list of combat arms PMOSs affected by the sex-neutral scoring requirement was not enumerated in available public sources.

## What NAVADMINs from mynavyhr.navy.mil cover PRT standards, body composition, or sailor readiness?

### Takeaway
NAVADMIN 264/25, issued December 30, 2025, announced the updated OPNAVINST 6110.1L and a comprehensive overhaul of the Navy Physical Readiness Program, including twice-yearly PFAs, a new Combat Fitness Assessment for combat-rated Sailors, and WHtR-based body composition screening effective for 2026.

### Cited Findings
- NAVADMIN 264/25 (December 30, 2025) announces the updated OPNAVINST 6110.1L and changes to the Physical Readiness Program (PRP). It establishes two fitness assessment cycles per calendar year: Cycle-1 (January 1 - June 30, 2026) and Cycle-2 (July 1 - December 31, 2026). — [NAVADMIN 264/25 (PDF)](https://www.mynavyhr.navy.mil/Portals/55/Messages/NAVADMIN/NAV2025/NAV25264.pdf); [Navy Times](https://www.navytimes.com/news/your-navy/2025/12/30/navy-doubles-annual-pt-test-requirement-updates-failure-guidelines/)
- Combat arms Sailors will take a Combat Fitness Assessment (CFA), while all other Sailors take two Physical Fitness Assessments (PFAs) per year, doubling the previous once-per-year requirement. — [Navy Times](https://www.navytimes.com/news/your-navy/2025/12/30/navy-doubles-annual-pt-test-requirement-updates-failure-guidelines/)
- Body Composition Assessment (BCA) procedures were revised to a two-step process: (1) Height, weight, and waist measurements collected on all Sailors, with sex-neutral WHtR calculation; (2) Body composition calculated using Navy-developed, sex-normed equations. Sailors failing BCA or PRT standards must participate in the Fitness Enhancement Program (FEP). — [Fact Sheet: NAV 264/25](https://www.mynavyhr.navy.mil/Portals/55/Messages/NAVADMIN/FACT_SHEETS/Fact_Sheet_NAV_264_25.pdf)
- Post-pregnant/postpartum Sailors who are medically cleared are required to participate in the PFA in which their 12-month deferment period ends. — [Fact Sheet: NAV 264/25](https://www.mynavyhr.navy.mil/Portals/55/Messages/NAVADMIN/FACT_SHEETS/Fact_Sheet_NAV_264_25.pdf)

### Inferences
- The Navy's approach to WHtR is a hybrid model: WHtR as an initial sex-neutral screen, followed by sex-normed body fat calculations. This differs from the Army and Marines, which use WHtR as the sole metric.
- Doubling the PFA requirement from once to twice annually is a major cultural shift for the Navy and aligns with the SecWar memorandum's directive for semiannual assessments.

### Gaps
- The specific passing WHtR threshold for the Navy (whether it uses the DoD standard of 0.55 or a service-specific number) was not explicitly stated in the available sources.
- Whether additional NAVADMINs were issued in 2026 with further implementation guidance for the CFA was not confirmed.

## What Air Force DTMs or AFIs address fitness testing or human performance?

### Takeaway
AFMAN 36-2905 (Department of the Air Force Manual 36-2905, Air Force Physical Fitness Readiness Program) was completely rewritten and published on March 24, 2026, overhauling the PFA with new test options, WHtR body composition scoring, and a phased rollout with diagnostic testing from March-June 2026 and official testing beginning July 1, 2026.

### Cited Findings
- AFMAN 36-2905 (also referenced as DAFMAN 36-2905), Air Force Physical Fitness Readiness Program, was published March 24, 2026 as a complete rewrite of the previous fitness policy. — [AFMAN 36-2905 (PDF via e-publishing.af.mil)](https://static.e-publishing.af.mil/production/1/af_a1/publication/afman36-2905/dafman36-2905.pdf)
- PFA testing was paused January 1 - February 28, 2026 to facilitate transition. Diagnostic testing ran March 1 - June 30, 2026. Official testing under new standards began July 1, 2026. — [AF.mil: Air Force updates fitness test requirements](https://www.af.mil/News/Article-Display/Article/4371953/air-force-updates-fitness-test-requirements/)
- New PFA components: Cardiovascular (2-mile run or 20-meter HAMR shuttle run); Strength (1 minute of push-ups or 2 minutes of hand-release push-ups); Core (1 minute of sit-ups, 2 minutes of cross-leg reverse crunches, or timed forearm plank); Body Composition (WHtR measurement). — [AF.mil: Air Force updates fitness test requirements](https://www.af.mil/News/Article-Display/Article/4371953/air-force-updates-fitness-test-requirements/)
- WHtR is worth 20 points on the PFA. A WHtR of 0.50 or lower earns maximum points; 0.60 or above earns zero. WHtR replaced the legacy abdominal circumference tape test. Measurement is taken on bare skin at the midpoint between lowest rib and iliac crest; three measurements taken, averaged, rounded down to nearest half inch. — [USAF PT Scores: WHtR guide](https://usafptscores.com/waist-to-height-ratio); [DVIDSHUB: Steel Airmen PFA standards](https://www.dvidshub.net/news/printable/562529)
- The Air Force modernized its Physical Fitness Program under a "Culture of Fitness" initiative applying to Total Force Airmen. — [AF.mil: Modernizes Physical Fitness Program](https://www.af.mil/News/Article-Display/Article/4418303/air-force-modernizes-physical-fitness-program-to-bolster-readiness-long-term-he/)

### Inferences
- The Air Force's approach to WHtR is notably different from other services: it is a scored component (0-20 points) rather than a pass/fail gate, allowing strong aerobic/strength performance to offset a marginally higher WHtR.
- The multiple exercise options (e.g., push-ups vs. hand-release push-ups, sit-ups vs. plank vs. reverse crunches) give Airmen more flexibility than the Army's or Marines' fixed-event tests.

### Gaps
- Whether a separate Combat Readiness Test for Air Force combat arms personnel (e.g., TACP, PJ, CCT) has been published as a distinct directive was not confirmed.

## What Space Force directives address fitness standards or guardian readiness?

### Takeaway
The Space Force published its first standalone fitness manual -- Space Force Manual (SFMAN) 36-2905, Human Performance and Readiness -- in late September 2025, establishing the Human Performance Assessment (HPA) with twice-yearly testing effective January 1, 2026, scored on an 80-point scale with a minimum composite of 60.

### Cited Findings
- SFMAN 36-2905, Human Performance and Readiness, was published in late September 2025 as the Space Force's first standalone fitness manual, separating from the previously shared Air Force fitness policy. — [SpaceForce.mil: Space Force publishes guidance on physical fitness](https://www.spaceforce.mil/News/Article-Display/Article/4371955/space-force-publishes-further-guidance-on-physical-fitness/); [ClearanceJobs: USSF Human Performance Manual](https://news.clearancejobs.com/2025/09/30/the-united-states-space-force-issues-a-human-performance-and-readiness-manual/)
- Effective January 1, 2026, all Guardians must complete two Human Performance Assessments (HPAs) annually, regardless of enrollment in the Continuous Fitness Assessment program. The initial HPA must be completed between January 1 and June 30, 2026. — [SpaceForce.mil: Space Force publishes guidance](https://www.spaceforce.mil/News/Article-Display/Article/4371955/space-force-publishes-further-guidance-on-physical-fitness/)
- The HPA measures muscular strength, muscular endurance, and cardiorespiratory fitness on an 80-point scale. Guardians must earn a composite score of 60 or higher to be considered physically ready. Cardio options: 2-mile run or 20-meter HAMR, with at least one annual assessment using the 2-mile run. — [SpaceForce.mil: Space Force publishes guidance](https://www.spaceforce.mil/News/Article-Display/Article/4371955/space-force-publishes-further-guidance-on-physical-fitness/)
- Squadrons establish Unit Fitness Cells staffed by Guardian Resilience Teams. Fitness scores are included on officer and enlisted performance briefs beginning February 2026. — [SpaceForce.mil: Space Force publishes guidance](https://www.spaceforce.mil/News/Article-Display/Article/4371955/space-force-publishes-further-guidance-on-physical-fitness/)

### Inferences
- The Space Force's 80-point scale and 60-point minimum threshold is structurally different from the Air Force's 100-point system, representing a deliberate break from shared DAF fitness policy.
- The integration of fitness scores into performance briefs starting February 2026 signals that the Space Force intends fitness to carry promotion/evaluation weight early in the program.

### Gaps
- Whether SFMAN 36-2905 includes a WHtR body composition component aligned with the DoD-wide standard was not explicitly confirmed in search results, though the Orbital Today article references an "overhaul" of fitness rules.
- Whether Guardian Resilience Teams are analogous to the Army's H2F performance teams in terms of staffing (dietitians, strength coaches, mental health professionals) was not detailed.

## Any DoDIs from esd.whs.mil on force health, readiness, or human performance optimization?

### Takeaway
DoDI 1308.03, DoD Physical Fitness/Body Composition Program, was reissued with an effective date of August 31, 2026, incorporating the September 2025 and December 2025 SecWar/USD(P&R) memoranda. This is the overarching DoD instruction that sets the floor for all service-level fitness and body composition policies.

### Cited Findings
- DoDI 1308.03, DoD Physical Fitness/Body Composition Program, was reissued effective August 31, 2026, canceling the previous version from March 10, 2022. It incorporates guidance from the Secretary of War Memorandum "Military Fitness Standards" (September 30, 2025) and the USD(P&R) Memorandum "Additional Guidance on Military Fitness Standards" (December 18, 2025). — [DoDI 1308.03 (PDF via esd.whs.mil)](https://www.esd.whs.mil/Portals/54/Documents/DD/issuances/dodi/130803p.PDF)
- The universal DoD standard for body composition is a WHtR of less than 0.55. Body composition may be evaluated using body fat calculations, waist-to-height ratio, abdominal circumference, height-weight screening, or any combination, giving services flexibility in implementation. Service members undergo body composition assessments twice per year. — [DoDI 1308.03 (PDF)](https://www.esd.whs.mil/Portals/54/Documents/DD/issuances/dodi/130803p.PDF); [Air & Space Forces Magazine](https://www.airandspaceforces.com/dod-services-more-options-measuring-body-composition/)
- A Congressional Research Service defense primer on military physical fitness testing and body composition standards provides additional context on the DoD-wide framework. — [CRS Defense Primer (PDF)](https://www.congress.gov/crs_external_products/IF/PDF/IF12192/IF12192.2.pdf)

### Inferences
- DoDI 1308.03 sets the DoD floor (WHtR < 0.55) but explicitly allows services to set stricter standards, which explains why the Marines adopted 0.52 and the Air Force scores WHtR on a gradient rather than a simple pass/fail.
- The August 2026 reissue date suggests a deliberate sequencing: SecWar memo (Sep 2025) -> USD(P&R) guidance (Dec 2025) -> service implementation (Jan-Jul 2026) -> formal DoDI codification (Aug 2026).

### Gaps
- Whether DoDI updates specifically addressing human performance optimization (beyond fitness testing/body composition), such as sleep policy, nutrition standards, or mental performance, were issued in 2025-2026 was not confirmed in available search results.
- The status of DoDI 6130.03 (Medical Standards for Military Service) updates related to readiness was not investigated due to scope constraints.
