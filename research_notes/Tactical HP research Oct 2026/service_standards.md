# U.S. Military Fitness Test Standards, Body Composition, and Readiness Metrics (2025–2026)

## What is the current state of the Army fitness test — scoring, event standards, gender/age norming, minimum passing scores?

### Takeaway
The ACFT was replaced on June 1, 2025 by the Army Fitness Test (AFT), a five-event, 500-point test with gender- and age-normed scoring for the general population and a sex-neutral, age-normed "Combat MOS" standard requiring 350 points, effective January 1, 2026 (Active) and June 1, 2026 (Reserve/Guard).

### Cited Findings
- The ACFT was officially replaced by the AFT effective June 1, 2025; the AFT is the Army's test of record — [Army AFT Standards 2026 (The Battle Bunker)](https://thebattlebunker.com/blogs/logbook/army-aft-standards-events-scoring-what-changed)
- The AFT has five events (the Standing Power Throw was removed): 3-Repetition Maximum Deadlift, Hand-Release Push-Ups, Sprint-Drag-Carry, Plank, and 2-Mile Run — [AFT Calculator & Score Chart 2026 (The Battle Bunker)](https://thebattlebunker.com/pages/army-acft-calculator)
- Each event is scored 0–100 points; maximum composite is 500; minimum passing per event is 60 points — [AFT Calculator (aftcalc.net)](https://aftcalc.net/)
- General Standard: 300 total on gender- and age-normed tables, with 60 minimum per event — [Army AFT Standards 2026 (The Battle Bunker)](https://thebattlebunker.com/blogs/logbook/army-aft-standards-events-scoring-what-changed)
- Combat MOS Standard: 350 total on sex-neutral, age-normed tables (the "M|C" column), with 60 minimum per event. Effective January 1, 2026 for Active Component; June 1, 2026 for Reserve and National Guard — [AFT Calculator (aftcalc.net)](https://aftcalc.net/); [Army Fitness Test (USAMM)](https://www.usamm.com/blogs/news/army-fitness-test-aft-new-standards-guide)
- AFT scores are now factored into the Army's Quality Tiered Incentive (QTI) retention bonus program, weighting physical fitness alongside technical expertise and command assessment — [Army updates retention program for 2026 (Stars and Stripes)](https://www.stripes.com/branches/army/2026-03-04/army-launch-retention-bonus-program-20952896.html)

### Inferences
- The shift from six to five events (dropping Standing Power Throw) and from 600 to 500 points simplifies scoring while the new combat MOS standard effectively raises the bar for infantry and other combat occupational specialties.
- Tying AFT scores to retention bonuses marks the first time the Army has financially incentivized PT performance in its reenlistment framework.

### Gaps
- Exact scoring tables (raw performance to points) per age group for all five events were not available from primary Army sources in this search; third-party calculator sites replicate them but the authoritative AR/DA PAM source was not directly accessible.

## What are current Marine Corps PFT and CFT standards and any recent changes?

### Takeaway
The Marine Corps announced major changes effective January 1, 2026 (MARADMIN 613/25): combat-arms Marines must now meet sex-neutral PFT standards with a 210-point minimum (70% of 300) scored against the male, age-normed table, and the legacy height/weight screening was replaced by a waist-to-height ratio (WHtR) of 0.52 or below (MARADMIN 066/26).

### Cited Findings
- MARADMIN 613/25 instituted sex-neutral PFT scoring for Marines in combat-arms Primary Military Occupational Specialties (PMOS), effective January 1, 2026 — [Marine Corps Announces Updated Physical Fitness Standards (marines.mil)](https://www.marines.mil/News/Press-Releases/Press-Release-Display/Article/4363803/marine-corps-announces-updated-physical-fitness-standards/)
- Combat-arms Marines must achieve a minimum 210 points (70% of 300) using the male, age-normed scoring standard per MCO 6100.13A — [marines.mil press release](https://www.marines.mil/News/Press-Releases/Press-Release-Display/Article/4363803/marine-corps-announces-updated-physical-fitness-standards/)
- Non-combat-arms Marines continue to follow existing sex- and age-normed PFT standards — [marines.mil press release](https://www.marines.mil/News/Press-Releases/Press-Release-Display/Article/4363803/marine-corps-announces-updated-physical-fitness-standards/)
- Marines who do not meet the 210-point requirement by the end of the reporting period are assigned to remedial PT and may face PMOS reclassification and/or promotion restriction — [marines.mil press release](https://www.marines.mil/News/Press-Releases/Press-Release-Display/Article/4363803/marine-corps-announces-updated-physical-fitness-standards/)
- PFT continues to be administered annually, January 1 through June 30 — [USMC PFT and CFT Standards 2026 (The Battle Bunker)](https://thebattlebunker.com/blogs/logbook/usmc-fitness-standards-2026-what-is-new)
- Body composition screening changed from the legacy height/weight tables to a waist-to-height ratio standard of 0.52 or less, per MARADMIN 066/26 — [Marines Get Tougher: 2026 Brings Unified Fitness Standards (Military.com)](https://www.military.com/feature/2025/12/19/marines-get-tougher-2026-brings-unified-fitness-standards.html)
- The Marine Corps WHtR standard (0.52) is stricter than the DoD-wide 0.55 ceiling — [MOAA](https://www.moaa.org/content/publications-and-media/news-articles/2026-news-articles/reads/pentagon-announces-new-approach-to-how-the-military-will-measure-troops-body-fat/)
- PFT events remain: pull-ups (or push-ups), crunches (or plank), and a 3-mile run — [USMC PFT and CFT Standards 2026 (The Battle Bunker)](https://thebattlebunker.com/blogs/logbook/usmc-fitness-standards-2026-what-is-new)

### Inferences
- The Marines adopted the most aggressive version of the DoD-wide fitness reset: tighter WHtR (0.52 vs. 0.55) and an immediate sex-neutral combat-arms requirement without a diagnostic transition period.
- PMOS reclassification as a consequence (not just promotion restriction) is a uniquely Marine Corps lever.

### Gaps
- Detailed CFT (Combat Fitness Test) event changes for 2026 were not found in available sources; it is unclear whether the CFT also adopted sex-neutral scoring for combat-arms Marines.
- Updated scoring tables for the sex-neutral PFT standard were not directly accessible.

## What are current Navy PRT standards and recent modifications?

### Takeaway
The Navy moved to twice-yearly Physical Readiness Tests in 2026 (NAVADMIN 264/25), adopted a sex-neutral waist-to-height ratio as the first-step body composition screen (aligning with DoD 0.55 standard), and retained sex- and age-normed scoring for PRT events (push-ups, plank, 1.5-mile run).

### Cited Findings
- Most Sailors now take the PRT twice per year: Cycle 1 January–June, Cycle 2 July–December — [Navy adds second fitness test requirement in 2026 (Stars and Stripes)](https://www.stripes.com/branches/navy/2025-12-31/navy-fitness-test-twice-a-year-20258576.html)
- PRT events: two-minute push-ups, timed forearm plank, and 1.5-mile run (or walk) — [OPNAV PRP Guide-5A (December 2025)](https://www.mynavyhr.navy.mil/Portals/55/Support/Culture%20Resilience/Physical/Guide-5A%20Physical%20Readiness%20Test.pdf)
- Body composition assessment is now two-step: Step 1 is a sex-neutral waist-to-height ratio (WHtR) screen; Step 2 (for those exceeding the WHtR cutoff) uses Navy-developed body-fat equations that remain sex-normed — [Navy Publishes Updated PFA Guidance (USNI News)](https://news.usni.org/2025/12/30/navy-publishes-new-physical-fitness-assessment-guidance-requiring-two-tests-per-year)
- Scoring categories range from Maximum (100 points) through Outstanding, Excellent, Good, Satisfactory Medium, to Probationary (45 points), with standards varying by age group — [Navy PRT Standards by Age and Gender (OperationMilitaryKids)](https://www.operationmilitarykids.org/navy-prt-standards/)
- Example minimum passing (Probationary) for males aged 20–24: 37 push-ups, 1:10 plank, 13:30 1.5-mile run — [Navy PRT Standards (OperationMilitaryKids)](https://www.operationmilitarykids.org/navy-prt-standards/)
- The WHtR screening cutoff aligns with the DoD standard of less than 0.55 — [MOAA](https://www.moaa.org/content/publications-and-media/news-articles/2026-news-articles/reads/pentagon-announces-new-approach-to-how-the-military-will-measure-troops-body-fat/)

### Inferences
- The Navy's move to twice-yearly testing doubles the administrative fitness burden but aligns with the DoD push for continuous readiness monitoring.
- The Navy did not adopt sex-neutral PRT event scoring; the gender-neutral requirement applies only to the body composition screen.

### Gaps
- Specific details on how PRT failure now affects Navy promotion boards (beyond general "adverse fitness report" language) were not found in the available search results.
- Alternative cardio options (swim, bike) and whether they remain available under the 2026 policy were not confirmed.

## What are current Air Force fitness assessment standards and changes?

### Takeaway
The Air Force overhauled its Physical Fitness Assessment (PFA) effective 2026 with four scored components (cardiorespiratory, strength, core, body composition via WHtR), a diagnostic period from March 1–June 30, 2026, and official testing resuming July 1, 2026, with tests required every six months.

### Cited Findings
- The new PFA evaluates four categories: Cardiorespiratory Fitness (2-mile run or 20-meter HAMR), Muscular Strength (1-minute push-ups or 2-minute hand-release push-ups), Muscular Core Endurance (1-minute sit-ups, 2-minute cross-leg reverse crunches, or timed forearm plank), and Body Composition (Waist-to-Height Ratio) — [Air Force Updates Fitness Test Requirements (af.mil)](https://www.af.mil/News/Article-Display/Article/4371953/air-force-updates-fitness-test-requirements/)
- Diagnostic testing period: March 1 – June 30, 2026; official testing resumes July 1, 2026 under the new PFA standards — [Air Force Updates Fitness Test Requirements (af.mil)](https://www.af.mil/News/Article-Display/Article/4371953/air-force-updates-fitness-test-requirements/)
- The PFA is mandatory every six months — [Air Force Updates Fitness Test Requirements (af.mil)](https://www.af.mil/News/Article-Display/Article/4371953/air-force-updates-fitness-test-requirements/)
- Airmen can choose either the 2-mile run or the 20-meter High Aerobic Multi-shuttle Run (HAMR) on both yearly tests — [Airmen can now swap out the 2-mile run on both yearly fitness tests (Task & Purpose)](https://taskandpurpose.com/news/air-space-force-fitness-tests-2026/)
- WHtR standard aligns with DoD ceiling of less than 0.55; those exceeding it undergo secondary body-fat evaluation using multi-site tape, BIA, or DEXA — [MOAA](https://www.moaa.org/content/publications-and-media/news-articles/2026-news-articles/reads/pentagon-announces-new-approach-to-how-the-military-will-measure-troops-body-fat/)
- The Air Force modernized its Physical Fitness Program "to bolster readiness, long-term health" — [Air Force modernizes Physical Fitness Program (af.mil)](https://www.af.mil/News/Article-Display/Article/4418303/air-force-modernizes-physical-fitness-program-to-bolster-readiness-long-term-he/)

### Inferences
- The Air Force took a gradual approach with a four-month diagnostic window, unlike the Marines who went live on January 1, 2026.
- The addition of WHtR as a scored PFA component (not just a pass/fail screen) represents a significant shift; previously the AF used a waist-circumference measurement scored as part of the composite.

### Gaps
- Exact scoring charts (points per event tier) for the new PFA were not directly available from primary AF sources.
- Whether the new PFA scoring replaces the old 75-point composite minimum is not confirmed.

## What are Space Force physical fitness requirements — do they follow AF standards or have their own?

### Takeaway
The Space Force has its own fitness program governed by SPFMAN 36-2905, separate from the Air Force's DAFMAN 36-2905. The Space Force Human Performance Assessment (HPA) is scored on a 100-point composite, with separate performance categories (Fit to Fight, Health Maintenance, Health Concern), and includes a voluntary wearable fitness tracking study as an alternative pathway.

### Cited Findings
- The Space Force published its own Human Performance and Readiness manual (SPFMAN 36-2905), establishing a fitness program distinct from the Air Force — [US Space Force releases new Human Performance and Readiness manual (spaceforce.mil)](https://www.spaceforce.mil/News/Article-Display/Article/4316219/us-space-force-releases-new-human-performance-and-readiness-manual/)
- Effective January 1, 2026, all Guardians must complete two HPAs annually — [Space Force publishes further guidance on physical fitness (spaceforce.mil)](https://www.spaceforce.mil/News/Article-Display/Article/4371955/space-force-publishes-further-guidance-on-physical-fitness/)
- HPA composite is 100 points: 60 points for cardiorespiratory fitness (2-mile run or 20-meter HAMR), 20 points for muscular strength (tempo push-ups or hand-release push-ups), 20 points for muscular endurance (sit-ups, cross-leg reverse crunches, or timed forearm plank) — [Space Force PT Calculator (VetRafi)](https://recruiter.vetrafi.com/space-force-pt-calculator); [SPFMAN 36-2905 (e-publishing.af.mil)](https://static.e-publishing.af.mil/production/1/sf_s1/publication/spfman36-2905/spfman36-2905.pdf)
- Scoring categories: 72+ points = "Fit to Fight"; 60–71.9 = "Health Maintenance"; below 60 = "Health Concern" (must enroll in fitness improvement program and retest) — [From Wearables to Waist Ratios (ClearanceJobs)](https://news.clearancejobs.com/2026/01/14/from-wearables-to-waist-ratios-updates-made-to-the-space-force-fitness-rules/)
- Body Composition (WHtR) is assessed annually during the Guardian's birth month and is NOT part of the 100-point HPA composite. WHtR bands: below 0.49 = Fit to Fight; 0.49 = meets standard; 0.50–0.54 = Health Maintenance; 0.55+ = Health Concern — [Space Force PT Calculator (VetRafi)](https://recruiter.vetrafi.com/space-force-pt-calculator)
- At least one of the two annual HPAs must include the 2-mile run (the HAMR cannot be used for both) — [From Wearables to Waist Ratios (ClearanceJobs)](https://news.clearancejobs.com/2026/01/14/from-wearables-to-waist-ratios-updates-made-to-the-space-force-fitness-rules/)
- The Space Force partnered with AFRL on a two-year voluntary wearable fitness study (started June 2023); participants meeting criteria (600 exercise minutes/month, monthly cardiorespiratory readings) are exempt from the traditional HPA — [From Wearables to Waist Ratios (ClearanceJobs)](https://news.clearancejobs.com/2026/01/14/from-wearables-to-waist-ratios-updates-made-to-the-space-force-fitness-rules/)

### Inferences
- The Space Force has decisively separated from AF fitness governance, establishing its own manual, scoring system, and performance categories.
- The wearable study represents the most innovative approach to fitness monitoring across all services, potentially signaling a future shift to continuous monitoring over point-in-time testing.
- Space Force WHtR bands are stricter than the DoD floor (Health Maintenance starts at 0.50 vs. the DoD 0.55 ceiling).

### Gaps
- Whether the wearable study will become the permanent standard after the two-year study period concludes is not confirmed.
- Exact scoring tables (raw performance to points) for HPA events were not found in primary USSF sources.

## What body composition/body fat standards apply in each service and any recent changes?

### Takeaway
In January 2026, the DoD directed all services to adopt waist-to-height ratio (WHtR) as the primary body composition screening tool, replacing legacy height/weight tables and circumference-based tape tests. The DoD ceiling is WHtR < 0.55, but the Marines set a stricter 0.52, and the Space Force uses graduated bands starting at 0.50.

### Cited Findings
- On January 12, 2026, the Pentagon released implementing guidance directing all services to use WHtR as the primary body composition screening tool, with an upper limit of less than 0.55 — [Additional Guidance on Military Fitness Standards (media.defense.gov)](https://media.defense.gov/2026/Jan/12/2003855613/-1/-1/1/ADDITIONAL-GUIDANCE-ON-MILITARY-FITNESS-STANDARDS.PDF)
- Defense Secretary Hegseth previewed the WHtR shift on September 30, 2025, as part of "restoring the warrior ethos" — [Pentagon implements new waist-to-height standards (Task & Purpose)](https://taskandpurpose.com/news/military-waist-height-ratio/)
- All service members will be screened twice per year on WHtR — [Pentagon to start measuring troops' body fat by WHtR (Military Times)](https://www.militarytimes.com/news/pentagon-congress/2026/01/13/pentagon-to-start-measuring-troops-body-fat-by-waist-to-height-ratio/)
- Marines adopted WHtR 0.52 or less (per MARADMIN 066/26), the strictest across all services — [Marines Get Tougher (Military.com)](https://www.military.com/feature/2025/12/19/marines-get-tougher-2026-brings-unified-fitness-standards.html)
- Army adopted the DoD standard of WHtR < 0.55 — [Army rolls out rules on waist-to-height body standards (Task & Purpose)](https://taskandpurpose.com/news/army-waist-height-2026/)
- If a service member exceeds the WHtR cutoff, a secondary evaluation is performed using multi-site tape test, bioelectrical impedance analysis (BIA), or DEXA scan (when available) — [MOAA](https://www.moaa.org/content/publications-and-media/news-articles/2026-news-articles/reads/pentagon-announces-new-approach-to-how-the-military-will-measure-troops-body-fat/)
- Failure to meet body composition standards results in enrollment in remedial programs, referral to medical evaluation, withholding of favorable personnel actions (including promotions), and potential administrative separation for continued non-compliance — [Pentagon implements new waist-to-height standards (Task & Purpose)](https://taskandpurpose.com/news/military-waist-height-ratio/)
- New fitness standards compliance will be checked during Inspector General reviews — [New fitness standards will be checked in Inspector General reviews (Task & Purpose)](https://taskandpurpose.com/news/dod-accountable-fitness-ig-reviews/)

### Inferences
- The DoD-wide WHtR mandate represents the largest standardization of body composition assessment across all services in decades, eliminating per-service measurement methodologies.
- IG reviews for fitness compliance suggest the DoD is treating fitness as a readiness metric subject to institutional accountability, not just individual consequence.

### Gaps
- The exact body-fat percentage cutoffs for the secondary evaluation (after exceeding WHtR) by service were not found in available sources.

## How do promotion/retention implications of fitness testing differ across services?

### Takeaway
All services now tie fitness performance to promotion and retention, but the mechanisms differ: the Army links AFT scores to retention bonuses via the QTI program; the Marines threaten PMOS reclassification for combat-arms failures; all services can withhold promotions and initiate separation for repeated failures.

### Cited Findings
- Army: The Quality Tiered Incentive (QTI) retention bonus program weights AFT scores alongside technical expertise and command assessment to determine bonus tier placement — [Army launching new merit-based retention bonus program (Stars and Stripes)](https://www.stripes.com/branches/army/2026-03-04/army-launch-retention-bonus-program-20952896.html)
- Marines: Failure to meet the 210-point sex-neutral PFT standard results in remedial PT, potential PMOS reclassification, and/or promotion restriction — [marines.mil press release](https://www.marines.mil/News/Press-Releases/Press-Release-Display/Article/4363803/marine-corps-announces-updated-physical-fitness-standards/)
- All services: Service members who fail body composition standards face withholding of favorable personnel actions (including promotions), enrollment in remedial programs, and potential administrative separation — [Pentagon implements new waist-to-height standards (Task & Purpose)](https://taskandpurpose.com/news/military-waist-height-ratio/)
- Space Force: Guardians scoring below 60 on the HPA are classified "Health Concern" and must enroll in fitness improvement program and retest — [ClearanceJobs](https://news.clearancejobs.com/2026/01/14/from-wearables-to-waist-ratios-updates-made-to-the-space-force-fitness-rules/)
- DoD-wide: IG reviews will now include compliance with fitness standards — [Task & Purpose](https://taskandpurpose.com/news/dod-accountable-fitness-ig-reviews/)

### Inferences
- The Army is the only service to directly tie fitness to financial incentives (retention bonuses), making PT a dollar-and-cents proposition.
- The Marines have the steepest career consequence (PMOS reclassification) short of separation, which could end a combat-arms career path even if the Marine remains in service.

### Gaps
- Navy-specific promotion board weighting of PRT scores was not detailed in available sources.
- Air Force-specific consequences for PFA failure under the new 2026 system (beyond general DoD guidance) were not found.

## What MOS/AFSC-specific physical standards exist (e.g., infantry, special operations, aviation)?

All services maintain occupation-specific physical standards beyond the baseline fitness test, most notably the Army's combat MOS AFT standard (350 points, sex-neutral), the Marine Corps' combat-arms PFT requirement (210 sex-neutral), and separate selection/pipeline tests for special operations across all services (Army SFAS, Air Force IFT/PAST, Navy SEAL PST).

### Cited Findings

## What are the current height/weight and tape test procedures across services?

### Takeaway
The DoD-wide shift to waist-to-height ratio (WHtR < 0.55) as the primary body composition screen has replaced or supplemented traditional height/weight tables across all services as of January 2026. The legacy tape test now serves as a secondary evaluation method rather than the primary screen.

### Cited Findings
- DoD directive (January 12, 2026): WHtR replaces height/weight tables as the primary screening tool; upper limit is WHtR < 0.55 across all services — [Additional Guidance on Military Fitness Standards (media.defense.gov)](https://media.defense.gov/2026/Jan/12/2003855613/-1/-1/1/ADDITIONAL-GUIDANCE-ON-MILITARY-FITNESS-STANDARDS.PDF)
- Secondary evaluation for those exceeding WHtR: multi-site circumference tape test, bioelectrical impedance analysis (BIA), or DEXA scan (when available) — [MOAA](https://www.moaa.org/content/publications-and-media/news-articles/2026-news-articles/reads/pentagon-announces-new-approach-to-how-the-military-will-measure-troops-body-fat/)
- Army adopted WHtR < 0.55 (matching DoD standard) — [Army rolls out rules on waist-to-height (Task & Purpose)](https://taskandpurpose.com/news/army-waist-height-2026/)
- Marines adopted WHtR ≤ 0.52 (stricter than DoD) — [Marines Get Tougher (Military.com)](https://www.military.com/feature/2025/12/19/marines-get-tougher-2026-brings-unified-fitness-standards.html)
- Space Force uses graduated WHtR bands: <0.49 Fit to Fight, 0.49 meets standard, 0.50–0.54 Health Maintenance, 0.55+ Health Concern — [ClearanceJobs](https://news.clearancejobs.com/2026/01/14/from-wearables-to-waist-ratios-updates-made-to-the-space-force-fitness-rules/)
- Navy WHtR screen is sex-neutral; secondary body-fat calculation uses Navy-developed sex-normed equations — [USNI News](https://news.usni.org/2025/12/30/navy-publishes-new-physical-fitness-assessment-guidance-requiring-two-tests-per-year)

### Inferences
- The WHtR standard represents a major simplification: one measurement (waist) divided by height replaces complex multi-site tape procedures as the first gate.
- The Marines' 0.52 standard will likely drive the highest body composition failure rates given it is significantly more restrictive than the 0.55 DoD floor.

### Gaps
