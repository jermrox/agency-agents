Vybe Health: Instagram Targeting Research

Prepared 11 October 2026 for @vybehealthinc. Evidence window: 2010–2026; all web sources accessed 2026-10-11. First-party data pulled live from Instagram and Meta via Composio the same day.

Executive summary

The current method is wrong. Since Oct 3, we have picked people by searching the web for coaches in each sport and keeping any 3K–50K account with no wearable sponsor. That checks that a person is real. It says nothing about whether they will follow back or whether their audience will buy. The founder was right to call it guessing.

Five findings change the plan:

  1. Following people cannot reach 1,000 real followers by Oct 23. No study gives an Instagram follow-back rate for a brand following strangers; that number is unknown. Twitter-network studies put mutual follows at 17–42% (Kwak 2010: 22.1%). At 15–30 follows a day, that gives roughly 40–145 follow-backs by Oct 23, against about 955 needed. [calc] Following is a supporting tactic, not the engine.
  2. We target the wrong kind of account. A field experiment (Que, Rajagopalan & Zaman, MIT, 2018) found follow-back is most likely from accounts that follow many others, have few followers, and already share mutuals with you. Larger accounts reciprocate less (arXiv 1302.0677). Our lists skew to 10K–50K coaches, who are the least likely to follow back.
  3. Reach comes from content and collaborations, not follower count. Mosseri (Jan 2025) names watch time, likes per reach and sends per reach as the top signals. Sends matter most for reaching non-followers. For 1K–5K accounts, Reels reach 9.78% of followers, against 7% for images (Socialinsider 2026, 140K Reels). In a collaborative post, the smaller account gains reach (Schöttl, n=100 collab posts).
  4. HYROX is closed to Vybe as a sponsorship lane. Amazfit signed a 3-year global exclusive on 2026-04-15 covering watches, rings and smart straps, plus the HYROX 365 coach network. It sells a $99 screenless Helio Strap and gave one free to every 2025 World Championships racer. HYROX stays the founder's anchor audience, but every HYROX coach we follow sits inside Amazfit's ecosystem. Our edge there is the everyday 35+ racer, not coaches or the event.
  5. The open lanes are masters swimming, pickleball 40+, indoor rowing, masters 40+ strength and strongman. We found no wearable sponsor for USMS, the PPA Tour, rowing federations or World's Strongest Man. Tennis (Oura–USTA, 5 years from May 2026), padel (WHOOP–Premier Padel, Mar 2026), trail/ultra (COROS and Suunto at UTMB) and HYROX (Amazfit) are owned.

One blocker for the founder: masters swimming ranks #1 on evidence. It only works if the band can be worn in the pool. Vybe's water rating is not in the brand brief, the concept doc or the dashboard. It is unknown, and the founder needs to supply it.

3b. Collab targets (for reach)

Separate list. These are 10K–50K clean creators in the open lanes, approached for Instagram collab posts (co-authored Reels shown to both audiences). Pre-launch there is no product to seed, so the offer is content: a "What changed?" Reel built from their week, credited and co-posted. Never imply sponsorship or HYROX affiliation. Start with 3 per lane. The founder sends the DMs; Claude drafts them only.

3c. Where to find them without hashtag search

Meta blocks hashtag search until app review, so the alternatives are: 1. Followers of community hubs. Candidates come from the follow lists and comment sections of @usms-adjacent clubs, PPA/USA Pickleball, nurse hubs (@nursesofinstagram) and Concept2. The founder sees these in the app; Claude verifies each handle with Meta. 2. Our own engagers. Anyone who likes or comments on a Vybe Reel. 3. The Meta App Review for Instagram Public Content Access. Apply now. It unlocks hashtag search for daily candidate discovery. Typical review time is unknown; the documentation is at developers.facebook.com/docs/apps/review.

What would change the plan Masters swimming is unrated for water → drop it to a test lane. Everyday athletes follow back below coaches → revert the 3a type rule. A collab Reel delivers 100+ follows → shift effort from follows to collabs

The test cannot run unless follows are ticked. The founder ticks each person on the site after following; that is the only follow-back data available. The Instagram "Followers and following" export, uploaded on the site, does it in one step.

7. What we are deliberately not doing

  • No follow/unfollow cycling and no automation apps (enforcement since Nov 2018; action-block risk).
  • No buying followers. Removal of the Oct 2 block is postponed until real followers pass 1,000 (Trial Reels eligibility).
  • No health-condition ad targeting (removed by Meta Jan 2022); any boost uses broad targeting with self-selecting creative.
  • No HYROX branding, logos or footage, and no implied partnership.
  • No quitters, tech reviewers or privacy audiences.
  • No medical or accuracy claims.

8. Data limits

  • The research agents could not open full pages (proxy and DNS block). Figures come from search summaries of the cited URLs; check the primary page before quoting externally.
  • Third-party hashtag counts conflict by up to 7x and are not used for decisions.
  • Unknown: an Instagram cold follow-back rate, follow-back by size tier, HYROX athlete income, wearable ownership by segment, current USMS membership, and Vybe's water rating.
