Vybe Health: Instagram community targeting, segment evidence pack

Prepared 2026-10-11 for @vybehealthinc (about 45 real followers). Thesis: "people whose progress is limited by recovery, not effort." Excluded: subscription-quitters, tech reviewers, privacy people.

Method and limits (read first)

  • Source access: All evidence came from web search result pages, accessed 2026-10-11. Direct page fetching failed for every domain because of a DNS/egress block in this environment, so I could not open the full PDFs or paywalled reports. Every number below is tied to the URL that the search returned. Where a figure came from a secondary write-up and not the primary study, it is marked (secondary).
  • "Unknown" means unknown. No figure in this report was estimated or filled in.
  • Instagram hashtag counts are not reliable. Instagram no longer shows public post totals, and third-party trackers contradict each other. For example, IQHashtags lists #crossfit at both 565.5M and 76.9M posts. Counts are given only where a tracker published one, and are labelled (3rd-party, unverified). Check them in the Instagram app before relying on them.
  • Vybe's own specs are an open dependency. The water rating, strap placement and price are not stated in the brief. The top-ranked swim segment depends on the band working in the pool.

3. Scoring (0–3)

Pain is how strongly the evidence shows recovery or sleep limiting progress. Fit is how well the segment matches a screenless, no-subscription band that measures against your own baseline, and how likely it is to buy. Moat is how open the segment is to Vybe (3 means no entrenched wearable partner).

Segment Pain Fit Moat Total One-line evidence
Masters swim / OW 2 3 3 8 Average age 46 [10] and 62.4% shoulder pain [11]. No wearable sponsor [13]
Pickleball 40+ 3 2 3 8 24.3M players [14], injuries concentrated in older players [16]. No wearable partner [20]
Shift nurses 3 2 2 7 40–90% poor sleep [34]. Pilot adherence was only 50% [35]
Masters 40+ (cross-sport) 2 3 2 7 Slower recovery after damaging exercise [37]. No single owner
Strongman 3 1 3 7 82% injured [69], but the base is tiny (400 at OSG) [68]
HYROX 3 2 1 6 49.8% injured [4], but Amazfit has exclusivity on straps [8]
Tactical 3 1 1 5 37–40% sleep disorders [25][26], but privacy resistance [28] and Oura DoD [29]
CrossFit 2 2 2 6 3.2 injuries per 1,000 h [51]. Open registrations down 32% [50]
Indoor rowing 2 2 2 6 32.6% masters low back pain [58]. Size unknown [56]
Triathlon 2 2 1 5 Overuse 37–91% [41]. Garmin and Wahoo ecosystem [44]
Hockey (rec) 1 1 3 5 Injuries are mostly acute and contact-related [63]
Climbing 2 1 2 5 47% pulley injuries [61]. Younger, and a wrist device conflicts with grip (assumption)
Trail / ultra 2 1 1 4 GPS-watch culture, with COROS and Suunto entrenched [48][49]
Tennis 2 2 0 4 Oura owns USTA through leagues [67]
Padel 2 1 0 3 WHOOP is Premier Padel's official wearable [55]. US base unknown [53]

Tactical is scored Fit 1 on purpose. The firefighter pushback on wearables is a privacy objection [28], and privacy people are an excluded audience.

4. Top 5 for an Instagram follow/engage strategy (real follow-backs and future buyers)

Rank Segment Why Weakest test (what could kill it)
1 Masters swimmers & open water (40+/USMS) Average age 46 [10], a recovery story (shoulders) [11], no wearable brand owns the community [13]. A small, tight community is likely to follow back Can Vybe be worn in the pool or open water? If it is not swim-rated, Fit drops to 0. A second risk is the small base (about 60k members [9]) and an IG handle and hashtag volume that are not verified
2 Pickleball 40+ Biggest base (24.3M) [14], affluent core players [19], injury burden in older adults [16]. No wearable partner [20] Is pickleball pain recovery-limited or just acute falls and fractures? [17]. Older adults also use wearables less (17% of 50+ in 2019) [38]
3 Shift-work nurses Strongest sleep-pain evidence [34]. Huge base (3.4M) [33]. Visible IG community [79] Adherence. Only 10 of 20 kept wearing a band in the pilot [35]. They are also "recovery-limited" in life rather than in training, which is a looser match to the thesis
4 HYROX, 35+ age groups Explosive growth (1.5M+) [1], 49.8% injury rate [4], very active IG community [71] Amazfit's exclusive covers smart straps [8], so Vybe cannot get event or brand presence. The median athlete is about 30–35 [6] and may be effort-maximisers rather than recovery-limited
5 Masters 40+ strength/endurance (incl. indoor rowing/Concept2) Recovery slows with age after damaging work [37]. Rowing has no wearable owner [56][58] Not one community on IG. Without a single hashtag or hub, follows scatter. The evidence on age and recovery is mixed [37]

Not in the top 5: - Tactical: strong pain evidence, but privacy resistance and Oura/DoD entrenchment. - Strongman: pain is high but the base is too small. - Padel and tennis: WHOOP and Oura own them. - Trail and triathlon: GPS-watch ecosystems are entrenched.
