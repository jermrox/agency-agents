Auto-check from Instagram

Instagram's API doesn't list who follows you, but its data export does. In the Instagram app: Settings → Accounts Center → Your information and permissions → Download your information → Download or transfer information, pick @vybehealthinc, choose Some of your information → Followers and following, format JSON, date range All time. Instagram emails a .zip, usually within minutes. Drop it here and every Followed / Followed back box ticks itself, dated from Instagram's own timestamps.
