Do these first — they gate every federal row and take weeks to clear

None of the federal grants below can be submitted without these, and none of them are same-day. Starting them is free and independent of any deadline, so there is no reason to wait for a specific solicitation.

  1. SAM.gov UEI registration — the federal entity ID. Allow several weeks; entity validation is the usual bottleneck.
  2. SBIR.gov company registration — issues your SBC control number, required on every SBIR/STTR application.
  3. Grants.gov account — the submission portal, and NIH's single official source from FY2026 onward.
  4. eRA Commons — NIH-specific, needed alongside Grants.gov for anything NIH.
  5. eBRAP account — DoD CDMRP only, and its pre-application is a hard gate before the full application.
