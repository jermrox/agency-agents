# LinkedIn company page: Vybe Health

Drafted 3 Oct 2026 under `concept.md`. LinkedIn is a company channel, read by
builders, partners, researchers, team buyers, investors and hires. So the
page leads with the company and uses the band as proof.

## Tagline (120 characters max)

**Recommended:**

> You don't need more data. You need what it tells you, and how sure it is. A wearable others can build on.

Alternatives:

- Not another score. A screenless band that tells you how sure it is, and a platform others can build on.
- We build the wearable. They build what's possible with it. Answers that say how sure they are.

## About (2,000 characters max)

```
Most wearables hand you a score and leave you to work out what it means. Vybe Health is built on a different idea: you don't need more data, you need what the data tells you, and how sure it is.

The Vybe band is screenless. It reads five connected signals, Restore, Move, Nourish, Connect and Vitals, against your own changing baseline rather than a population average. The app is designed to answer in a plain sentence, say how confident it is and why, name what it could not see, and later check whether its own suggestion worked, including the times it got it wrong.

You buy the band once. There is no required subscription to understand your own body, and your health data is never sold.

We build the wearable. They build what's possible with it. Vybe is built open from day one for the teams who want to build on real physiology without becoming a hardware company: performance and tactical programs, researchers, startups and developers. If that is you, talk to us at vybe.health/developers.

Vybe is a general wellness product, not a medical device. We are pre-launch: join the waitlist at vybe.health.
```

## Concept check

1. **Audience and channel:** LinkedIn, so the company half leads. The band comes second, as proof.
2. **Swap test:** "how sure it is" and "a wearable others can build on" fit no competitor. Polar and Amazfit fit "screenless" and "no subscription", so those come later in the About text.
3. **Show it:** the About text names the four parts of an answer: the sentence, its confidence and reason, its blind spot, and the follow-up check. The first post on the page should show one sample answer, labelled as sample data.
4. **Order:** the unclaimed ideas come first. Screenless, own baseline and no subscription follow as proof.
5. **Two products:** both the band and the platform are in the tagline and the About text.
6. **Claims:** the design commitments (Register rows 14 to 16) are written as "designed to". "Never sold" is row 4. "Built open" is row 18, as a direction only, with no availability date. No ECG, heart-rhythm, battery or "validated" wording.
7. **Roadmap:** no dates, ring, retail partners or unreleased features.
8. **Voice:** plain, one ask per audience, and it admits it is pre-launch.
