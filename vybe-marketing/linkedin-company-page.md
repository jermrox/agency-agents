## About (2,000 characters max)

```
Most wearables hand you a score and leave you to work out what it means. Vybe Health is built on a different idea: you don't need more data, you need what the data tells you, and how sure it is.

Our band is screenless. It reads five connected signals, Restore, Move, Nourish, Connect and Vitals, against your own changing baseline rather than a population average. The app is designed to answer in a plain sentence, say how confident it is and why, name what it could not see, and later check whether its own suggestion worked, including the times it got it wrong. An example from our prototype, built on sample data:

"You are less recovered than usual. The 1am bedtime after Saturday fits best; Friday's hard session and two short nights fit too. Moderate confidence: three nights of data since the change. What Vybe could not see: how you actually feel today."

You buy the band once. There is no required subscription to understand your own body, and your health data is never sold.

We build the wearable. They build what's possible with it. Vybe is built open from day one for the teams who want to build on real physiology without becoming a hardware company: performance and tactical programs, researchers, startups and developers. Teams see readiness in aggregate, never individuals without their consent, and every person can revoke it. If that is you, talk to us at vybe.health/developers.

Vybe is a general wellness product, not a medical device. We are pre-launch: join the waitlist at vybe.health.
```

## Concept check

1. **Audience and channel:** LinkedIn, so the company half leads. The band comes second, as proof.
2. **Swap test:** "how sure it is" and "a wearable others can build on" fit no competitor. Polar and Amazfit fit "screenless" and "no subscription", so those come later in the About text.
3. **Show it:** the About text shows one sample answer from the prototype, labelled as sample data: the sentence, its confidence with the reason, and one blind spot. It also names the follow-up check.
4. **Order:** the unclaimed ideas come first. Screenless, own baseline and no subscription follow as proof.
5. **Two products:** both the band and the platform are in the tagline and the About text.
6. **Claims:** the design commitments (Register rows 14 to 16) are written as "designed to", and the example is labelled as prototype sample data. "Never sold" is row 4. The team and research invitation carries "aggregate only, with consent", per the tensions table. "Built open" is row 18, as a direction only, with no availability date. No ECG, heart-rhythm, battery or "validated" wording.
7. **Roadmap:** no dates, ring, retail partners or unreleased features.
8. **Voice:** plain, one ask per audience, and it admits it is pre-launch.
