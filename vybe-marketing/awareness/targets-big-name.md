## 1. The segment

**Who.** Organised adult communities under a big name whose leaders can approve an opt-in pilot and a member offer without a procurement cycle: the sport-club offices of flagship universities, the national member bodies of veterans and firefighters, and national governing bodies with no wearable partner on their public pages.

**The shared problem.** They train and compete hard with no sport-science staff and no budget for one: university club sports are "student-led, student-run" ([Michigan Recreation, read 13 Oct 2026](https://recreation.umich.edu/clubs/)) and "administered by UCLA Recreation but run by student officers" ([UCLA Alumni, read 13 Oct 2026](https://alumni.ucla.edu/parents/student-life/)); the CU Boulder Triathlon Team, 18-time national champions, raised 81% of a $25,000 goal to get to the 2026 nationals ([CU Boulder giving page, read 13 Oct 2026](https://giveto.colorado.edu/cu-boulder-triathlon-nationals-2026)). Veteran and fire-service bodies run fitness as a wellbeing programme, not a performance programme, and buy nothing per member: Team RWB chapters "host weekly fitness activities" and its members hosted "nearly 17,000 events" in 2024 ([Team RWB media center, read 13 Oct 2026](https://teamrwb.org/media-center)); the NVFC's Heart-Healthy Firefighter Program released a sleep video and functional-fitness videos in February 2026 ([NVFC, read 13 Oct 2026](https://www.nvfc.org/?p=23031)).

**The trigger to switch now.** None of them has a wearable partner on its public pages (checked organisation by organisation in §4, 13 Oct 2026), while the names one tier above them have just been taken: Oura is "the exclusive provider for Team USA and LA28 in the category of continuous health and fitness monitoring devices" ([LA28, 6 Feb 2026, read 13 Oct 2026](https://la28.org/es/sala-de-prensa/oura-es-nombrado-el-dispositivo-wearable-oficial-de-team-usa-y-la28.html)), WHOOP has Notre Dame's "select programs" (Growsom `sponsor_competitors` lane, 10 Oct 2026) and Amazfit sits in Planet Fitness's PF Perks ([Zepp, 4 Dec 2025, read 13 Oct 2026](https://huami.com/blog/amazfit-expands-access-to-smart-wellness-through-new-partnerships-with-planet-fitness-and-rei)). The tier below those deals is open and knows it is being passed over.

**Size (estimate, with method).** Flagship-university sport-club programmes: the four programmes opened on 13 Oct 2026 list 30 (Michigan), "40+" (Texas), 38 (Stanford) and 59 (UCLA) clubs, so about 30 to 60 clubs each; Ohio State's 2022–23 handbook said "over 60" ([recsports.osu.edu, read 13 Oct 2026](https://recsports.osu.edu/programs/sport-clubs)). Taking one flagship per state gives 50 programmes × 30 to 60 clubs = **1,500 to 3,000 clubs (estimate)**; the count of programmes that would run a pilot is unknown, and the way to find out is the §8 test. National bodies are counted one at a time in §4: Team RWB "nearly 320,000" members and supporters, SVA "over 1,600 chapters", IAFF "more than 344,000" members, NVFC about 27,500, USA Cycling 60,000, USRowing 78,500 individual members, USA Weightlifting "over 21,000", USA Ultimate 39,433. None of these totals is the segment: the segment is the programme office or chapter that says yes, and a yes is counted one organisation at a time (rule 12).

## 2. Wedge tests (0 to 3, with evidence; hypotheses until the §8 test runs)

- **Pain 2 (weakest).** They feel the gap, but as a wish, not a budget line. Evidence that they look for data: ACSM's 2026 survey of about 2,000 professionals ranks wearable technology the #1 worldwide fitness trend ([ACSM, read 13 Oct 2026](https://acsm.org/top-fitness-trends-2026)); campus-rec trade press carries that result to rec professionals ([Campus Rec Magazine, read 13 Oct 2026](https://campusrecmag.com/top-fitness-trends-of-2026-and-what-campus-rec-pros-need-to-know/)). Evidence against: no club or veteran body in §4 publishes a complaint about not having a wearable, and the one university body that replied so far (Ohio State Triathlon Club) "wants to collaborate", which is interest, not pain (briefing JSON, 13 Oct 2026).
- **Fit 2.** A band with no required subscription (row 2) suits a group that buys nothing per member, and a group view "designed to show readiness in aggregate, with consent" (row 22, founder to confirm, hold) suits a club with no staff to read individual data. Against: the band is pre-launch and no bands exist to give (Growsom charter), so every pilot is a promise dated to the founding batch; and HRV and heart-rate sensing specifications "remain unconfirmed" on /faq (Firecrawl, 5 Oct 2026, `concept.md` learning log), so no club copy can describe what the band measures.
- **Moat 2.** WHOOP, Oura and Garmin would not have to break their model to follow; they would have to care. Their public deals go to NCAA departments, NGB national teams, pro leagues and gym chains (§4c). A club sports office with 40 student-run teams, or a volunteer fire council, is below the cheque size those teams work at; nothing stops them, so this is 2, not 3.

**Weakest test: Pain.** The cheapest way to find out whether it is 2 or 1 is the §8 test: whether big-name programme offices reply to a no-cost pilot ask at the rate Vybe's own rows show (5 of 9 so far).
