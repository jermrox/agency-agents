### Saturday: Facebook post (Connect: morning daylight). Scored 18/18.

> Vybe is designed to name what it couldn't see, even on a question about
> morning daylight.
>
> What the research does and doesn't say: in UK Biobank studies of about
> 86,000 adults, brighter days and darker nights went with better mental
> health and lower mortality. That is an association, and those studies
> compared day and night light, not mornings. A morning outside is one simple
> way to get more daylight into your day.
>
> Prototype, sample data:
> "On the 6 mornings you logged time outside before 9:00, your estimated sleep
> start that night was about 25 minutes earlier than your own usual. The
> outdoor mornings fit; so do the lighter workdays on four of those six days.
> Low confidence: six logged mornings in three weeks. What Vybe could not see:
> the mornings you didn't log. This is a pattern, not a cause. Next: log 10
> minutes outside before 9:00 on two weekdays."
>
> The band is designed to produce answers like that one from your nights and
> what you log. There's no required subscription for the band. We do not use
> your data for advertising targeting.
>
> The band isn't out yet, and nobody knows your pattern until you've logged a
> few weeks. Join the waitlist at vybe.health.
