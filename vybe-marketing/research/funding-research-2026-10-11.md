# Money in, at no cost: funding research (11 Oct 2026)

Research only. Nothing has been applied for, drafted or sent. Every opportunity costs $0 to apply. Facts come from each funder's own page. "Unverified" means the fact came from a search summary or a third party.

## Two facts decide most of this list

1. **Is the founder a veteran?** Documentation such as a DD-214 counts. A yes opens Veteran Shark Tank, Clemson, VETCON, Patriot Boot Camp, Military Founders Lab and Hivers & Strivers.
2. **Is Vybe based in Raleigh (NC) or Akron (OH)?** NC opens NC IDEA ($10K or $50K grants), One NC and RIoT. Ohio opens JumpStart.

## Honest readiness check

Vybe is not ready to pitch investors yet. Every early-stage wearable deal found from the last three years was built on new science: Clair Health ($11.6M seed, Khosla), Somnee (Khosla), Elemind (Village Global) and Level Zero Health (HAX). No investor was found with a record of backing a basic HRV and sleep band before it had a prototype or traction.

Garmin CIRQA ($199.99, screen-free, no subscription, launched 21 Jul 2026) and Polar Loop now hold "screenless, no subscription". Investors will ask why not a Garmin.

**What investors want before a meeting:**
- a working prototype that someone outside the team has worn
- about 30 well-chosen users and their reactions (Bolt's "30 perfect customers": https://blog.bolt.io/show-traction/)
- refundable deposits or pre-orders
- a draft parts list and unit cost
- a manufacturer shortlist
- an FCC and battery certification plan

**The realistic order:** prototype and 30 users, then deposits, then free accelerator applications, then grants, then investors.

## Free growth that comparable companies used

- **Pre-orders with a working prototype:**
  - Oura's Kickstarter raised $651,803 from 2,383 backers, and 77% had backed projects before.
  - Bellabeat sold $1.2M of pre-orders on its own site.
  - Lief, an HRV patch, raised $405,922 on Kickstarter.
- **Kickstarter pre-launch pages:** 32% of followers back the campaign, against 17% of people who arrive after launch (Kickstarter).
- **WHOOP:** started by putting prototypes on athletes through their trainers.
- **Reels:** Vybe's own Reels reach 7–20 times more people than its image posts. This is the only channel already proven for Vybe.
- **Reddit:** most wearable subreddits ban promotion (r/SmartRings, r/Garmin, r/Biohacking). Answer questions openly as the founder.
- **Not yet:** a crowdfunding campaign. At about 4 signups a week, a public failure next to Garmin and Polar is the main risk.

## Talking about a raise (SEC)

- **Rule 506(b):** no public promotion. https://www.sec.gov/resources-small-businesses/exempt-offerings/private-placements-rule-506b
- **Rule 506(c):** public promotion is allowed, but every investor must be accredited and verified. https://www.sec.gov/resources-small-businesses/exempt-offerings/general-solicitation-rule-506c
- **Regulation Crowdfunding:** up to $5M in 12 months, through a registered portal only.
- **Before posting:** choose the route with a securities lawyer before saying "raising" anywhere public.
