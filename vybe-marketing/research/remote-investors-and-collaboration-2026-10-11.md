**Before any pitch:**
- Confirm whether the founder is a veteran.
- Confirm whether the base is Raleigh or Akron.
- Have a working prototype, worn by real users.

Investors will ask "why not a Garmin CIRQA or Polar Loop?" There needs to be an answer first.

### Once the band works

- **Elite HRV and HRV4Training:** both work with any band that broadcasts standard Bluetooth heart-rate R-R intervals. No partnership needed.
- **Reviewers:** YouTube channels that reviewed Garmin CIRQA or Polar Loop in 2026 include DC Rainmaker, The Quantified Scientist, DesFit and Shervin Shares. Each needs a unit shipped to them, which costs hardware and postage.
- **Communities:** Show HN (needs a working product) and Open Humans (free research data projects).

### Costs money, so the founder decides

- Apple Health (HealthKit) needs the Apple Developer Program: **$99 a year**.
- Google Health Connect needs a Play Console account: **$25 once**.

Both are probably needed for the phone app anyway. Apple Health is also the route into Bevel, Athlytic, Rise, SleepSpace and Welltory.

**Excluded because they cost money:**
- Strava ("A Strava subscription is a prerequisite for creating an app")
- BetaList ("All submissions are paid")
- Terra's paid plans
- Junction beyond its free sandbox ($300/month)
