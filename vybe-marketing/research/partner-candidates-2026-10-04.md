# Vybe partner candidates: indoor rowing, tested powerlifting, hybrid racing (not HYROX)

All sources read 4 Oct 2026 (via Exa; usapowerlifting.com blocked direct fetch). Read-only: nothing contacted. "Planned" = needs the Vybe SDK, which is not shipped. No candidate has agreed to anything. Any Vybe-to-app heart-rate link assumes the band broadcasts standard Bluetooth HR, which is **unverified**.

**Off limits (confirmed):** Amazfit's HYROX deal covers "smart straps" and extends to "the HYROX 365 training network of gyms" ([Zepp, 15 Apr 2026](https://www.zepp.com/press-release/hyrox-and-amazfit-strengthen-alliance-with-global-three-year-partnership)). That rules out HYROX Training Clubs (about 960 US, [Hyrox Vault](https://www.hyroxvault.com/gyms/country/united-states/), secondary), EōS ([26 Aug 2026](https://www.eosfitness.com/press-releases/eos-fitness-and-hyrox-join-forces)), F45 ([Nasdaq, 11 Mar 2026](https://www.nasdaq.com/press-release/f45-training-extends-global-gym-partner-hyrox-anchored-title-partnership-hyrox)), VASA ([PRN, 10 Sep 2026](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/vasa-fitness-announces-hyrox-enterprise-partnership-launching-at-10-clubs-across-north-america-302874475.html)), Anytime Fitness and Les Mills. **TrainHeroic is Garmin-owned since 22 Jul 2026** ([Garmin](https://www.garmin.com/en-US/newsroom/press-release/corporate/garmin-acquires-trainingpeaks-and-trainheroic-leading-endurance-and-strength-training-platforms-for-athletes-and-coaches/)): CONFLICT, dropped.
