# Concept-learning check: Mon 5 Oct 2026

The monthly check for the Vybe Health Marketing Director agent. It is read-only: nothing was contacted, changed or edited.

**Inputs.** `vybe-marketing/concept.md` (built 3 Oct 2026) and `vybe-marketing/brand-brief.md` (Claim Register v1).

**Method.** Composio Firecrawl, session `sail`. FIRECRAWL_MAP ran on https://vybe.health and returned 28 URLs (24 on 3 Oct). 25 FIRECRAWL_SCRAPE calls went to vybe.health (markdown, `onlyMainContent: false`, `waitFor: 3000`). They covered 22 unique pages: /faq was read twice, once as rawHtml to get the FAQPage JSON-LD because its answers sit in collapsed accordions, and two calls hit the rate limit and were retried. 19 calls went to category players (FIRECRAWL_SEARCH with `site:` filters, or FIRECRAWL_SCRAPE of the homepage). **Total: 45 of 45 Firecrawl calls.** I used only markdown and raw text; JSON extraction was not used. Every quote below was read on **5 Oct 2026**.

  - the "30 days" battery claim (Claim Register row 7);
  - the bio line "Own your personalized SDK and Application" (row 12).

## 3. Proposed learning-log entries

All entries are dated **2026-10-05** and are proposals only. concept.md and brand-brief.md were not edited.

1. **The miss: Register row 22 and the tactical channel row quote /enterprise wording that the site no longer carries.**
   - The concept requires that wording to go in exactly ("with per-person detail where the individual has consented to share it").
   - Two days later, /enterprise reads "individual detail only by consent" and "only when consented".
   - **Rule change:** every quoted site wording in the Register carries its scrape date. Before any piece that uses it is scheduled, the live page is re-read. If the wording has changed, the row goes back to "founder to confirm" and copy uses neither version. Add a Register note that the new /enterprise wording is itself an "only" sharing promise, which needs counsel's view before reuse.

2. **The miss: the concept's Vitals job and example answers assume the band senses HRV.** The site now says "HRV and heart-rate sensing specifications remain unconfirmed" (/faq) and "vital-sign sensing unconfirmed" (/hardware).
   - **Rule change:** treat HRV and heart rate like daylight, battery and GPS, as unconfirmed specs.
   - Vitals keeps its search job, but Vitals content teaches HRV itself and never states that the band measures it until the founder confirms the sensor.
   - Example answers that read HRV from a Vybe band must carry "prototype, sample data" and must not appear in search pages as product behaviour.
   - Add "HRV and heart-rate sensing" to Q6's blind-spot rule and Q7's unconfirmed-spec list.
   - Flag the /learn/hrv-tracker card "capture HRV every night" to the founder.

3. **The miss: unclaimed idea #4 was declared on 3 Oct without a targeted search.** The Ultrahuman check read PowerPlugs and Jade, but not the "For Developers · Ultrasignal" page ("World's first wearable-based developer platform").
   - **Rule change:** for each unclaimed idea, the monthly refresh runs one targeted search per category player in that idea's own words (developer, SDK, raw data, build on; confidence, uncertainty; recommendation worked). Any "No one…" sentence in concept.md cites that search with URL and date.
   - Reword #4 to the narrower ground: a band, consent-first owner access, start at the interpretation. Ban "first", "only" and "no one else" in builder copy.

4. **The miss: the hygiene table under-counted the subscription and screenless claims.**
   - Amazfit now uses Vybe's exact Register phrase ("No required subscription.").
   - Garmin CIRQA uses Vybe's footer phrase ("no subscription required") and "screen-free".
   - Polar says "No locked insights", which contradicts concept.md's line that Polar charges for its AI coach.
