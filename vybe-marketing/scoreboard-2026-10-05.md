## The change

**Pause all paid spend now, and keep it at $0** until three things hold:
1. the consent gate on /waitlist is live (plan week 1, action 7);
2. the GA4 key events are cleaned up;
3. Ads clicks and GA4 paid sessions reconcile within 30%.

This replaces the first draft of this file, which kept Search-1 running with
new settings. That draft broke two written rules: plan action 3 says to import
no conversions until counsel rules on /privacy §3, and the plan's budget rule
sets no cost ceiling until the founder sets price and margin. Pausing is also
a test, with one caution: GA4 can credit a returning visitor's later
session to an earlier ad click, so paid-labelled sessions at $0 spend are not
proof of someone else's traffic on their own.

Pausing spend does not stop the tags. The Google Ads tag and GA4 still load
on /waitlist with no Global Privacy Control check and no health privacy link,
so the consent gate is urgent in its own right, not only as a condition for
restarting paid. Counsel should read it against Washington's My Health My
Data Act and the other state consumer-health-data laws.

- **Metric:** paid-labelled GA4 sessions at $0 spend, plus non-paid US
  sessions and US signups per week, from GA4 and Google Ads.
- **Decision rules, checked on 12 and 19 Oct:**
  - More than 10 paid-labelled sessions in a week at $0 spend: check whether
    they are new users and whether their landings carry a `gclid`. If they
    are new users with no `gclid`, the traffic is not Vybe's ads; exclude it
    in reports, but never delete GA4 data on one week alone.
  - Signups: two or three a fortnight cannot show whether paid helped. Track
    US signups by source, and draw no conclusion about paid until at least 5
    signups have arrived with paid off.
  - Fewer than 15 non-paid US sessions a week: the problem is not paid, so
    look at Instagram and the site.
- **Restart only when:** the consent gate is live, counsel has ruled on
  /privacy §3, and the founder has set a maximum cost per reservation and a
  test budget. A test needs about 150 clicks to read.
- **For when paid restarts:** set the location to US only, "presence: people
  in or regularly in". Use phrase match with these negatives: blood pressure,
  bmi, tabata, workout, routine, gym, music, hypnosis, white noise, podcast,
  huberman, weight watchers, timer, videos, free, app, jobs, login, charger,
  strap. Write ad text from the Claim Register only.

Credit: the pause and the decision rules came from this week's eval run of
targeting case 7, which caught that the first draft broke the plan's rules.
