## Answer first (40 to 60 words)

WHOOP is a screen-free strap built around training load and recovery, with
Strain, Recovery and Sleep scores. Oura is a titanium ring built around sleep
and readiness, with Sleep, Readiness and Activity scores. Both read HRV and
heart rate against your own baseline. Choose by the question you ask most,
and by strap or ring.

## Outline

1. **What is the difference between WHOOP and Oura?** Form and focus, in
   each brand's words. WHOOP: "A screen-free design means no pings,
   distractions" [W1]; worn on the wrist, or off-wrist with WHOOP Body
   apparel [W1]. Oura: a titanium ring worn on the index, middle or ring
   finger [O2, O3]. Then the comparison table below.
2. **What is better, Oura Ring or WHOOP?** Neither, in general. Say who each
   one suits, granting each its strength:
   - *WHOOP suits people who train and want a daily target.* Strain is a
     0 to 21 score built from cardiovascular and muscular load, and WHOOP
     suggests a Strain target from that morning's Recovery [W5].
   - *Oura suits people who want sleep and readiness on a ring.* Its Sleep
     Score draws on total sleep, HRV, nighttime movement and sleep
     regularity, and it recognises over 40 activities automatically [O2].
   - Close with the honest line: the choice is mostly form factor and
     which question you ask every morning ("how hard can I train?" or "how
     did I sleep and how ready am I?").
3. **How do the scores work?** One H3 each, built from each brand's own
   explanation:
   - *WHOOP Recovery* uses HRV, resting heart rate, Sleep Performance and
     respiratory rate [W3].
   - *WHOOP Sleep* combines hours against need, consistency, efficiency
     and sleep stress [W7].
   - *Oura Readiness* uses nine contributors, including resting heart rate,
     HRV balance and body temperature, scored against your personal
     averages [O4].
   - The shared point: both compare you with yourself, not with a
     population average (WHOOP: "follow your own long-term trends" [W9];
     Oura: "your daily readings are evaluated against them" [O4]). That is
     why one person's "good" number tells another person nothing.
   - No learning-period figure for either device: no current WHOOP 5.0 or
     MG page gives one, so neither product gets one.
4. **Which is more accurate?** (answers "whoop vs oura accuracy" and "is
   whoop or oura more accurate"). Both publish their own validation and
   both cite comparisons with lab sleep studies [O2, W7]. WHOOP's own
   article says its sleep-lab findings "should be interpreted as an

   declares no accuracy winner and quotes no accuracy percentage.
5. **Can WHOOP tell if you're sick?** Short answer, no number. WHOOP's own
   page says a low Recovery score "does not indicate that you have COVID-19
   or any other illness" [W8]. HRV and resting heart rate move with
   training, alcohol, the menstrual cycle and illness, so no single
   reading says which one it was (Altini & Plews 2021). Symptoms or worry
   are for a clinician. Apply the same answer to Oura (its Health Radar
   features are "intended for general wellness purposes only" [O1]).
