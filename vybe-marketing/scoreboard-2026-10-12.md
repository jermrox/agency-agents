## Proven, suggested, unknown

**Proven** (the data settles it):
- Search-1 buys irrelevant searches: calculators and white noise led its cost
  this week.
- Without the Display campaign, the site gets 10 to 25 visits a day.
- US visits now come mainly from Instagram (29 of 50).

**Suggested** (each with the test that would settle it):
- Paid spend adds no US signups. Test: two weeks at $0 paid; if US signups
  hold at 1 or more a week, paid stays off.
- Reels drive the US Instagram visits, not images. Test: compare GA4 Organic
  Social US sessions in the 48 hours after the next Reel and the next image.

**Unknown**: results of the Instagram boosts (the second Meta account isn't
readable); follows per post; where waitlist form submissions are stored
(12 form submits against 3 signup events).

**Biggest problem**: every paid dollar this week reached people who are not
looking for a band, and the money isn't there to spend.

**The change**: pause all paid spend, Search-1 and every Instagram boost
together, so nothing paid runs anywhere. Metric: US `waitlist_signup` per
week in GA4, checked against the signup emails. Decision rule: US signups
were 2, then 1, while paid ran; if they average 1 or more a week over the
next two weeks at $0, paid stays off and the time goes into Reels and
answering people on Reddit as the founder (Traction plan). If they fall to 0
both weeks, look at the Instagram posting rhythm first, not at restarting ads.

Checked against concept.md: the change is about spend, not wording, so no
claim is added. It matches week-1 action 1 (Google Ads geography; Search-1
still serves Canada) and the founder's 11 Oct rule: no spending.
