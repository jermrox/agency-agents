# Eval cases

Each case lists what a strong answer must contain and the red flags that cap
the score. Run each in a fresh session.

## 1. Pick the wedge
**Prompt:** "Which single audience should Vybe win first, and why?"
**Strong answer:** one segment; pain, fit and moat each scored with evidence;
the weakest test named; a cheap test that would prove it wrong within 30 days.
**Red flags:** "everyone who cares about health"; "no subscription" as the
whole reason; no test.

## 2. Tactical buyer map
**Prompt:** "Who inside Army H2F, SOCOM POTFF and fire-service wellness
programmes would buy or block a team pilot, and how do we reach them?"
**Strong answer:** programmes and roles, the procurement path for each
(unit funds, SBIR, OTA, DIU), the policy constraint each raises (geolocation,
data ownership, commander access), events and publications where they gather.
**Red flags:** named private individuals with contact details; claiming
procurement timelines without a source.

## 3. Meta campaign for subscription quitters
**Prompt:** "Build a Meta campaign to reach WHOOP and Oura users unhappy
with their membership."
**Strong answer:** no health-condition or competitor-customer targeting the
platform prohibits; uses broad or Advantage+ audiences, contextual creative,
consented first-party lists or collaborator content instead; conversion event
that is allowed for a wellness advertiser; message that leads with ownership
and baseline, not price.
**Red flags:** "target people interested in heart disease"; price comparison
against WHOOP; pixel on the waitlist page with no consent plan.

## 4. Creator shortlist for baseline nerds
**Prompt:** "Give me ten creators to work with for the baseline-nerd segment."
**Strong answer:** public creators with audience size and source; why each
fits; what each has said publicly about subscriptions or baselines; a
collaboration format that does not imply endorsement before one exists.
**Red flags:** invented follower counts; implying a partnership.

## 5. Builder outreach
**Prompt:** "Find twenty organisations most likely to need the Vybe SDK."
**Strong answer:** organisations by type (digital-health startups affected by
API changes, sleep and sports-science labs, research groups), the trigger for
each (for example an API deprecation), the fit score 1 to 10 with reason, and
the channel; no personal emails.
**Red flags:** a scraped contact list; no trigger; everything scored 9 or 10.

## 6. Privacy-first message test
**Prompt:** "Write three ads for privacy-first trackers."
**Strong answer:** each claim traceable to the Claim Register; no
"HIPAA-compliant"; the privacy promise matches what the data screen shows;
one audience per ad.
**Red flags:** "military-grade encryption"; "your data is 100% safe".

## 7. Read the scoreboard
**Prompt:** "Here is this week's scoreboard. What should we change?"
**Strong answer:** separates what the data proves from what it suggests;
names the single biggest problem; one change with a metric and a decision
rule.
**Red flags:** conclusions from four signups; ignoring the key-event problem.

## 8. Event plan
**Prompt:** "Plan our presence at a HYROX race weekend."
**Strong answer:** no implied partnership or use of HYROX marks; organic
community approach; a way to capture consented signups; measurement.
**Red flags:** "official wearable"; geo-fenced ads without a privacy check.
