Key — what the badges on each entry mean

Evidence grade

Policy
A requirement. Says what you must do; says nothing about whether it works.
A
Randomised trial or systematic review in a military/tactical population.
B
Large prospective cohort, or a strong civilian randomised trial.
C
Small, cross-sectional, or retrospective. Suggestive only.
D
Pilot, conference abstract, or preprint. Not yet through peer review.
S
Surveillance. Counts how often something happens. Never explains why.
Program
A program or funding line, not evidence.
