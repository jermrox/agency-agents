Army directive (PDF) · Army Publishing Directorate · reported effective 07 JUL 2026

Army Fitness Test (AFT) — program page and scoring

Program page · army.mil · continuously updated

The AFT replaced the ACFT on 1 June 2025. Reported scoring: combat MOSs move to one sex-neutral, age-normed standard requiring 350 total with a 60-point event minimum, effective 1 JAN 2026 (active) and 1 JUN 2026 (RC); combat-enabling specialties keep sex- and age-normed scoring at 300. Waist-to-height ratio is replacing height/weight across the Joint Force

P&R guidance in December 2025 and January 2026, then Army Directive 2026-13 (WHtR 0.55, effective July 2026), Marine Corps MARADMIN changes, Air Force reinstating scored body composition on WHtR, and Navy revising BCA procedures. One measurement change, four services, twelve months — and it changes who enters remedial programs.

Sleep is becoming a measured readiness metric, not a wellness topic

A DoW-level summit, a practitioner convening, Total Force Fitness policy language on cognitive performance, and an RCT protocol treating insomnia in an active-duty TBI population. The trajectory runs from encouragement toward measurement — and measurement eventually means standards.

04

Women's health is widening past load carriage

Pelvic floor prevalence near 10% of active-duty service women, sex-specific patellofemoral loading, hip fracture surveillance by sex, a perimenopause scoping review, Millennium Cohort HRQOL work, and sex-specific environmental extremes research. The research base is moving toward retention-relevant midlife health while the standards conversation moves toward sex-neutral.

Energy availability is the mechanism under several separate problems

USARIEM work on performance in caloric deficit, RED-S reviews in military and sport populations, bone stress and hip fracture surveillance, and a half-billion-dollar food program overhaul. Fueling deficit connects the injury, women's health, and nutrition threads — and is rarely programmed as one problem.

Brain health is separating out as its own readiness domain

The Warfighter Brain Health Initiative, a dedicated CDMRP TBI/psychological health program in the FY26 line, TBICoE worldwide surveillance numbers, and 2026 RCTs on exercise therapy and insomnia in TBI populations. Repetitive blast overpressure is the piece that reaches routine training, not just combat.

07

Wearables arrived with their governance layer, and with a contradiction

Total Force Fitness memo language on fielding wearables and analytics, WARP/LifeLens fielding from FY26, AR 25-99 on biometric data management effective July 2026 — against standing prohibitions on wireless devices in certain areas and operations. Expect procurement and data-governance questions to land before validation evidence does.

08

Musculoskeletal injury remains the readiness problem nobody has solved

MSK drove the largest absolute rise in ambulatory visits in 2024 (a reported 1.68 million more than 2020) and the largest share of deployed encounters; there is a 2026 call for cross-institution collaboration and a dedicated CDMRP orthopaedic line — and no 2026 GAO or DoD IG assessment of whether the prevention programs work. The gap between spend and independent evaluation is the story.
