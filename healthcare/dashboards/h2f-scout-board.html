Effects of Task-Specific and Strength Training on Simulated Military Task Performance in Soldiers

Controlled training study · PMC7662950 · older, still load-bearing

42 soldiers, three groups (task-specific, strength, control), 12-week intervention with pre/mid/post measurement on a repeated simulated military task course. Both training arms beat control; task-specific was as effective as strength training, supporting a combined approach. Not a 2026 item — included because it is the cleanest citable evidence for the combined-programming argument H2F teams make constantly.
